# Traffic fines, fixed units (UF), hearings, camera tickets and the licence points system

For drivers and owners in Argentina, many with older cars. A fine (*multa*) is an administrative sanction decided by a traffic court (*juzgado de faltas*) or a controller; it is not a criminal case, but ignoring it can block a licence renewal, end in a property seizure and, in some places, cost points on the licence. This is information, not legal advice: for a serious case (alcohol, an accident, a suspended licence, a large amount) talk to a lawyer or a public defender (*defensoría*). Every amount in pesos in this file is an example with a date: the unit value changes every two months (PBA) or every six months (CABA). Never pay through a link in a message: see VEH-MUL-41.

Rules and amounts differ by jurisdiction (the place where the fine was issued, not where you live). PBA and CABA are the worked cases; for any other province or municipality read its own site and coupon.

## Pricing: the fixed unit (UF)

### VEH-MUL-1: Read every fine as a number of UF times the UF value of the day
- **Rule:** a fine (*multa*) is set in fixed units (*unidades fijas*, UF), and the pesos you pay are the number of UF multiplied by the value of one UF on the day you pay. By national law one UF is tied to the price of a litre of petrol (Ley 24.449 art. 84); each jurisdiction publishes its own value.
- **Why:** the number of UF stays the same while the UF value follows fuel prices, so the same fine costs more pesos every update. In PBA one UF was ARS 79.90 on 2021-01-01 and ARS 2,340 for Nov-Dec 2026.
- **How:** amount = UF count of the infraction x UF value of the day. PBA: the value is published every two months and follows the highest-octane petrol price quoted by the Automovil Club Argentino (La Plata) at the start of each two-month period (Ley 13.927, Decreto 532/09). Example as of 2026-11-15: PBA UF = ARS 2,340 for 1 Nov - 31 Dec 2026 (Resolution RS-2026-39113196-GDEBA-SSPYSVMTRAGP). Earlier PBA values reported by news (secondary): 1,336 (Nov-Dec 2024), 1,398 (Jan-Feb 2025), 1,711 to 1,896 (Mar-Apr 2026), 2,271 (Jul-Aug 2026), 2,281 (Sep-Oct 2026). Each +100 pesos in the UF adds 100 pesos per UF: on a 300 UF fine that is 30,000 pesos. The UF page shows the current value: `infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar`, menu "uf $".
- **Verify:** open the UF page of the jurisdiction that issued the fine and compare with the pesos printed on the coupon; the printed coupon may use an older UF.
- **Don't:** budget with the peso amount printed on an old notice, or assume the national petrol-price rule gives the same UF in every province.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 84 (text read on leyes-ar.com, a secondary transcription of the consolidated law, and on argentina.gob.ar, original text, 2026-11-15); infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar/unidad-fija-detalle (read 2026-11-15); gba.gob.ar news 2021-01-15 (UF 79.90 and the rule of the highest-octane petrol at ACA La Plata); earlier PBA values: Not verified in a primary source: news articles only (Canal 26, iProfesional, Ambito, 2025-2026).

### VEH-MUL-2: Know that CABA uses its own, lower UF value
- **Rule:** the City of Buenos Aires prices its fines (*faltas*) in UF too, with its own value, fixed by the City and updated about every six months. Do not use the PBA value for a fine issued in CABA.
- **Why:** the same number of UF costs far fewer pesos in CABA than in PBA, and mixing the two gives wrong estimates.
- **How:** example as of 2026-11-15 (one secondary source, not the official table): CABA UF ARS 949.99 from 2026-03-03 and ARS 1,173.08 from 2026-09-02. The pesos of any CABA fine are in the City consultation page and on the payment slip; the cost of procedures follows the "Ley Tarifaria" in force (said on the City's page).
- **Verify:** check the amount on the City infraction lookup (VEH-MUL-5) and on the slip; ask the City's line 147.
- **Don't:** copy an amount from a news article without its date and jurisdiction.
- **Source:** buenosaires.gob.ar/tramites/turnos-para-resolver-infracciones (read 2026-11-15; says costs follow the Ley Tarifaria); Not verified in a primary source: the CABA UF values come from one news site (comparaencasa.com, updated 2026-10-21) and iProfesional 2026; the City resolution that sets the UF was not read.

### VEH-MUL-3: Expect an unpaid fine to be repriced at each UF update
- **Rule:** while a fine stays unpaid its peso amount rises with every UF update, so waiting has a cost even when nothing else happens.
- **Why:** the amount is UF x value of the day you pay (VEH-MUL-1), and the discount for voluntary payment exists only at the start (VEH-MUL-10).
- **How:** worked example with an invented 100 UF fine in PBA: at UF 1,896 (reported for Mar-Apr 2026) it is 189,600 pesos; at 2,281 (reported for Sep-Oct 2026) it is 228,100, a rise of 20.3% in six months; at 2,340 (Nov-Dec 2026) it is 234,000, 23.4% above the first figure. A 50% voluntary payment of the same fine at 2,340 would be 117,000 (see VEH-MUL-10 for the exact base of the discount).
- **Verify:** reprint the coupon: the amount shown is calculated with the UF of the day it is displayed.
- **Don't:** leave a notice in a drawer "until it arrives at court".
- **Source:** calculation on the figures of VEH-MUL-1; the 1,896 and 2,281 figures are Not verified in a primary source (news 2026); 2,340 is from infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-MUL-4: Treat the printed UF range of an infraction as a guide and read the actual record
- **Rule:** the fine for an infraction is a number of UF within a range set by the regulation; national law caps the range (50 UF minimum to 5,000 UF; up to 20,000 UF for overweight vehicles) and treats it as an aggravating factor when the liable person is the owner. What counts is the number written on your record (*acta*).
- **Why:** press tables disagree with each other and with older notes, and a wrong table leads to a wrong pay-or-dispute decision.
- **How:** typical reported ranges (June 2026, orientative, secondary): seat belt 100-300 UF; no valid technical inspection (*VTV*) 100-300 UF; red light 100-300 UF; phone or earphones while driving 150-500 UF; speeding 150-1,000 UF; positive alcohol test 200-1,000 UF; driving against traffic 200-1,000 UF; refusing the alcohol test 300-1,000 UF. An older note carried over from an earlier version of this guide gave red light as 300 UF at the start to 1,000 UF: sources disagree, so read the number on the record. Example with the PBA UF of 2,340: 300 UF = 702,000 pesos; 1,000 UF = 2,340,000 pesos (calc).
- **Verify:** the code and the UF count appear on the coupon and in the lookup result (VEH-MUL-7).
- **Don't:** compare your fine with a headline figure for "the most expensive fine".
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 84 (read 2026-11-15, argentina.gob.ar and leyes-ar.com); Disposicion ANSV 167/2026 (BO 2026-06-18; read 2026-11-15) creates unique codes per infraction and does not change the amounts; ranges: Not verified in a primary source (c5n.com and lmneuquen.com, 2026-06-18, which use a UF of 2,249 as example; the national regulation annex was not read).

## Checking whether there are fines

### VEH-MUL-5: Check every jurisdiction where the car was driven, on the official sites only
- **Rule:** each jurisdiction keeps its own record, so a clean result in one place says nothing about the others. Use the official lookup of each place and never pay anyone (a *gestor*, an app) just to look.
- **Why:** a fine can sit unseen for months and is repriced meanwhile (VEH-MUL-3); a missing result in the national system does not prove there is no provincial fine.
- **How:**

| where | what it covers | how | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| PBA: `infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar`, "Consultar/Pagar infracciones" | provincial roads and the municipalities in the provincial system | by plate (*dominio*) or by ID number | has a reCAPTCHA a person must solve; shows the court, the coupon and the case state; the 0-800-222-0024 line (8-17 h Monday-Friday, 9-15 h Saturday) answers |
| CABA: the City's infractions and scoring consultation (reachable from buenosaires.gob.ar and from the City's chatbot) | the City | by plate or ID number | the status "A resolver en UACF" only means it is with the City controller; the discount can still apply if no controller hearing was requested |
| National: `consultainfracciones.seguridadvial.gob.ar` (system SINAI of the national road-safety agency, ANSV) | the jurisdictions adhered to the national infractions system (the page says so itself) | by plate or by ID document and sex | one reported case showed no fines while a provincial fine existed: one case, not a rule, but a clean national result never replaces the local lookups |
| Other provinces and municipalities | their own | each has a site | check the one where you drove; many municipalities also keep their own records |
| Unofficial apps and aggregators (some gather 30+ jurisdictions) | repackage official data | | free but verify on the official site; never enter more than a plate |

- **Verify:** every result must be confirmable on the issuer's site or in its call centre; note the record (*acta*) number.
- **Don't:** give an unofficial site your ID number, address or a photo of your licence; paying "to find out" is a scam sign (VEH-MUL-41).
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar (home and que-hago, read 2026-11-15); consultainfracciones.seguridadvial.gob.ar (read 2026-11-15; page text: "jurisdicciones adheridas al sistema nacional"); buenosaires.gob.ar/tramites/turnos-para-resolver-infracciones (read 2026-11-15); the case where the national lookup missed a provincial fine, the aggregator apps and the exact list of jurisdictions feeding SINAI: Not verified in a primary source.

### VEH-MUL-6: Search by plate, keep the plate private and check every two months
- **Rule:** use the plate rather than the ID number when you can, keep the plate out of public repositories and chats you do not control, and check at the start, every two months (the PBA UF rhythm) and before any procedure (licence, transfer, sale).
- **Why:** the lookup costs nothing; an early fine can be paid at half price (VEH-MUL-10) and a late one cannot.
- **How:** put a reminder after each PBA UF change (Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Sep, Nov). Use the plate on the three official lookups of VEH-MUL-5; add the City's lookup if you drove in CABA.
- **Verify:** save the date and the result of each check (a screenshot without personal data).
- **Don't:** post a plate with a fine on a forum "to ask for help".
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar/unidad-fija-detalle (the value changes bimonthly, read 2026-11-15); practical advice from the earlier version of this guide, Not verified in a primary source.

### VEH-MUL-7: Read a PBA lookup result field by field
- **Rule:** the record number, the dates, the coupon state and the case state are different things; read each before acting.
- **Why:** a "pending validation" case state means a defence is in, a coupon "expired" means the discount is gone, and the amount shown is recalculated every time.
- **How:**

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| record (*acta*) number | the id of the fine; quote it in every message to the court |
| generated / coupon due date | when the coupon was made and when its voluntary or normal payment window ends |
| coupon state | paid, pending, expired |
| case state | state of the case, separate from the coupon: for example "dispute pending validation" = a defence (*descargo*) was filed and is not decided; ask the court office for the exact meaning |
| amount | UF x UF value of the day the page is shown, so it can differ from the printed coupon |
| infraction code and description | what the fine is for |
| court (*radicacion*) | which office holds the case; this is who to call |
| interest | recalculated when the coupon is printed again |

- **Verify:** compare the court shown with the list "Ver Juzgado donde radica la causa" on the PBA page "Recibi una infraccion, que hago".
- **Don't:** pay a coupon whose state says the defence is pending without asking the court (VEH-MUL-16).
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar (que-hago and preguntas-frecuentes, read 2026-11-15); the field meanings come from the earlier version of this guide (read as screens, 2026-10) and the exact meaning of each case state: Not verified in a primary source.

### VEH-MUL-8: Ask the vehicle registry for a fines report (SUGIT) when buying or when the lookups disagree
- **Rule:** any interested person can ask a car registry office (*registro seccional*) for the report of fines debt (*informe de deuda de multas*) of a plate using the type request 13-D; the registry consults the unified system SUGIT under its agreements with each jurisdiction.
- **Why:** it checks several jurisdictions at once and is the document used in a sale (VEH-MUL-39).
- **How:** at the registry, or online through the DNRPA procedures site, paying the fee online; the registry sends the report by email (2018 notice: within 24 hours for the fines report, 48 hours for others). Any adult with an ID can ask. If there is debt, the owner or a person designated by the owner can pay it, or the owner can sign a refusal to pay (*negativa de pago*) at the registry (VEH-MUL-23). The holder, or a third person with a notarised note listing the records, signs the refusal.
- **Verify:** the report carries the issuing registry office and date; check that the plate and holder match.
- **Don't:** treat a report as valid for months: it is a snapshot of the day.
- **Source:** Disposicion DNRPA 142/2024 art. 4 (substitutes arts. 19 and 27, Part 5, Chapter XIV, Title II of the Digesto; BO 2024-09-19; read on argentina.gob.ar 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar news "Ahora es posible tramitar online el informe de infracciones" (2018-07-11, read 2026-11-15; the fee and delivery times may have changed: Not verified for 2026).

### VEH-MUL-9: Keep your address current: notices are valid at three addresses
- **Rule:** in PBA a notification is valid at the electronic address you declared, at the address written in the record (*acta*) or at the address on your driving licence, in that order; legal persons must set up an electronic road address (*Domicilio Vial Electronico*).
- **Why:** if a notice goes to an old address and nobody reads it, the voluntary payment window and the time to file a defence still run.
- **How:** update the address on the licence at the issuing office; register or log in on `infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar` ("Acceso Usuarios", "Mi Web") and set the electronic address to receive notifications (the PBA ministry resolution on the Domicilio Vial Electronico extends the obligation to any person or company doing procedures at licence centres, administrative courts and inspection plants). If a notice never arrived or arrived late and no defence has been filed, you can ask for a 30-day extension of the voluntary payment with the 50% discount from "Mi Web" (VEH-MUL-10).
- **Verify:** from the same login you can see the cases linked to your ID and plates.
- **Don't:** say "I never got it" without proof of your address on record.
- **Source:** Ley 15.002 PBA art. 4 (modifies art. 35 b and c of Ley 13.927; text read on normas.gba.gob.ar 2026-11-15); PBA ministry resolution on Domicilio Vial Electronico (2023, read 2026-11-15); infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15).

## Paying and the voluntary-payment discount

### VEH-MUL-10: Pay inside the voluntary-payment window to get the 50% reduction
- **Rule:** if you accept the infraction, pay inside the voluntary-payment period of the first notice and you get a 50% reduction. National law provides it (Ley 24.449 art. 85 a); PBA and CABA apply it with their own deadlines. Paying this way has the effect of a final sanction.
- **Why:** it is the cheapest legal way to close a fine, and it disappears when the window ends or when you file a defence.
- **How:** PBA: 50% of the minimum value of the sanction; the due date is printed on automatic (camera) notices; on records written by an officer the window is 30 business days from the record. If the notice never arrived or arrived late, and you have not filed anything at court, "Mi Web" lets you get a 30-day extension with the same discount. CABA: 40 days from the notification, and the discount is lost if you ask for a hearing with a controller (VEH-MUL-19); a fine with the status "A resolver en UACF" can still be paid with the discount if no controller summoned you; the legend "A resolver con mandatarios" means it was already resolved and only the assigned collector can cancel it. Windows can change by regulation: read the coupon.
- **Verify:** the coupon shows the due date; after paying, save the receipt and check that the lookup shows "paid" (VEH-MUL-13).
- **Don't:** pay with the discount a fine you plan to dispute, or let the date pass "to think about it".
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 85 (text on leyes-ar.com read 2026-11-15; the original 1995 text on argentina.gob.ar said 25% and it was later raised to 50%); infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes and que-hago (read 2026-11-15); buenosaires.gob.ar/tramites/turnos-para-resolver-infracciones (read 2026-11-15); the national art. 85 limit "twice a year for serious faults" appears in the original text: Not verified in the current consolidated text.

### VEH-MUL-11: Know what paying voluntarily means beyond the money
- **Rule:** a voluntary payment is a recognition of the infraction and counts as a final sanction, so it stays in your record; ask what it does to your points before paying a serious fault.
- **Why:** the 50% saving can be small compared with losing points (VEH-MUL-30) or with a repeat-offender surcharge.
- **How:** Ley 24.449 art. 85 a says the voluntary payment "tendra los efectos de una sancion firme" in all cases; the same article, in its original text, also limited the use for serious faults to twice a year. National points are deducted from the driver named in the record, or from the registered owner for automatic records (VEH-MUL-31). Ask the issuing court, or read the points rules of your licence jurisdiction, whether points are deducted at the moment of a voluntary payment.
- **Verify:** after paying, look at your points on Mi Scoring (VEH-MUL-34) or on the City lookup.
- **Don't:** assume paying "makes it disappear" from the licence record.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 85 a (read 2026-11-15); Decreto 437/2011 official summary, argentina.gob.ar (read 2026-11-15); the exact moment at which points are deducted after a voluntary payment: Not verified in a primary source.

### VEH-MUL-12: Pay by official channels only and keep the receipt
- **Rule:** pay from the official site or with the printed coupon, in the channels the jurisdiction lists, and keep the receipt and the record number.
- **Why:** a payment in the wrong place is not credited, and fake coupons exist (VEH-MUL-41).
- **How:** PBA: on the infractions site with credit or debit cards, e-transfer, DEBIN, home banking (Red Link, Pago Mis Cuentas), Banco Provincia branches, Provincia Net, Link ATMs, Rapipago and Pago Facil. CABA: Pago Facil, Rapipago, Banco Ciudad, Banco Provincia, Mercado Pago, Pago Mis Cuentas, the City's website, Bapro Pagos and Provincia Net; the City says credit is immediate in all channels on its page (cash channels can take up to 24 hours on a second line of the same page).
- **Verify:** the lookup (VEH-MUL-5) shows the coupon as paid.
- **Don't:** transfer money to a person's account "to clear the fine".
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar que-hago (read 2026-11-15); buenosaires.gob.ar/tramites/turnos-para-resolver-infracciones (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-MUL-13: Pay at least 72 hours before any procedure that checks fines
- **Rule:** in PBA a payment takes about 72 hours to register, and until then the fine still blocks procedures; pay with margin before a licence renewal or a transfer.
- **Why:** bank rules delay crediting; the system does not know you paid yet.
- **How:** pay at least three working days before the appointment; if the block remains after that, go with the original receipt to the nearest provincial administrative traffic court (*juzgado administrativo de infracciones de transito provincial*) to check that the payment was applied to the right record. CABA says crediting is immediate on its channels and up to 24 hours for some.
- **Verify:** the lookup shows "paid"; ask the court for a written confirmation if you need it for an appointment.
- **Don't:** pay the day before the licence appointment.
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15); buenosaires.gob.ar/tramites/turnos-para-resolver-infracciones (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-MUL-14: If the window expired, reprint an updated coupon and expect no discount
- **Rule:** after the voluntary period you can still pay in PBA by reprinting an updated coupon (or wait for the summons that will carry a new coupon), without the 50% reduction.
- **Why:** the debt does not disappear; it is repriced (VEH-MUL-3).
- **How:** reprint from `infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar` (VEH-MUL-5). The coupon of the court cites an interest article of the fiscal code of the province (Ley 10.397); whether and how much late interest applies to traffic fines is not confirmed in a primary source, so read the amount on the new coupon and ask the court. Ask for a 30-day extension first if the notice never reached you (VEH-MUL-10).
- **Verify:** the coupon shows the due date and the amount; compare with UF x value (VEH-MUL-1).
- **Don't:** keep paying "the old amount" from a first coupon.
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15); interest on traffic fines: Not verified in a primary source (the earlier version of this guide read a coupon that cites Ley 10.397, text not checked).

### VEH-MUL-15: Ask for a payment plan only after reading what you give up
- **Rule:** after the voluntary period a judge can grant instalments; joining a plan is an irrevocable acknowledgement of the debt.
- **Why:** it makes a big debt payable but you waive all defences and appeals, and it interrupts prescription (VEH-MUL-25).
- **How:** PBA: the judge may grant instalments under the rules in force; the request is made in person by the offender or a representative. Joining implies express and irrevocable acknowledgement, waiver of defences and appeals (including those already filed) and counts as an interruption of prescription. A 2021 provincial plan (Resolution 69/20, in force from 2021-01-15) let people pay in 6 or 12 instalments without interest, taking the UF value of the day of joining, so the price froze while the UF kept rising; it had a validity of 90 days and required login on `infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar`, tab "mis planes de pago". News in 2025 reported a similar plan: whether any plan is open today must be read on the PBA page. A plan lapses if instalments go unpaid (reported: three alternating unpaid instalments).
- **Verify:** the lookup shows the plan; ask the court for the plan terms in writing.
- **Don't:** join a plan for a fine you have a strong defence against.
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15); gba.gob.ar news "La Provincia lanza plan de pagos" (2021-01-15, read 2026-11-15); Ley 24.449 art. 85 c (instalments for low-income offenders); whether a plan is open now and the lapse rule: Not verified in a primary source (news 2025-2026).

### VEH-MUL-16: Do not pay a fine you are disputing without asking the court first
- **Rule:** if you filed a defence (*descargo*) or a request for a hearing, ask the court before paying the same record.
- **Why:** paying can be read as acknowledging the infraction and closing your defence.
- **How:** ask the court that holds the case (the lookup names it) in writing or in person and keep the answer; if you decide to give up the dispute, pay from the updated coupon and keep the receipt.
- **Verify:** the case state in the lookup changes to paid or closed.
- **Don't:** pay a coupon of a case marked "defence pending" because a collector or a message says it is "the last day".
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: practical rule from the earlier version of this guide; consistent with Ley 24.449 art. 85 a (voluntary payment as acknowledgement), read 2026-11-15.

## Disputing: defence, hearings and courts

### VEH-MUL-17: Decide between paying now and disputing with a calculation
- **Rule:** paying inside the voluntary window costs 0.5 x F, where F is today's amount. Disputing costs (1 - p) x F' if you lose with probability (1 - p), where F' is the amount when the case is decided and p is your chance of winning. Disputing only pays if p > 1 - 0.5 x F / F'.
- **Why:** filing a defence loses the discount (VEH-MUL-10), so disputing is a bet; because the UF rises, it pays only with a strong case.
- **How:** worked example with invented figures: a 100 UF fine at the PBA UF of 2,340 is F = 234,000; paying now = 117,000. If the case takes a year and the UF rises 20%, F' = 280,800 and you need p > 1 - 117,000 / 280,800 = 58.3%. If the UF rises only 10%, F' = 257,400 and p > 54.5%. With F' = F the threshold is 50%. Dispute only with strong evidence: dated photos, signage, lights, speed limits, the record's errors (VEH-MUL-28).
- **Verify:** redo the calculation with the amount in the lookup and your best guess of the UF rise.
- **Don't:** dispute "on principle" a fine you committed.
- **Source:** calculation (this file); discount and its loss on defence: infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes and buenosaires.gob.ar turnos page (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-MUL-18: File a defence (*descargo*) in PBA with evidence and the record number
- **Rule:** if you believe you did not commit the infraction you can file a defence in person or in writing (by registered mail if you live more than 60 km from the court) before the competent judge; there is also a web form.
- **Why:** if you say nothing, the judge decides and the fine is collected; you also keep the right to appeal if you filed in time.
- **How:** no special form is required, but the defence must include evidence and contact data and, to be identified, the case number, the holder's name, the ID number and the plate. On `infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar` use "Presentar Descargos" (web) or download the form and instructions. National law (Ley 24.449 art. 71) says a person living more than 60 km from the court can defend in writing before the jurisdiction where the fault happened or before their own. Keep the receipt, copies of the evidence (dated photos, street views, anything showing signs, lights, speed limits) and the written answers.
- **Verify:** the case state changes to "dispute pending validation" or similar (VEH-MUL-7).
- **Don't:** send only a complaint with no evidence and no record number.
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes and que-hago (read 2026-11-15); Ley 24.449 art. 71 (text read on leyes-ar.com 2026-11-15; consolidated); the deadline to file a defence is not stated on the PBA FAQ: read it on the notice.

### VEH-MUL-19: In CABA, ask for a hearing with a fault controller (*controlador de faltas*)
- **Rule:** in the City of Buenos Aires a defence is made in a hearing with a controller, by video call or in person with an appointment; doing so forfeits the 50% voluntary-payment discount and resolves all records linked to the ID or the plate you use.
- **Why:** the hearing is the only way to contest, and it takes in every record of that ID or plate.
- **How:** check the infractions and scoring lookup with ID or plate, choose "Audiencia virtual" and book the video call (the page says through the City chatbot, Boti); upload a valid ID (front and back) of the person on the record or of the holder or authorised person of the plate, plus the green or blue vehicle card (*cedula verde o azul*); in person, request an appointment first. The procedure is free; the cost of the infractions follows the Ley Tarifaria. The office is the Direccion General de Administracion de Infracciones (Monday-Friday 8-19:30 h); the City line is 147.
- **Verify:** you receive a notification with the time and the link; records resolved "con mandatarios" cannot be reviewed again by a controller.
- **Don't:** book a hearing for one record if others in the same ID or plate are fines you want to pay with the discount.
- **Source:** buenosaires.gob.ar/tramites/turnos-para-resolver-infracciones (read 2026-11-15, Ministerio de Justicia - Direccion General de Administracion de Infracciones).

### VEH-MUL-20: Know what happens if you do nothing
- **Rule:** if you do not pay and do not file a defence, the administrative judge issues a decision and notifies it; if the fine is still unpaid it is collected by an executive proceeding (*juicio de apremio*).
- **Why:** an executive proceeding can lead to seizure of property and financial assets, inhibitions and vehicle seizure.
- **How:** PBA FAQ: "Se dictara resolucion por parte del Juez Administrativo y se notificara ... Caso contrario se procedera a su cobro por la via de juicio de apremio (habilitando la procedencia de embargo de inmuebles y activos financieros, inhibiciones, secuestros, etc.)". National law (Ley 24.449 art. 85 b) allows collection by executive proceedings and makes the certificate of the adjudicating authority sufficient title.
- **Verify:** the case state of the lookup moves to an executed or "firm" state; call the court.
- **Don't:** assume that ignoring the notice makes the fine expire: see VEH-MUL-25.
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15); Ley 24.449 art. 85 b (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-MUL-21: Say who was driving or who owns the car if it is not you
- **Rule:** when the driver is not identified, the law presumes the owner committed the infraction, unless the owner proves the car was sold or was not in their possession or custody and names the buyer, holder or custodian.
- **Why:** fines from cameras always go to the registered owner; if you sold a car without transferring it, you keep receiving them.
- **How:** in the defence say "I sold the car on [date]" or "the car was lent to X", attach the proof (bill of sale or the sale report to the registry, VEH-MUL-40) and name the buyer or custodian. PBA says that without this the registered holders are made liable.
- **Verify:** the court answers in writing and the record moves to the named person.
- **Don't:** reply "that was not me" without naming anyone.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 75 c (original text on argentina.gob.ar read 2026-11-15); infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-MUL-22: Know which court handles what, and the appeal path
- **Rule:** the issuing jurisdiction decides: in PBA the provincial administrative traffic courts (*juzgados administrativos de infracciones de transito provinciales*) for provincial roads and each municipality's own *juzgado de faltas* for municipal fines; in CABA the controllers and the administrative unit that resolves faults. Ask the court named by the lookup.
- **Why:** calling the wrong office wastes the voluntary window.
- **How:** PBA: `infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar` lists the courts with address and hours ("Ver listado de Juzgados"). Appeal: the national law, as consolidated, allows an appeal for revocation (*recurso de revocatoria*) with a subsidiary appeal, filed and argued before the same authority that decided within 10 business days of notification; if the revocation is denied the appeal goes to the provincial administrative-contentious chamber with suspensive effect. Read the deadline on the decision itself.
- **Verify:** the notice names the court and says how to appeal.
- **Don't:** let the appeal deadline pass; it is short.
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar que-hago (read 2026-11-15); Ley 24.449 art. 74 (consolidated text on leyes-ar.com read 2026-11-15; the original 1995 text read on argentina.gob.ar said 5 days, so the current deadline is Not verified in a primary source).

### VEH-MUL-23: Understand the refusal to pay (*negativa de pago*) before signing it
- **Rule:** the refusal to pay is a declaration made at the car registry, by the holder or by a third person with a notarised note, that you will not pay the fines reported on the plate at that moment, so that the transfer or procedure goes ahead.
- **Why:** since 2024 the registry cannot block an inscription for regular debts, but the fines do not disappear: they stay with the person who committed them and the DNRPA says the refusal is made on type request 13-D.
- **How:** at the registry that issued the fines report (VEH-MUL-8); the system does not accept a partial refusal: you declare the refusal for the whole of the reported fines. The refusal does not cancel or reduce the debt.
- **Verify:** ask for a copy of the form and of the report.
- **Don't:** sign it as a way to "get rid" of old fines.
- **Source:** Disposicion DNRPA 142/2024 (BO 2024-09-19; read on argentina.gob.ar 2026-11-15; request 13-D for the refusal); argentina.gob.ar news 2018-07-11 (who signs it); the "all or nothing" rule comes from the DNRPA SUGIT manual found by search (dnrpa.gov.ar) and was Not read in full.

### VEH-MUL-24: If the licence was retained, follow the 30-day and 90-day rule
- **Rule:** when an officer retains a licence in PBA, the record works as a summons and lets you drive for at most 30 calendar days; present yourself in person at the competent court with your ID and the situation that caused the retention solved.
- **Why:** if you do not appear within 90 days from the summons, the retained licence is destroyed and the right to drive lapses.
- **How:** read the date on the record; go to the court on the list of `infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar`; bring the ID and the proof that the cause is fixed (for example the renewed document).
- **Verify:** the court gives a receipt or returns the licence.
- **Don't:** keep driving after the 30 days.
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15; the page cites art. 38 of Ley 13.927).

## Prescription

### VEH-MUL-25: Do not rely on prescription; know the periods and what interrupts them
- **Rule:** under national law the action for a minor fault prescribes in 2 years and the action for a serious fault and the sanctions in 5 years; in all cases the period is interrupted by committing a serious fault or by the course of the contravention, executive or judicial proceedings.
- **Why:** an open case can move at any time, and a plan adhesion also interrupts it (VEH-MUL-15); a fine is not "cancelled" just because years passed.
- **How:** national text (art. 89 of Ley 24.449 as modified by Ley 26.363 art. 36): "a) A los DOS (2) anos para la accion por falta leve; b) A los CINCO (5) anos para la accion por falta grave y para sanciones; En todos los casos, se interrumpe por la comision de una falta grave o por la secuela del juicio contravencional, ejecutivo o judicial." The 1995 original text said 1 and 2 years. PBA follows the national criterion through Ley 13.927. CABA: reported 5 years for all infractions under Ley 451 art. 15 (secondary). Other provinces differ (reported: Cordoba 3 years; Mendoza 2, 3 and 4 years; secondary): read your own. Example: a minor fault recorded on 2023-03-10 would prescribe on 2025-03-10 unless something interrupted it (invented).
- **Verify:** ask the court in writing whether the record shows as prescribed, and whether the case was interrupted; a prescribed record can still appear in the lookup until the court updates it.
- **Don't:** ignore a fine for two years and then renew your licence expecting it to be gone.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 89 (consolidated text on leyes-ar.com read 2026-11-15, with "Vigente, con las modificaciones"; the original text on argentina.gob.ar read 2026-11-15 shows 1 and 2 years; Ley 26.363 art. 36 modifies it per leyes-ar.com/transito_y_seguridad_vial/36.htm); infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar (plan interrupts prescription; read 2026-11-15); CABA, Cordoba and Mendoza periods: Not verified in a primary source (iProfesional, La Gaceta, Ambito 2025-2026); a 2023 bill (expediente 4686-D-2023) to change art. 89 was not found enacted.

## Camera and radar tickets

### VEH-MUL-26: Check that the device was homologated and authorised
- **Rule:** a speed camera or radar (*cinemometro*) must be an approved device that cannot be altered by hand and must be authorised by the competent authority; in PBA the provincial road-safety office authorises every device in provincial and municipal jurisdiction, enforcement cannot be privatised or conceded and the people who operate them or issue the records must be public officials.
- **Why:** a ticket from an unauthorised or unapproved device is the strongest ground for a defence.
- **How:** PBA publishes a map of its fixed devices: the page says there are 252 fixed devices on provincial roads and attaches the documents required for each to operate (menu "Radares" on `infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar`); devices listed there have the authorization of the Direccion Provincial. On national routes the national agency (ANSV) keeps the list of homologated and authorised devices (a public listing of homologated radars was published by the agency in 2019; the agency removed homologations of some devices in 2026, reported by news).
- **Verify:** find the device or place on the PBA map or the ANSV list and compare with your notice.
- **Don't:** claim "the radar was not approved" without having checked the lists.
- **Source:** Ley 15.002 PBA art. 3 (new art. 28 of Ley 13.927; text read on normas.gba.gob.ar 2026-11-15); infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar equiposDeConstatacion (read 2026-11-15); ANSV removals and unauthorised radars in 2026: Not verified in a primary source (minutouno.com, canal26.com, iprofesional.com, diariojudicial.com 2026).

### VEH-MUL-27: Count the 60 business days of a fixed-camera notice
- **Rule:** in PBA the notice of an infraction caught by a fixed automatic speed camera must be made within 60 business days (*dias habiles*) of the date it was committed; any other infraction detected on the road must be notified or made known to the driver within 10 km of the place, when circumstances allow.
- **Why:** if the notice is late the defence has a legal ground; if you do not compare the dates you cannot use it.
- **How:** compare the infraction date with the date the notice was issued or arrived. Business days exclude weekends (and holidays): 60 business days are about 12 weeks (84 calendar days) before holidays. Example (invented): infraction on Tuesday 2026-03-03; counting only Monday-Friday and ignoring holidays, the 60th business day is Tuesday 2026-05-26; a notice made after that date is late. Ask the court which date counts (issue or reception).
- **Verify:** the notice or the lookup shows the infraction date and the notice date (VEH-MUL-7).
- **Don't:** count calendar days or assume the deadline applies to a ticket from an officer on the road.
- **Source:** Ley 15.002 PBA art. 3 (new art. 28 of Ley 13.927: "no mayor a sesenta (60) dias habiles de la fecha de su comision"; read on normas.gba.gob.ar 2026-11-15); a general national notice deadline for national routes: Not verified in a primary source (a legal template and one news article mention 60 business days without citing a norm).

### VEH-MUL-28: Check the evidence the notice must contain
- **Rule:** an automatic photographic record must show at least the identification of the vehicle, the infraction committed and the place, day and hour; check the photo or video, the signage and the device data before deciding.
- **Why:** an unreadable plate, a wrong place or a missing date weakens the ticket.
- **How:** read the notice and ask the court for the photo or video; compare the place and the speed limit with the street (signs, road works); check the plate, model and colour; check the date of the last verification of the device if it is printed (reported by news: device brand, model, serial number, and last verification date should appear; Not verified). Keep your own dated photos of the signs.
- **Verify:** note every mismatch in writing in the defence with the record number (VEH-MUL-18).
- **Don't:** use a defence for a clear, correct ticket (VEH-MUL-17 explains the cost).
- **Source:** Decreto 196/2025 art. 7 (substitutes art. 21 of the regulation of Ley 24.449; text read on argentina.gob.ar 2026-11-15); signage, INTI verification and the printed device data: Not verified in a primary source (La Gaceta 2025-12-26 and similar guides).

### VEH-MUL-29: Remember a camera ticket goes to the registered owner
- **Rule:** the fine of an automatic record goes to the registered owner of the plate, and so do the points on the driving licence, unless the owner proves the car was stolen or was not in their custody.
- **Why:** an owner who lent the car still receives it and must name the driver.
- **How:** see VEH-MUL-21; for the points, VEH-MUL-31. If the car was stolen, attach the police report.
- **Verify:** the court acknowledges the named driver.
- **Don't:** pay a fine for someone else without a written agreement.
- **Source:** Decreto 437/2011 official summary (argentina.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Ley 24.449 art. 75 c (read 2026-11-15).

## The national points licence (scoring)

### VEH-MUL-30: Know that the licence carries 20 points and where the system applies
- **Rule:** the national driving licence (*Licencia Nacional de Conducir*) carries 20 points, deducted gradually for infractions; the national system is operated by the road-safety agency and each jurisdiction applies it when it adheres.
- **Why:** points decide suspension; a driver can be disqualified without any new accident.
- **How:** the legal base is Decreto 437/2011 (modified by Decreto 242/2022); article 13 f of Ley 24.449 says the licence is issued with a fixed number of points. Adhesion differs: in 2022 the national agency listed 9 provinces and 7 towns (Santa Cruz, Jujuy, Misiones, Catamarca, La Rioja, Tucumán, Buenos Aires, Rio Negro, Neuquén, and the towns Viedma, Neuquén, La Rioja, Ingeniero Jacobacci, San Antonio Oeste, Puerto San Julian, Villa La Angostura); more may have joined since. PBA applies a unified points system; CABA has its own system, the permanent driver-evaluation system (also 20 points, but other deductions and sanctions per a news article). In 2026 the agency approved unique codes for every infraction in the points table (Disposicion ANSV 167/2026, in force from 2026-06-19); the points per infraction did not change.
- **Verify:** use Mi Scoring (VEH-MUL-34) and the City lookup if your licence was issued in CABA; ask your licence office whether your jurisdiction adhered.
- **Don't:** assume that a driving licence from another province works under the same points rules.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar Licencia de conducir - Sistema de puntos and Scoring nacional (read 2026-11-15; the scoring page is dated 2022); Ley 24.449 art. 13 f and art. 84 (consolidated; leyes-ar.com read 2026-11-15); Disposicion ANSV 167/2026 (BO 2026-06-18, read 2026-11-15); current list of adhered jurisdictions and the CABA system: Not verified in a primary source (iProfesional 2026-06-28, palabrasdelderecho.com).

### VEH-MUL-31: Learn who loses the points
- **Rule:** points are deducted from the driver named in the record; in automatic records they are deducted from the registered owner, unless the owner proves the vehicle was stolen or not in their custody.
- **Why:** the owner of a lent car can lose points for a camera ticket.
- **How:** if several faults are committed together, at most 15 points are deducted, except when the sum is lower, in which case the lower figure applies. Name the driver in time (VEH-MUL-21) or prove the theft.
- **Verify:** Mi Scoring shows the history (VEH-MUL-34).
- **Don't:** lend the car to people who will not answer for it.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar Licencia de conducir - Sistema de puntos (Decreto 437/2011 summary, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-MUL-32: Use the reported points table only as a guide
- **Rule:** each infraction takes a fixed number of points from the table in Annex I of Decreto 437/2011; the main rows reported by the press are listed below, but the table in force is the official one.
- **Why:** the official page shows the table as an image, and news sites copy it with errors.
- **How:** reported main deductions (2022 and July 2026 news, consistent; not complete): 4 points: driving without a valid technical inspection (*VTV*), not wearing the seat belt properly; 5 points: expired licence, no helmet on a motorcycle, running a red light, speeding by less than 30%; 10 points: driving with physical or mental impairments, driving under alcohol, drugs or certain medicines, speeding by more than 30%; 20 points: driving while disqualified or suspended, taking part in illegal races. Read the full table on the national scoring page and in the decree annex.
- **Verify:** the points of an infraction show on your record in Mi Scoring.
- **Don't:** quote this list as complete or as the table of another jurisdiction (CABA has another scale).
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar/transporte/politicas-seguridad-vial/scoring-nacional (table is an image; read 2026-11-15); Not verified in a primary source: rows from unidiversidad.com.ar (2022-08-03) and minutouno.com (2026-07-13).

### VEH-MUL-33: Know what happens at zero points and how to recover
- **Rule:** if the licence loses all points the holder is disqualified: 60 days the first time, 120 days the second, 180 days the third, doubling for each further time; for professional licences the periods are halved; road-safety courses must be taken and passed each time; after the period the licence is restored with 20 points.
- **Why:** it is the penalty that actually removes the licence.
- **How:** while points remain, the lost points are recovered in two ways: 2 years without traffic sanctions restores all of them; approving road-safety courses gives up to 4 points each, usable once every 2 years (once a year for professional licences). Example (invented): a driver with 6 points who takes an approved course recovers up to 4 points, reaching 10; two years later with no sanction the licence goes back to 20.
- **Verify:** the courses are offered on the national agency's site (reported as free); confirm in Mi Scoring.
- **Don't:** keep driving with a zero-point licence during the disqualification.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar Licencia de conducir - Sistema de puntos (Decreto 437/2011 summary, read 2026-11-15); the same page lists "Mi Scoring Nacional".

### VEH-MUL-34: Check your points on Mi Scoring
- **Rule:** to know your points, use the official Mi Scoring page of the national road-safety agency.
- **Why:** it shows the balance and the infractions, and avoids paid intermediaries.
- **How:** open `puntos.seguridadvial.gob.ar/scoring` (the official page links to it from the national scoring page) and follow the steps; the CABA lookup (infractions and scoring, from buenosaires.gob.ar) shows the City's score for licences issued in the City.
- **Verify:** the domain ends in `.gob.ar` and the page is reached from argentina.gob.ar.
- **Don't:** enter your ID number in a site found by an ad or a message.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar Licencia de conducir - Sistema de puntos (read 2026-11-15; link to Mi Scoring); puntos.seguridadvial.gob.ar/scoring (page loads as a script application; its content was Not read).

### VEH-MUL-35: Novice drivers can be suspended for serious faults
- **Rule:** a novice driver who commits a serious fault during the first two years of the licence can be suspended and has to take the theory-practice exam again.
- **Why:** the first two years are watched; a serious fault ends the licence early.
- **How:** Decreto 196/2025 (new art. 13 d of the regulation of Ley 24.449) added this cause of suspension; the national agency set the procedure in Disposicion 91/2025: it can ask the issuing jurisdiction to suspend the licence of a novice driver for serious faults and of a driver who shows a psychophysical unfitness. Ley 24.449 art. 13 d also requires the novice sign on the car for the first six months.
- **Verify:** the issuing jurisdiction notifies the suspension and the conditions.
- **Don't:** treat a serious fault in the first two years as "just a fine".
- **Source:** Disposicion ANSV 91/2025 (considerandos; BO read on boletinoficial.gob.ar 2026-11-15); Ley 24.449 art. 13 d (read 2026-11-15); whether other jurisdictions also apply it: Not verified.

## Licence renewal, certificates and records

### VEH-MUL-36: Expect fines to be checked when you renew the licence
- **Rule:** many offices check for unpaid fines when you renew a driving licence; in CABA this is reported as a requirement since May 2025; in PBA its legal basis is disputed.
- **Why:** an old fine can stop the renewal; and the law lets the licence office ask for the national record of traffic antecedents.
- **How:** Ley 24.449 art. 13 c lets the licence be granted for up to 5 years and says the holder with records of serious infractions, or of more infractions than the regulation sets, must pass the theory-practice exams again at renewal; art. 14 b says that before granting a licence the office must ask the national traffic antecedents registry. CABA: the City says that the infraction-free certificate has always been required; its ombudsman disagrees. PBA: sources say that the certificate of no debt is not a legal requirement but many municipalities ask for it; the PBA regulation (Decreto 532/09) is quoted for it and courts have ruled against it in individual cases; a bill to remove it was reported in August 2026. The safe route is to check fines (VEH-MUL-5), pay or settle them 72 hours before (VEH-MUL-13) and ask the licence office what it requires.
- **Verify:** the office tells you in writing what is blocking the renewal; the Defensoria of PBA asked municipalities in 2023 to guarantee renewals when fines have no final judgment (secondary).
- **Don't:** drive with an expired licence while you dispute the renewal: an expired licence is itself a fault with points (VEH-MUL-32).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 13 and 14 (consolidated, leyes-ar.com, read 2026-11-15); Not verified in a primary source: renewal blocked by fines in CABA and PBA (iProfesional, Ambito, Cronista, canal26.com 2023-2026).

### VEH-MUL-37: Do not confuse the CENAT with a fines record
- **Rule:** the national traffic antecedents certificate (*CENAT*, *Certificado Nacional de Antecedentes de Transito*) reports criminal records connected with traffic events; it is not the list of your administrative fines.
- **Why:** people pay for the CENAT thinking it clears their fines, and it does not.
- **How:** before going to a licence office, complete the CENAT form on the national agency's site, print the payment slip and pay it at the listed entities; PBA residents use the PBA link on the same page. The certificate comes from the national recidivism registry. The price changes by disposition of the agency: one secondary source reported ARS 10,150 in July 2026 (read the slip). For fines use the lookups (VEH-MUL-5).
- **Verify:** the slip is generated on an argentina.gob.ar or seguridadvial.gob.ar page.
- **Don't:** pay a middleman for the CENAT.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar/node/26702 (page "CENAT", read 2026-11-15); price: Not verified in a primary source (indicadores.ar 2026-07-03).

### VEH-MUL-38: Pay or settle fines and keep records when you change plates, address or jurisdiction
- **Rule:** a fine follows the person who committed it (or the owner for camera tickets), not the car; moving or changing the licence jurisdiction does not erase it.
- **Why:** each jurisdiction keeps its own records, and the licence office queries them (VEH-MUL-36).
- **How:** before moving, check your plates and ID in every jurisdiction where you drove (VEH-MUL-5), pay or dispute, keep receipts for years, and keep the court's confirmation that a record is closed.
- **Verify:** the lookups show nothing pending; keep a copy of the closure.
- **Don't:** throw away the receipts.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 75 and art. 85 (read 2026-11-15); practical advice: Not verified in a primary source.

## Buying or selling a car

### VEH-MUL-39: When buying, check fines everywhere and know the registry no longer blocks the transfer
- **Rule:** check the plate in every jurisdiction and ask for the fines report (VEH-MUL-8) before paying a seller; the registry now registers the transfer even when there are regular fines or patente debts, so the debt is a matter between buyer and seller.
- **Why:** the fine stays with the person who committed it, but a buyer can be the one chased for a plate with camera tickets and a seller who disappears.
- **How:** since DNU 70/2023 art. 355, the article 9 of the Regimen Juridico del Automotor says that regular debts for fines or patentes cannot prevent the registration of a car or its transfer; the DNRPA (Disposicion 142/2024) reflected that: registries only collect stamp tax, patente tax or fines if the parties ask expressly, and the fines report is requested with request 13-D when the holder or buyer marks it in the form 08. Agree in writing who pays what: ask the seller to settle first or retain the amount from the price, and keep proof. The DNRPA 2019 buyer guide still said the debt had to be paid or a refusal signed at the registry: that guide is older than the 2024 rule; ask the registry what applies now.
- **Verify:** get the fines report dated on the day of the sale and a copy of form 08 with the box marked.
- **Don't:** pay in cash without a receipt and a report.
- **Source:** DNU 70/2023 art. 355 as quoted in Disposicion DNRPA 142/2024 (BO 2024-09-19, read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar news "Compra-venta de autos: 15 preguntas" (2019-04-26, older, read 2026-11-15); what exactly happens with irregular debts (for example executed fines): Not verified in a primary source.

### VEH-MUL-40: When selling, report the sale and transfer, and cancel the toll tag
- **Rule:** report the sale to the registry (*denuncia de venta*) and complete the transfer; keep proof of the date and the buyer; cancel any toll tag linked to the plate (see tolls-and-telepase.md).
- **Why:** until the transfer is registered the owner on record receives the fines; a sale report and the buyer data are the proof used to name the buyer (VEH-MUL-21).
- **How:** the DNRPA offers the sale report as an online procedure; keep the bill of sale, the copy of the buyer's ID and the registry receipt. Check the plate in the lookups after one month.
- **Verify:** the registry's historical report shows the new holder.
- **Don't:** hand over the car with just a signed paper and a promise to transfer "next week".
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 75 c (read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar news 2018-07-11 listing online "denuncia de venta" (read 2026-11-15); DNRPA current requirements: check ownership-and-paperwork.md.

## Scams and personal data

### VEH-MUL-41: Treat any message that asks you to pay a fine through a link as a scam until proven
- **Rule:** do not pay a fine from a link, QR code or number in an SMS, WhatsApp or email; go to the official site yourself or use the printed coupon.
- **Why:** fake fine messages are used to steal card data and bank access; Argentine media and police warned about SMS and QR scams in 2024-2026, and neighbouring countries' agencies state that they do not charge fines by message (Brazil's agency said the same about tolls).
- **How:** type the address of the official site yourself (`infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar`, the City's lookup, the national lookup) and search with the plate; the real notice has the record number and the court; call the court on the official list or the PBA line 0-800-222-0024 or the City's 147 to confirm. Warning signs: urgency ("your licence will be suspended today"), a short link, a payment to a person's account, a request for card codes, an unknown sender. If you already paid or typed card data, call your bank at once, block the card and report to the police cybercrime unit.
- **Verify:** the domain ends in `.gba.gob.ar`, `.buenosaires.gob.ar` or `.gob.ar`; the coupon opens in the official site.
- **Don't:** reply to the message or open the attachment.
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar que-hago (official channels, read 2026-11-15); Not verified in a primary source: the Argentine scam patterns (cronista.com, iprofesional.com 2024-2026, one note dated 2026-09-13 from the PBA police cybercrime unit via elancasti.com.ar); gov.br/antt notice that the agency and concessionaires do not send toll bills by WhatsApp, e-mail or SMS (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-MUL-42: Give out only the plate when you look
- **Rule:** to look for fines you only need the plate or the ID number on an official site; never give a licence photo, home address, bank data or a password to a lookup site, an app or a "gestor".
- **Why:** an unofficial lookup that asks for more is collecting data.
- **How:** use the official lookups (VEH-MUL-5); if an app asks for a login with your bank or digital ID, stop.
- **Verify:** the address bar shows an official domain and a lock; the site does not ask for payment to show results.
- **Don't:** upload your ID to a forum or a chat group.
- **Source:** infraccionesba.gba.gob.ar and consultainfracciones.seguridadvial.gob.ar (the lookups ask only for plate or ID, read 2026-11-15); the apps that gather jurisdictions: Not verified in a primary source.
