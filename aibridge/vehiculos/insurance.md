# Vehicle insurance in Argentina

For owners and drivers of private cars in Argentina, many with old cars. Insurance is a contract: the policy text, its front page (*carátula*) and its particular conditions decide what is paid, so the rules below tell you what to ask for and where to look, not what your insurer must pay. **Information, not legal advice.** Money figures (cover limits, premiums, deductibles) change every year; they are given only as dated examples with the official place to check. Where a point could not be confirmed in a primary source, the Source line starts with "Not verified in a primary source".

## Legal basis and proof

### VEH-SEG-1: Insure every vehicle with a valid mandatory third-party policy
- **Rule:** every motor vehicle, trailer, semi-trailer and motorcycle must be covered by insurance for damage to third parties (*seguro obligatorio*, Ley 24.449 art. 68). The insurer must be authorised by the insurance regulator (*Superintendencia de Seguros de la Nación*, SSN). To circulate you must carry the proof of valid insurance (art. 40 c). Circulating without it is a serious infraction (art. 77 f); the fine amounts are in fines-and-points.md.
- **Why:** if you cause injury or damage without cover you answer with your own assets, and the victim can claim against you directly. Without a policy, a claim becomes a long dispute even if you win it.
- **How:** (1) get quotes from at least three authorised insurers (VEH-SEG-5); (2) for a vehicle that is already running, ask for "RC" (civil liability) at minimum; (3) keep the policy, the proof of insurance and the insurer's claim phone number together (VEH-SEG-4, VEH-SEG-21); (4) put the renewal date in a calendar: the policy states when it expires.
- **Verify:** read the front page: policy number, endorsement number, insurer name, start and end hour of the cover, vehicle plate, engine and chassis numbers (these are the items the official page lists for the proof of insurance).
- **Don't:** assume the dealer, the bank or the previous owner left you insured; do not drive a car bought today before the cover starts (the cover starts at the hour printed on the policy, not when you pay).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts. 40, 68, 77 (text read at argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-24449-818/texto, 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar "Seguro automotor: preguntas y respuestas", read 2026-11-15.

### VEH-SEG-2: Know what the mandatory cover pays, with dated figures
- **Rule:** the mandatory policy is a civil liability policy (*responsabilidad civil*) for harm to third parties. The SSN sets its limits by resolution and updates them every year for policies issued or renewed from 1 January. Check the limit on your policy against the current resolution.
- **Why:** the minimum limits are set in pesos and inflation makes an old figure meaningless; a policy renewed in a given year follows that year's limits.
- **How:** example as of 2026-11-15, check the SSN resolution and your policy: Res. SSN 589/2025 (policies issued or renewed from 2026-01-01): art. 5, compulsory insurance, death or total permanent disability: ARS 20,800,000 per person; art. 4, medical expenses up to ARS 910,000 and funeral expenses up to ARS 533,000 per person, payable within 5 days of the claim being verified; art. 8, voluntary civil liability limit per event: ARS 208,000,000 for private cars, pickups, trailers and motorcycles (ARS 455,000,000 for taxis, trucks and others). Reported for policies issued or renewed from 2027-01-01 (Res. SSN 480/2026, secondary summary only): compulsory death or disability ARS 27,000,000 per person, medical up to ARS 1,200,000, funeral up to ARS 700,000, voluntary limit for cars ARS 270,000,000 per event. The resolution wording for the compulsory amounts is about death and permanent total disability of people: ask the insurer in writing what your "RC" policy pays for damage to another car or building and for partial injuries (the voluntary limit per event).
- **Verify:** compare the numbers printed on your policy front page with the resolution in force on the issue or renewal date (SSN site, or the Boletín Oficial, boletinoficial.gob.ar).
- **Don't:** quote last year's limits to a client, a lawyer or a buyer as if they were current; do not take "mandatory" to mean "covers everything I might cause".
- **Source:** Res. SSN 589/2025 arts. 4, 5, 8, 11 (synthesised text read at boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/333893/20251103, 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the 2027 figures (Res. SSN 480/2026 summarised by a tax-news site, siap.blogdelcontador.com.ar, read 2026-11-15; full text not read); Decreto 1716/2008 (cited by argentina.gob.ar as complementary regulation; text not read).

### VEH-SEG-3: Remember that the annual mandatory policy presupposes a technical inspection
- **Rule:** the law says the mandatory insurance is annual and that, beforehand, the vehicle must have passed the mandatory technical inspection (*revisión técnica obligatoria*, *VTV*) or be in regulatory safety condition if the inspection was not done in the previous year (Ley 24.449 art. 68).
- **Why:** an expired inspection can make a new policy hard to get, and it is the main trap when you cancel a policy on a parked car (VEH-SEG-28).
- **How:** before changing insurer or re-insuring a car that has been parked for a long time, check the date on the inspection sticker and book the inspection first (ownership-and-paperwork.md). Ask any insurer you approach whether it asks for a valid inspection certificate or for inspection photographs before issuing.
- **Verify:** the inspection certificate or sticker is in date; ask the insurer's producer in writing what it requires.
- **Don't:** let the insurance and the inspection both expire at once on an old car and expect to re-insure it the same day.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 68 (text read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: how each insurer applies this requirement in practice (insurer practice, ask the insurer).

### VEH-SEG-4: Carry the proof of insurance, on paper or in digital form
- **Rule:** you must carry the proof of valid insurance (*comprobante de seguro obligatorio*). The insurer must hand it over together with the policy; the official page lists its content: policy and endorsement number, insurer name, address and phone, start and end hour of the cover, and the vehicle data. A digital version is accepted with the same validity as the paper one.
- **Why:** the traffic law makes carrying valid proof of insurance a condition to circulate (art. 40 c). Authorities can also check the cover in databases; carrying the proof makes a roadside check short.
- **How:** (1) save the insurer's PDF or app card on the phone and keep a printed copy in the glovebox; (2) check the hours of validity on it each renewal (the new proof has a new endorsement number); (3) if you pay by instalments, ask for the new proof when each period starts if the insurer sends one.
- **Verify:** the end date and hour printed on the proof are in the future; the plate on it matches the car.
- **Don't:** carry the receipt of an old period; do not rely on a phone with a dead battery as the only copy.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 40 c (read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar Derecho Fácil "Seguro automotor" (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the digital validity rule (Disposición 13/2019 of the Subsecretaría de Gobierno Digital, per a government-site search summary and a legal-news site; not read in the Boletín Oficial). The earlier owner page also recorded that a 2009 national road-safety agency disposition (Disp. 70/2009 art. 2) says an authority cannot use the lack of the premium receipt alone as proof of non-compliance; this disposition was not found or read, so do not rely on it to skip carrying the proof.

### VEH-SEG-5: Check that the insurer and the producer are authorised, and check your cover
- **Rule:** use only an insurer authorised by the SSN, and check an insurer or a producer (*productor asesor de seguros*) before paying them. The SSN also has a tool to check whether a vehicle currently has insurance in force.
- **Why:** an unauthorised seller can take premiums and leave you with no cover; a quick check takes a minute.
- **How:** (1) open the SSN search of habilitated subjects (seguro2.ssn.gob.ar/sujetoshabilitados, linked from argentina.gob.ar/superintendencia-de-seguros/portal-ciudadano/sujetos-habilitados) and type the name of the insurer or the producer; (2) for the plate check, the SSN "Mi seguro" tool (argentina.gob.ar/aplicaciones/mi-seguro) is described as checking insurance in force from what insurers report; (3) pay only to the insurer's own account, card page or authorised collector, never to a personal account.
- **Verify:** the name appears as authorised; the premium receipt names the insurer, not the producer.
- **Don't:** accept a "policy" sent only by a chat message without the insurer's name, policy number and a check on the SSN list.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar/superintendencia-de-seguros/portal-ciudadano/sujetos-habilitados (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the exact current scope of the "Mi seguro" tool (its page returned an error when read; description from a government-site search summary, 2026-11-15).

### VEH-SEG-6: Know what the mandatory cover does not cover
- **Rule:** the compulsory policy does not cover damage caused in war, guerrilla, rebellion or terrorism; strikes or riots; driving without a licence; excessive, very flammable or explosive cargo; towing another vehicle (except occasional emergency help); and racing or speed events. It does not pay the owner's own damage (theft, fire, own repairs).
- **Why:** many people believe "I have insurance" means the car is covered; only a policy that lists theft, fire or own damage pays those.
- **How:** read the policy front page for the list of covers (VEH-SEG-13). If you tow a trailer or carry heavy loads, ask the insurer before the trip. Make sure every regular driver holds a valid licence (see ownership-and-paperwork.md).
- **Verify:** your policy lists each cover you think you have, with its limit or deductible.
- **Don't:** lend the car to someone without a valid licence; do not use the car in a race or "rally" event on the road.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar Derecho Fácil "Seguro automotor" (exclusions list, read 2026-11-15); Ley 17.418 art. 70 (cover is lost for intentional or grossly negligent acts of the policyholder or beneficiary; read 2026-11-15).

## Insurance law (Ley 17.418): the articles that matter

### VEH-SEG-7: Read the policy within one month: the difference from your proposal is accepted (art. 12)
- **Rule:** if the policy text differs from your proposal, the difference is deemed approved if you do not claim within one month of receiving the policy (Ley 17.418 art. 12). The presumption only applies when the insurer warned you about this right in a clause printed in a prominent way on the front of the policy.
- **Why:** a wrong plate, a smaller insured sum, a missing cover or a different risk address becomes your contract if you stay silent.
- **How:** on the day the policy arrives: (1) compare it line by line with the quote or proposal you accepted (cover type, insured sum, deductible, vehicle data, address, driver data); (2) send any difference in writing to the insurer and the producer, keep the proof of sending; (3) calendar day 25 as a reminder.
- **Verify:** you have a written reply or a corrected endorsement; the clause about art. 12 is printed on the front page (the earlier owner page found it printed on a real policy).
- **Don't:** file the policy unread; do not argue by phone only.
- **Source:** Ley 17.418 art. 12 (text read at argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/39520/texto, 2026-11-15).

### VEH-SEG-8: Report any claim to the insurer within three days (arts. 46, 47, 115)
- **Rule:** the policyholder must notify the insurer of the claim within three days of knowing it (Ley 17.418 art. 46); for civil liability, the insured must report the fact that may lead to liability within three days, or from the third party's claim if it was unknown (art. 115). A late report can cost the right to be indemnified unless force majeure or impossibility without fault is proved (art. 47). The official pages tell people to report within 72 hours, which is the safe practical deadline.
- **Why:** this is the rule that most often turns a covered claim into a refused one.
- **How:** (1) report the same day by the insurer's app, claim phone line or producer; (2) get a claim number in writing; (3) if the insurer's line is closed, send an email or a message and keep it; (4) if you are hurt, ask someone else to report for you.
- **Verify:** you hold a claim number and the date and hour of the report.
- **Don't:** wait until "the other driver calls"; do not report only to the police and think the insurer knows.
- **Source:** Ley 17.418 arts. 46, 47, 115 (text read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar Derecho Fácil "Seguro automotor" (72 hours, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-SEG-9: A missed premium suspends the cover: act before the due date (art. 31, SSN FAQ)
- **Rule:** the law makes the insurer not liable for a claim that happens before the first or single premium is paid (Ley 17.418 art. 31). For instalments, the SSN states that if you do not pay on time the cover is suspended automatically at hour 24 of the due date, and that after 60 days from the first due date without payment or reinstatement the insurer may cancel the contract. Read your policy general conditions for the exact clause.
- **Why:** a failed direct debit or a blocked card can leave you uncovered the next day without any warning on the road.
- **How:** (1) have funds in the account the day before each debit; (2) if a debit fails, pay by another means the same day (the insurer's page, a bank transfer, a collection point) and keep the receipt; (3) ask in writing from what hour the cover is back after paying; (4) if the insurer cancels without telling you, complain to the SSN (VEH-SEG-17).
- **Verify:** receipt in hand and a written confirmation that the cover is active; the SSN FAQ page states the suspension and the 60-day rule.
- **Don't:** drive after a failed debit "because it is only one day"; do not assume the insurer will call.
- **Source:** Ley 17.418 art. 31 (text read 2026-11-15); SSN portal ciudadano FAQ "Automotor" at argentina.gob.ar/superintendencia-de-seguros/portal-ciudadano/preguntas-frecuentes/automotor (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-SEG-10: Tell the insurer about any change that makes the risk worse (arts. 37-42; not art. 70)
- **Rule:** if something happens that makes the risk bigger than the one the insurer accepted (for example using the car for delivery or ride-hailing trips, a new young regular driver, a gas conversion, parking in a high-risk area), you must tell the insurer before it happens if it is your own act, or immediately after you learn of it if not (Ley 17.418 art. 38). If the change is yours, the cover is suspended until the insurer decides (art. 39); if you stay silent and a claim happens during the aggravation, the insurer may refuse to pay (art. 40, with two exceptions: no fault or negligence of yours, or the insurer already knew).
- **Why:** the premium was calculated for a described use; a hidden change gives the insurer a legal reason to refuse.
- **How:** write to the insurer before the change: describe it, ask whether it is accepted, at what extra premium, and get the answer in writing; for ride-hailing or delivery use, ask for a policy that covers it (Res. SSN 589/2025 art. 9 sets a uniform cover for vehicles connected to a technology platform).
- **Verify:** the endorsement lists the new use or equipment.
- **Don't:** assume the policy covers "any use"; do not mix up this rule with art. 70, which is about intentional or grossly negligent acts that cause the loss (earlier notes cited art. 70 for aggravation: the text read shows art. 70 is the wilful or grave-fault article).
- **Source:** Ley 17.418 arts. 37-42 and 70 (text read 2026-11-15); Res. SSN 589/2025 art. 9 (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-SEG-11: Claims on the contract prescribe in one year (art. 58)
- **Rule:** actions based on the insurance contract prescribe in one year, counted from when the obligation is due. The steps of the claim settlement procedure set by law or by the contract interrupt prescription (Ley 17.418 art. 58).
- **Why:** a refused or unpaid claim left alone for a year can be lost.
- **How:** keep a dated file of every message with the insurer; when the insurer refuses or stalls, write a formal letter and consult a lawyer or a consumer-protection office well before the first anniversary of the date the claim was due; ask the lawyer what interrupts the term in your case.
- **Verify:** the date of the loss, of the report, of each reply and of the refusal are all written in your file.
- **Don't:** negotiate by phone for months without a written trail; do not assume an SSN complaint stops the clock (the SSN complaint is not a court action).
- **Source:** Ley 17.418 art. 58 (text read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: whether an SSN complaint or a mediation interrupts the term (ask a lawyer).

### VEH-SEG-12: Never admit liability or settle without the insurer; the victim can sue the insurer (arts. 116, 118)
- **Rule:** the insured cannot acknowledge liability or settle with the victim without the insurer's consent (Ley 17.418 art. 116). The injured third party has a privilege over the insured sum and can cite the insurer into the lawsuit (art. 118). The insurer defends you and pays legal costs to resist the claim (art. 110 a).
- **Why:** an on-the-spot "it was my fault" or a private payment can be used by the insurer to avoid paying and leaves you exposed.
- **How:** at the scene say only facts; do not sign admissions; give the insurer any letter, mediation summons or lawsuit immediately (VEH-SEG-25).
- **Verify:** every document you receive is forwarded to the insurer with proof of delivery.
- **Don't:** pay the victim "to avoid trouble" before talking to the insurer.
- **Source:** Ley 17.418 arts. 109, 110, 116, 118 (text read 2026-11-15).

## Cover types and how to compare them

### VEH-SEG-13: Know the four common cover types
- **Rule:** the market sells, in increasing order of cost: civil liability only (RC); third party complete (*terceros completo*); third party complete with hail (*terceros completo con granizo*, sometimes called "con granizo" or "con cristales"); all risk with deductible (*todo riesgo con franquicia*). The names are commercial: what counts is the list of covers in the policy.
- **Why:** the same name can hide different lists; a cheap "complete" policy may not cover total theft of an old car at its real value.
- **How:** typical contents (confirm against each policy; names vary):

| Cover | Third parties (RC) | Total theft | Total fire | Partial theft and glass | Hail | Own damage in a crash |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RC only | yes | no | no | no | no | no |
| Terceros completo | yes | yes | yes | often limited (tyres, locks, glass in set amounts) | no | no |
| Terceros con granizo | yes | yes | yes | often | yes | no |
| Todo riesgo con franquicia | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes, minus the deductible |

   The official pages list the extra covers available: theft, total fire, hail and all risk with or without deductible.
- **Verify:** every cover you need is named in the front page with its limit, and the exclusions and deductible are readable in the particular conditions.
- **Don't:** buy on the price of the monthly instalment alone; do not assume "complete" includes own damage.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar Derecho Fácil "Seguro automotor" (other policies: theft, fire, hail, all risk with or without deductible; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the table contents are the usual market structure and differ by insurer (commercial pages of several insurers, not quoted).

### VEH-SEG-14: Compare quotes on the same six numbers
- **Rule:** to compare offers, ask each insurer for the same cover and compare: (1) cover type and what each item covers; (2) insured sum of the car and whether it is updated during the year; (3) deductible (*franquicia*) amount or percentage and when it applies; (4) civil liability limit per event; (5) the premium per period and the total of the year, with taxes, fees and financing; (6) the services (towing, replacement car, glass, locksmith) with their limits.
- **Why:** a lower premium often means a lower insured sum, a higher deductible or fewer services.
- **How:** write the six numbers for each offer in a table; for towing, the SSN FAQ says a policy offering the tow service must include clause CA-CO 15.1 (round trip of at least 100 km each way and at least six annual services, no more than one per month); ask for the policy general conditions before paying.
- **Verify:** same cover, same car, same driver data in every quote; prices dated; total year cost computed.
- **Don't:** compare the monthly price of a three-month plan with the price of a yearly plan.
- **Source:** SSN portal ciudadano FAQ "Automotor" (clause CA-CO 15.1, total-loss payout, read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the usual structure of quotes (market practice).

### VEH-SEG-15: Read the policy front page: plan, period, premium breakdown, payment
- **Rule:** at issue, read: the plan (a basic policy is civil liability with a limit; the plan label is the insurer's own; anything beyond third parties must be listed); the validity versus the billed period; the premium breakdown; the payment plan; the printed article 12 notice; and who to call.
- **Why:** this single page tells you what you bought; the later endorsements are priced separately.
- **How:** (1) validity versus billed period: a policy can run a year while the premium is billed in six-month endorsements, each with its own price (the next is usually dearer); (2) premium breakdown: net premium, VAT, stamp tax, other taxes and fees, and, when paying in instalments with a card, a financing charge with an annual effective rate printed: in the example the earlier owner page read it was about 4% of the endorsement before its VAT; paying the half-year in one go avoids it at the cost of cash now; (3) payment plan: dates, amounts, means (card or bank account); a card plan uses card limit and fails if the card is blocked or full; (4) art. 12 notice: the one-month term (VEH-SEG-7); (5) who to call: the customer line and, if a claim is not solved, the SSN (its phone and email are printed on the policy).
- **Verify:** you can explain each number on the page; the plan covers what you need.
- **Don't:** accept a payment plan without writing down the debit dates.
- **Source:** Ley 17.418 art. 12 (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the 4% financing figure, which comes from a single policy read in the earlier owner page of the sibling repository (2026-10-20), not from a rule.

### VEH-SEG-16: Understand total loss, insured value and the adjustment clause
- **Rule:** in a total loss (*pérdida total*) the insurer pays the cash market price of a vehicle of the same make, model and features at the time of the loss plus applicable taxes and fees, up to the insured sum printed on the policy. If the insured sum is lower than the car's value, an adjustment clause may reduce the payment. The insurer may require the damaged vehicle (the wreck). If the car is not in the insured's name, payment may require the car to be transferred to the insured or a notarial authorisation, but only if the front page warns of it.
- **Why:** with old cars the gap between the insured sum and the market price, and the paperwork (title in another name), are the usual sources of disputes.
- **How:** (1) put the real market price as insured sum, checking prices of similar cars; (2) keep the title (*título del automotor*) in your name or your name on the policy matching the title; (3) do not sign blank forms (the SSN FAQ says the required documents are listed in clause CG-CO 3.1); (4) after a total loss the insurer has 30 calendar days from receiving all the information it asked for to say whether it pays, and 15 calendar days to pay after the amount is agreed.
- **Verify:** the insured sum and the clauses are on the front page; the owner on the policy is the owner on the title.
- **Don't:** insure the car for a "round" low sum to save premium; do not let someone else's name stay on the title after you bought the car.
- **Source:** SSN portal ciudadano FAQ "Automotor" (total loss, adjustment clause, 30 and 15 day terms, Ley 17.418 arts. 56 and 49 cited; read 2026-11-15).

## The regulator and claims that stall

### VEH-SEG-17: Complain to the SSN when the insurer does not answer, pays late or drops the policy
- **Rule:** if the insurer does not answer, delays, applies an adjustment clause it should not, drops your policy without notice, or does not keep a payment agreement, file a complaint with the SSN. It is free and is done online through the remote-procedures platform (*Trámites a Distancia*, TAD).
- **Why:** the SSN controls the insurers and a complaint is cheap and leaves a record. The SSN cannot intervene if you are a third party with no contract with the insurer you complain about.
- **How:** (1) gather the policy and proof of your status (policyholder, insured, beneficiary or heir); (2) log in to TAD with your DNI or clave fiscal; (3) open the procedure "Reclamo" at argentina.gob.ar/servicio/realizar-un-reclamo-ante-la-superintendencia-de-seguros-de-la-nacion, fill the form and attach documents; (4) wait for an email answer. For queries by phone, the SSN lists a toll-free line 0800-666-8400 (reported from a search summary; confirm on argentina.gob.ar/superintendencia-de-seguros/consultas-y-denuncias).
- **Verify:** you got a TAD file number and an email acknowledgement.
- **Don't:** file without keeping your own records of the dates of reports and replies; do not use a complaint as a substitute for the one-year deadline of VEH-SEG-11.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Realizar un reclamo ante la Superintendencia de Seguros de la Nación" (read 2026-11-15); SSN portal ciudadano FAQ "Automotor" (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the 0800 number and its hours (consultas page returned an access error when read; number from a search summary).

## After an accident

### VEH-SEG-18: At the scene: stop, secure, call, exchange data
- **Rule:** after any traffic accident all parties must stop, give their licence and insurance data to the other party and to the authority, report the accident to the authority, and appear to give a statement if summoned. Hit-and-run and not complying with what the law requires after an accident are serious infractions (Ley 24.449 art. 77 g, h).
- **Why:** leaving the scene turns a civil claim into a possible criminal and administrative matter and can cost the cover.
- **How:** (1) switch on the hazard lights, put the warning triangles (*balizas*) as the traffic law requires, move to a safe place only if there are no injured or if told by the police; (2) call 911 if there are injured people or a risk (fire, fuel, blocked road); (3) take photos of the cars, plates, the positions, the road, the signals and the damage; (4) exchange and write: place, date and hour; full name, address, phone and email of the other driver; licence and ID number; vehicle make and plate; their insurer and policy number; witnesses' names and phones; (5) do not say "it was my fault" and do not agree to pay (VEH-SEG-12).
- **Verify:** you have the other party's insurer and policy number, photos with timestamps and at least one witness contact if there was one.
- **Don't:** drive away, move the cars when someone is injured, or accept cash on the spot.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 77 (read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar "Seguro automotor: preguntas y respuestas" (what to note about the other car, driver, witnesses; read 2026-11-15); ACA insurer FAQ on claims (data to exchange, secondary; aca.org.ar/popup/faq-seguro-siniestros, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-SEG-19: File the claim report (denuncia de siniestro) with the insurer within 72 hours
- **Rule:** report the accident to your own insurer within 72 hours (the official page) and in any case within three days (Ley 17.418 art. 46). If there are injured or dead people, theft, robbery or fire, a police report is also required.
- **Why:** the report starts the settlement procedure and protects your cover (VEH-SEG-8).
- **How:** (1) report by the insurer's app or phone; (2) give the facts: date, hour, place, vehicles, injured, police station if any, a short neutral description; (3) send photos and the third party's data; (4) ask which workshops or surveyors to use and do not order repairs on your own before the insurer approves them for covered damage (insurer FAQ); (5) keep the claim number and every message.
- **Verify:** the insurer replied with a claim number and the next step.
- **Don't:** order repairs and then ask the insurer to pay; do not hide that the driver was not the usual one.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar "Seguro automotor: preguntas y respuestas" (72 hours; read 2026-11-15); SSN portal ciudadano FAQ "Automotor" (three days; police report for theft, robbery, fire, injured or dead; read 2026-11-15); ACA insurer FAQ (no repairs before approval; secondary, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-SEG-20: Know the traffic accident record (acta de choque) and the police report
- **Rule:** the law says that, for accidents without a criminal case, the traffic authority writes an accident record (*acta de choque*) with the data it verifies and the parties' statements and gives the original and a copy to the parties; claims to the insurer are received on the basis of that record (Ley 24.449 arts. 66 a and 68). A police report is needed for theft, robbery, fire or injuries.
- **Why:** the record is evidence for the claim and the authority can require it to be filed.
- **How:** ask the officer on scene for the record or the police station's reference; for theft or robbery file the police report first and then call the insurer and the registry (see ownership-and-paperwork.md); in a collision with no police present, write your own summary the same day and send it to the insurer with the photos.
- **Verify:** you hold a copy of the record or of the police report number.
- **Don't:** sign a record that does not match what happened; do not skip the report for a theft.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts. 66 a and 68 (read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar service "Denunciar el robo o hurto del automotor" (police report first, then insurer, then registry; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: how often a record is actually issued in each province.

### VEH-SEG-21: Keep the accident checklist and the insurer's assistance number in the glovebox
- **Rule:** keep on paper, with the policy, the insurer's claim and assistance phone lines, including the legal-assistance line for accidents with injured third parties, and a short card with the data to collect (VEH-SEG-18).
- **Why:** after a crash people forget numbers and the phone may be broken or dead; a printed card saves hours.
- **How:** write on one card: insurer name, claim phone, the legal-assistance phone for injured third parties (policies often list one), producer's phone, your policy number, your emergency contact; add the checklist of VEH-SEG-18; put it with the proof of insurance and the cédula.
- **Verify:** the numbers on the card match the current policy (check at each renewal).
- **Don't:** keep the only copy of the policy number on the phone.
- **Source:** earlier owner page of the sibling repository (the receipt lists the post-accident steps and insurers add a legal-assistance line; carried over, 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar "Seguro automotor: preguntas y respuestas" (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the existence of a legal-assistance line in each policy.

### VEH-SEG-22: Injuries: medical and funeral costs are paid at once; relatives and passengers
- **Rule:** the insurer must pay immediately the sanatorium and funeral costs of third parties, without prejudice to later claims, and the creditor for those services may take the place of the victim (Ley 24.449 art. 68). The renouncing of a later claim made when receiving that payment is not valid. Passengers in the car and any affected third party can claim against the insurer, except close relatives (spouse, child, parent, sibling) under the official page.
- **Why:** an injured person should not wait for a settlement to get care; and the amounts are fixed (VEH-SEG-2).
- **How:** (1) call 911 and ask for an ambulance; (2) report to the insurer immediately and ask to be put in touch with its legal-assistance line; (3) give the injured person the insurer's data; (4) do not discuss fault in front of the victim; (5) inform the insurer of any criminal summons (the insurer may take the defence under art. 110 b).
- **Verify:** the insurer confirmed in writing that the medical-expense payment process has started.
- **Don't:** negotiate private money for silence; do not sign a waiver at the hospital.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 68 (read 2026-11-15); Ley 17.418 art. 110 (read 2026-11-15); Res. SSN 589/2025 art. 4 (5-day payment term; read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar "Seguro automotor: preguntas y respuestas" (who can claim; read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-SEG-23: Use CLEAS when you are not at fault and both insurers belong to it
- **Rule:** the claims clearing system between insurers (*CLEAS*, "clearing asegurador", developed by CESVI Argentina) exists: when two cars of two different member insurers collide, the driver who is not responsible can be attended directly by his or her own insurer, which then settles with the other insurer. It applies only to collisions between exactly two vehicles insured in two different member companies, with property damage only (no injured, no deaths), under a maximum amount set by the system, and with some excluded vehicles (motorcycles, farm machinery, vehicles over 9 tonnes, motorhomes, trailers, total destruction).
- **Why:** it can shorten the repair of a car that was hit; it is not obligatory and not an all-risk cover.
- **How:** (1) ask your insurer whether it belongs to CLEAS and whether your case qualifies; (2) report the accident to your own insurer and provide the other driver's data; (3) the insurers decide liability from the information and the traffic law, so give photos and witnesses; (4) the system rules are in its published regulation (the insurer FAQ links to it).
- **Verify:** the insurer confirms CLEAS handling and tells you the next step in writing.
- **Don't:** assume CLEAS exists for your insurer; the member list changes.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: CLEAS scope, members and limits are taken from an insurer's fact sheet (Zurich Argentina, 2 pages, saved locally; it lists 11 members and the exclusions) and a comparison-site summary, read 2026-11-15; the rule text (reglamento) was not read. Ask your insurer and check cleas.com.ar.

### VEH-SEG-24: If you are the victim and the other driver has no insurance or another insurer
- **Rule:** the injured third party's claim is personal: the victim's own insurer does not file it for him or her, and the SSN cannot intervene if you are a third party without a contract with the at-fault insurer. You can sue the person responsible and include the insurer in the lawsuit.
- **Why:** many drivers do not know whom to claim against.
- **How:** (1) write down the other driver's insurer and policy number and ask for the proof of insurance; (2) if the other car is uninsured, note the plate and ask the police to record it; (3) report the accident to your own insurer in any case and ask whether your cover (own damage, CLEAS) can act; (4) for the claim against the other party consult a lawyer or a free legal-aid service within the one-year and any other term that applies to your claim.
- **Verify:** you have the other party's identification, the police or traffic record and photos.
- **Don't:** accept a verbal promise to pay "next week".
- **Source:** SSN portal ciudadano FAQ "Automotor" (injured third party; read 2026-11-15); Ley 17.418 art. 118 (victim may cite the insurer; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the prescription term for the victim's civil claim against the driver (ask a lawyer).

### VEH-SEG-25: If you get a claim letter, mediation summons or lawsuit, send it to the insurer at once
- **Rule:** if you are the insured or driver and you receive a registered letter (*carta documento*), a mediation summons, a lawsuit or any document linked to the accident, notify your insurer. The insurer decides whether to defend you; if it does not answer within two business days of receiving the documents, it is deemed to accept the defence and must appoint a lawyer for you and the driver.
- **Why:** deadlines in a lawsuit are short; if the insurer is not told it is not bound to defend.
- **How:** scan the document the same day, send it to the claim channel and by email to the producer, keep proof of sending, note the deadline printed on the summons; ask the insurer in writing who the appointed lawyer is.
- **Verify:** written reply naming the lawyer, within the two business days.
- **Don't:** answer the summons yourself or ignore it because "the insurer knows".
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar Derecho Fácil "Seguro automotor" (two business days; read 2026-11-15); Ley 17.418 arts. 115 and 116 (read 2026-11-15).

## Driving abroad

### VEH-SEG-26: Before crossing a border get the Mercosur civil liability certificate (*carta verde*)
- **Rule:** to prove your policy outside Argentina you need the Mercosur green card (*Tarjeta Verde del Mercosur*, also called "carta verde" or Mercosur/Cono Sur certificate), which the insurer issues with the policy. Insurers extend civil liability to the neighbouring countries of the agreement (Uruguay, Paraguay and Brazil are reported as covered automatically at no extra cost); confirm in writing which countries are covered, for what limits and for how long.
- **Why:** abroad you cannot show an Argentine mandatory-insurance receipt as it is; border officers ask for the green card, and an uncovered crash is expensive.
- **How:** (1) ask the insurer or producer for the certificate before the trip; it should be bilingual (Spanish and Portuguese) with the insurer's signature and seal, valid date, city of issue, your name and address, chassis and engine numbers, plate and policy number; (2) check each data item against the vehicle's registration card (*cédula verde*); (3) if you travel to Chile or Bolivia, ask the insurer specifically: it is not confirmed that the same certificate is accepted there (Chile is reported to require its own foreign-vehicle insurance); (4) carry also the car's documents and the authorisation required if the driver is not the owner (see trip-preparation.md); (5) keep the insurer's international assistance line with the certificate.
- **Verify:** the certificate shows the right plate and dates and names the countries; the insurer confirms the countries in writing.
- **Don't:** leave the certificate for the border; do not assume a paper "carta verde" bought for a Brazilian-registered car works for an Argentine one (those are different products).
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: country coverage and requirements for Chile and Bolivia (comparison-site pages comparaencasa.com and a Mercosur press page, read 2026-11-15; the Mercosur working-group minutes of 2019 show the green-card regime was still under study at regional level; no SSN or Mercosur text was read). Res. SSN 39.927/2016 is reported to update civil liability cover in the Mercosur (secondary: law-firm note), not read.

## Old cars

### VEH-SEG-27: Ask each insurer for its maximum vehicle age and what it offers for a 30-year-old car
- **Rule:** there is no national age limit for third-party civil liability cover, but each insurer sets its own conditions for older cars; own-damage and theft covers for very old cars are often refused or limited to RC-type products. A car of model year 1994 is over 30 years old: expect to be offered civil liability, and ask whether "terceros completo" (theft, fire) is available with an insured sum that reflects the car's real value.
- **Why:** for a very old car the market value is low and the insurer may refuse own-damage cover or pay at that low value.
- **How:** (1) get quotes from at least three insurers and from one specialised in classic or old cars; (2) state the model year, the mileage and any modification (gas equipment, engine swap) honestly (VEH-SEG-10); (3) ask in writing: maximum age accepted, insured sum, deductible, whether a valid technical inspection (VTV) is required at issue, whether photos are required; (4) compare the cost of the cover with the car's market value (a premium for own damage that approaches the car's value is not worth it); (5) check the classic-vehicle route in ownership-and-paperwork.md if the car is registered as a classic.
- **Verify:** written quote with the model year printed correctly.
- **Don't:** declare a newer model year to get a quote; do not accept an insured sum the insurer picks automatically without checking market prices.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: insurers' age limits (comparison-site page comparaencasa.com "seguros de autos de más de 20 años" and similar, read 2026-11-15, commercial and not quoted figures; each insurer sets its own). Ley 24.449 art. 68 (cover required for every motor vehicle; read 2026-11-15).

## A car that is parked, a different address, payment failure and cancelling

### VEH-SEG-28: Before cancelling a policy on a car you do not use, compare costs and ask for suspension in writing
- **Rule:** an idle car still costs money (insurance, tax, parking, maintenance). Before cancelling anything, compare N months of premium with the one-off cost of making the car insurable again (an expired technical inspection can block a new policy: the cancel-and-lose-cover trap), and ask the insurer in writing three things: can the policy be suspended; is there a cheaper parked-vehicle cover (theft and fire); can third-party cover be reactivated without a new inspection.
- **Why:** the owner answers for damage the car causes even while parked (a storm, a roll, a falling tree); cancelling is a risk decision, not only a saving.
- **How:** (1) write the premium per month and multiply by the months you will not use the car; (2) check the inspection price and rules in your jurisdiction (ownership-and-paperwork.md; going to the plant with an expired inspection can itself be an infraction); (3) send the three questions to the insurer and the producer and save the answers; (4) if the car will never be used again, selling it ends every recurring cost: check pending fines on the plate first and use the denuncia de venta (ownership-and-paperwork.md); (5) see the Worked example below.
- **Verify:** written answers on suspension, parked cover and reactivation without inspection; the total cost of keeping, suspending and selling is on one page.
- **Don't:** cancel the policy by phone and leave the car unprotected with an expired inspection; do not assume a parked car "cannot cause damage".
- **Source:** Cash-crunch playbook s.13 "A vehicle you do not use" (budgetai docs, read 2026-11-15) and the earlier owner page of the sibling repository (carried over). Not verified in a primary source: whether insurers in Argentina allow suspension and reactivation without a new inspection; no rule was found, ask the insurer. Ley 24.449 art. 68 says every motor vehicle must be covered and does not condition that on circulation; whether a car that never circulates must be insured was not confirmed (ask the automotive registry whether a de-registration exists for your case).

### VEH-SEG-29: Know that a car left in a garage can have its own cheaper cover
- **Rule:** some insurers sell covers for vehicles that do not circulate and stay in a garage or workshop, which keep theft, fire and similar covers; third-party cover is required again when the car circulates.
- **Why:** a car that is not used can still be stolen or burn; the owner answers for what it causes.
- **How:** ask the producer for the "parked vehicle" or "stored vehicle" cover and its conditions: how the car must be kept (closed garage, no use), whether you must tell the insurer before taking it out, how fast RC is reactivated, whether an inspection is needed; get all of it in writing.
- **Verify:** the policy lists the "no circulation" condition and the reactivation procedure.
- **Don't:** take a "garage" cover car out on the road without reactivating the RC first.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: the existence and terms of parked-vehicle covers (a national newspaper note and the earlier owner page, secondary; no insurer's conditions read).

### VEH-SEG-30: Keep the risk address and the registered address straight
- **Rule:** the insurer's risk address (where the vehicle is kept, garage or street) and the registered address of the vehicle at the registry (*domicilio del titular* at the *registro seccional*) can differ from where the owner really lives. The premium often depends on the first; official notices (fines, inspection reminders) go to the second.
- **Why:** a fine notice sent to an old address can pass unseen and the defence deadline can expire; a wrong risk address can lead to a refused claim for a wrong declaration.
- **How:** (1) tell the insurer the real place where the car is parked and update it when it changes; (2) tell the registry the new owner address (ownership-and-paperwork.md); (3) check the plate in every jurisdiction every couple of months (fines-and-points.md); (4) if the car's address moves between provinces or the City, ask the insurer how the premium changes.
- **Verify:** the policy front page shows the garage address you use; the registry record shows your current address.
- **Don't:** declare a safer area to get a cheaper quote.
- **Source:** earlier owner page of the sibling repository (section "Keep the addresses straight", carried over, 2026-11-15); Ley 17.418 arts. 37-42 (risk change and aggravation; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: how each insurer prices by risk address.

### VEH-SEG-31: Make the direct debit or card fail-safe
- **Rule:** many policies are paid by direct debit or by card. A failed debit can lapse the cover (VEH-SEG-9): keep funds in the account on the debit date and the card unblocked.
- **Why:** a card plan uses card limit and fails if the card is blocked, expired or full; a replaced card number is not updated automatically by every insurer.
- **How:** (1) list each debit date and amount in a calendar a few days before; (2) when a card is replaced or expires, update it with the insurer the same week; (3) when the budget is tight, ask the producer to move the debit date to just after your income arrives or to change the plan (monthly, quarterly or half-year), asking what each costs; (4) ask for an email or SMS alert on rejected payments.
- **Verify:** each month the bank statement shows the debit and the insurer shows the instalment as paid.
- **Don't:** let two instalments fail in a row hoping to "catch up": the SSN FAQ says that after 60 days from the first due date without payment the insurer may cancel.
- **Source:** SSN portal ciudadano FAQ "Automotor" (suspension at hour 24 of the due date, 60 days; read 2026-11-15); earlier owner page of the sibling repository (direct debit, carried over).

### VEH-SEG-32: Cancel a policy in writing, with the online cancel button (botón de baja) if you contracted online
- **Rule:** insurers that sell by electronic means must show a cancel button (*botón de baja*) prominently on their website home page and in their apps for electronically contracted policies of car, motorcycle and other listed branches (Res. SSN 435/2025, in force since 2025-08-21). Cancelling takes effect from the date it is communicated, going forward, not retroactively (the original 2021 rule, Res. SSN 685/2021, published 2021-09-17). The consumer-protection law (Ley 24.240) also gives the right to cancel a service by the same means used to contract it.
- **Why:** you can stop a policy you no longer need without waiting for a call, but only from the date you cancel: the cover you enjoyed is not refunded.
- **How:** (1) before cancelling, make sure the replacement cover starts the same day; (2) press the cancel button or send a registered letter or email asking for cancellation, saying the date from which it should apply; (3) keep the cancellation receipt, which the insurer must deliver or make available; (4) ask in writing how the unused premium is treated; (5) if you sell the car, cancel only after handing it over and after the sale is reported to the registry (ownership-and-paperwork.md), and tell the buyer to insure in their own name from the handover.
- **Verify:** the cancellation receipt names the policy, the date and the hour.
- **Don't:** stop paying as a way of cancelling (VEH-SEG-9); do not cancel before the new policy exists.
- **Source:** Boletín Oficial 2021-09-17 (Res. SSN 685/2021, text read at boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/249704/20210917, 2026-11-15); Res. SSN 435/2025 (law-firm summary, Marval, 2025-08-25, read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the exact text of Res. SSN 435/2025 and 158/2025; the Ley 24.240 article that gives the right to cancel by the same means (art. 10 ter, not read); how unused premium is refunded (ask the insurer); the effect of a sale on the policy (insurer practice).
