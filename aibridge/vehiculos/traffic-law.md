# Traffic law of Argentina (national, PBA, CABA)

This file is for people who drive in Argentina, many with old cars (a 1994 Gol is the reference case in the other files). It says what the law
requires, with article numbers, so the assistant can cite one rule at a time. The national law is Ley 24.449 (the *Ley de Tránsito*); its
regulation is Decreto 779/95 (Anexo 1, "the regulation" below). Each province and the City of Buenos Aires decide whether to adhere to it
and may add or change things, so **the rule that applies to you is the one of the place where you drive**. Fine amounts, points and how to
dispute a ticket are in [fines-and-points.md](fines-and-points.md); the meaning of signs, markings and lights is in [road-signs.md](road-signs.md).

**Information, not legal advice.** The rules changed a lot in 2025-2026 (see VEH-LEY-3) and some changes are being challenged in court or
are not adopted by every province. Where the text could not be confirmed in a primary source, the Source line says so. A police
officer's order is always obeyed on the spot (VEH-LEY-2); you challenge it afterwards through the proper procedure, never at the roadside.
For a crash with injured people, call 911 first and a lawyer second.

## Legal framework and jurisdictions

### VEH-LEY-1: Find out which law applies on the road you are driving on
- **Rule:** Ley 24.449 applies directly on national roads and other places of national jurisdiction (art 1: "ámbito de aplicación la jurisdicción federal"); provinces and municipalities may adhere (art 1) and may set different rules only by exception, for well-founded local reasons, never with softer sanctions, and the local rule must be "clearly stated at the place where it applies" to be valid (art 2). The province of Buenos Aires adheres to Ley 24.449 and Ley 26.363 "as long as they do not oppose" its own Ley 13.927 (art 1). The City of Buenos Aires (CABA) has its own code, Ley 2148 (*Código de Tránsito y Transporte*), which follows the national model but differs in several figures (VEH-LEY-4).
- **Why:** the same behaviour can be legal in one place and a ticket in the next (alcohol limit, child seat age, vest, right of way (*prioridad de paso*) at avenues, vehicle inspection deadlines).
- **How:** (1) national road (route with "RN" shield, motorway (*autopista*) run by Vialidad Nacional or a concession) = Ley 24.449 and the regulation; (2) provincial road or town street in PBA = Ley 13.927, which sends you back to Ley 24.449 for anything it does not change; (3) streets and motorways inside CABA = Ley 2148; (4) any other province = its own adhesion law, which you must read (not covered here). Before a long trip check the provinces you cross (see trip-preparation.md).
- **Verify:** ask the provincial or municipal traffic authority which law is in force; read the adhesion law in the official provincial legal database. For PBA: normas.gba.gob.ar, Ley 13927. For CABA: Boletín Oficial de la Ciudad, Ley 2148 consolidated.
- **Don't:** assume that a rule you learned in another province (or in the press, which often reports only the national rule) applies where you are.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 1-2, consolidated text at biblioteca.afip.gob.ar/dcp/LEY_C_024449_1994_12_23 (read 2026-11-15; art 2 shown there in the version restored by Decreto 627/2025, see VEH-LEY-3); Ley 13.927 PBA art 1, https://normas.gba.gob.ar/documentos/0YqDnfd0.html (read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA, https://juristeca.jusbaires.gob.ar/compilacion-normativa-juristeca/ley-2148/ (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-2: Obey the officer first, then the signs, then the written law
- **Rule:** on the road you must follow, in this order of priority, the indications of the authority (*autoridad de comprobación o aplicación*), the traffic signals, and the legal rules (art 36). Every licence (*licencia de conducir*) holder must comply with the controls and orders of the traffic authority (art 13 g).
- **Why:** an officer directing traffic may order something the signs forbid (for instance letting you pass a red light at a broken signal); following the order is correct even if it contradicts the sign.
- **How:** stop where the officer indicates, switch off the engine if asked, keep hands visible, and say politely that you will present your objection in writing later. Ask the officer to identify himself and his unit (art 70 a.3) and keep your copy of the infraction record (*acta de infracción*) (VEH-LEY-46).
- **Verify:** the infraction record (*acta*) must name the officer's unit and give you a copy (art 70 a.3-4).
- **Don't:** argue about the legality of the order at the roadside, drive off, or refuse to show documents: refusing or fleeing is an aggravating fact (VEH-LEY-52).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 13 g, 36, 70 a, consolidated text biblioteca.afip.gob.ar/dcp/LEY_C_024449_1994_12_23 (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-3: Know what changed in the national regulation in 2025-2026 and what is still contested
- **Rule:** the regulation of the traffic law was largely rewritten by Decreto 196/2025 (Boletín Oficial 2025-03-18). The main changes for drivers: the Licencia Nacional de Conducir is issued in digital format and the physical card is optional (reg art 13); documents may be shown in digital or physical form (reg art 37); the regulation lists the driving requirements as licence (*licencia de conducir*), valid compulsory insurance (*seguro obligatorio*) and vehicle identification card (*cédula de identificación del automotor*) (reg art 40); the technical inspection (*RTO/VTV*) is valid 24 months for private vehicles up to 10 years old since first registration and 12 months for older ones, with 60 months before the first inspection of a private zero-km car, and jurisdictions may set longer terms (reg art 34); the certificate of homologation of spare parts (CHAS) is removed; a new Anexo L (road signs) replaces the old one (decree art 24). Decreto 139/2026 (BO 2026-03-09) rejected an administrative claim by inspection-workshop chambers against it. Decreto 796/2026 (BO 2026-08-26) only rewrote reg art 28 (type approval of new vehicles). In 2025 Decreto 461/2025 also changed Ley 24.449 arts 2 and 96, but Decreto 627/2025 restored the previous provisions.
- **Why:** many articles, blogs and even police still quote the old text (for example a fixed list of documents including the blue card, or annual inspections for all cars).
- **How:** when someone quotes a requirement, ask for the article and check whether it is the pre-2025 text. For your own case: (1) licence and vehicle card in Mi Argentina are enough for the national rule; (2) PBA keeps its own inspection system and did not adhere to the new national terms (VEH-LEY-15); (3) the decree itself invites provinces and CABA to adapt (decree art 29), so local rules may still say otherwise (VEH-LEY-12 shows the CABA list).
- **Verify:** consolidated text of Ley 24.449 and of Anexo 1 at argentina.gob.ar/normativa (Decreto 779/1995, "texto actualizado"); the BO notes of each decree; for court challenges, search the Boletín Oficial and the ANSV news page for the current status (a press report mentioned a court order suspending part of the reform; not confirmed in a primary source).
- **Don't:** rely on the date of an article: a 2024 text may describe a rule that no longer exists, and a 2026 press note may describe a rule that is not in force in your province.
- **Source:** Decreto 196/2025, https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-196-2025-410682/texto and official PDF boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/322652/20250318 (read 2026-11-15); Decreto 139/2026, boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/339199/20260309 (read 2026-11-15); Decreto 796/2026, boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/346383/20260826 (read 2026-11-15, art 1 replaces reg art 28 only); Ley 24.449 library note on Decretos 461/2025 and 627/2025, biblioteca.afip.gob.ar/dcp/LEY_C_024449_1994_12_23 (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the court suspension of the reform (secondary press, 2026).

### VEH-LEY-4: PBA and CABA differences at a glance
- **Rule:** use the local figure when you are in that jurisdiction. Summary as of 2026-11-15:

| Topic | National (Ley 24.449 and regulation) | PBA (Ley 13.927) | CABA (Ley 2148) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alcohol, private car | above zero (art 48 a, Ley 27.714) | above zero, any motor vehicle (art 28 ter, Ley 15.402) | 0.5 g/l; motorcycles 0.2 g/l; professional, school transport and novice 0.0 (art 5.4.4) |
| Child in a car | under 10 in the rear seat; restraint system per regulation (art 40 g, reg 77 u) | follows national | under 12 not in the front seat (5.2.4 c); under 12 and under 1.50 m in a homologated child seat (6.1.3 b) |
| Speed, city street / avenue | 40 / 60 km/h (art 51 a) | follows national | 40 / 60 km/h, some avenues 70, calles de convivencia 20 (6.2.2) |
| Low beams day and night | on national routes (art 47 a) | no separate rule found | Gral. Paz, Cantilo, Lugones and all motorways (6.3.1 a) |
| Reflective vest in the car | not required in the text; announced as future measure (reg art 29) | for motorcycle driver and passenger (art 48) | in the car, in reach (6.1.2 h) |
| Documents listed | licence, vehicle card, insurance, inspection proof (see VEH-LEY-12) | national list | adds ID document (DNI) and VTV certificate (5.2.2) |
| Right of way at unsigned crossings | right-hand priority, exceptions in art 41 | avenues and roads of higher rank have priority (art 28 quater, Ley 15.657, see VEH-LEY-34) | the higher-ranked street wins (avenue, then street, then passage); equal rank: the right (art 6.7.2) |
| Vehicle inspection | reg art 34: 24 or 12 months | annual from 2 years old (press, see VEH-LEY-15) | certificate required; term to check (VEH-LEY-15) |

- **Why:** most disputes about "what the law says" are really about which jurisdiction.
- **How:** keep this table in mind for a trip through the City: low beams on Gral. Paz, vest in the glovebox, child seat until 12 years or 1.50 m.
- **Verify:** each cell cites an article; open it in the official text before challenging a ticket.
- **Don't:** quote the national alcohol rule in CABA (the City keeps 0.5 g/l for private cars) or the CABA vest rule in PBA.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 40, 47, 48, 51 (biblioteca.afip.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Ley 13.927 PBA arts 28 ter, 28 quater, 48 (normas.gba.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA arts 5.2.2, 5.2.4, 5.4.4, 6.1.2, 6.1.3, 6.2.2, 6.3.1, 6.7.2 (juristeca.jusbaires.gob.ar h-tit-5 and h-tit-6, consolidated to 2024-02-29 plus later notes, read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: PBA low-beam rule (none found in the text read).

## Who may drive

### VEH-LEY-5: Use the right licence class (car = class B.1)
- **Rule:** the licence (*licencia de conducir*) classes are A (motorcycles, mopeds, motorised tricycles), B (cars and vans), C (trucks), D (passenger transport, emergency), E (articulated trucks, special machinery), F (adapted vehicles), G (agricultural tractors) (art 16). The regulation divides them: B.1 = cars, utility vehicles, vans, motorhomes up to 3,500 kg total weight, and includes A.3; B.2 = the same up to 3,500 kg towing a trailer up to 750 kg or a non-motorised caravan, and requires one year of seniority in B.1 (Decreto 26/2019). Motorcycle subclasses (A.1 mopeds from 16, A.2 up to 150 cc, A.3 150-300 cc, A.4 300-600 cc, A.5 above 600 cc, A.6 commercial) require two years in the previous subclass unless the driver is of legal age (Decreto 32/2018).
- **Why:** driving a vehicle of a class you do not hold is "driving without being duly licensed", a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 d), and the vehicle can be held (art 72 c.2, VEH-LEY-48).
- **How:** a private car or a Gol: B.1. A car with a loaded trailer over 750 kg or a larger caravan: not covered by B.2 (needs class C/E rules: ask the licensing office). Check the classes printed on your licence (or in Mi Argentina) before renting or lending.
- **Verify:** Mi Argentina > licence shows classes; the licensing office of your municipality can confirm the exact subclass.
- **Don't:** assume that "B" covers a minibus or a pick-up above 3,500 kg total weight (gross vehicle weight, check the vehicle card (*cédula de identificación del automotor*)).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 16 and art 77 d; Decreto 26/2019 art 2 (B.1/B.2), https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-318584/texto; Decreto 32/2018 art 4 (A.1-A.6 text), https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-32-2018-305742/texto (all read 2026-11-15). Decreto 196/2025 replaced reg art 13-14 but not art 16.

### VEH-LEY-6: Respect the minimum driving ages
- **Rule:** minimum ages are 21 for classes C, D, E; 17 for the other classes (cars and motorcycles, with the parents' authorisation if under 18); 16 for mopeds without passenger (art 11, art 17). Under the decree, people up to 21 may only obtain classes A and B (reg art 13 c.1). Local authorities may set different ages for justified local reasons, valid only for their jurisdiction (art 11 last paragraph). A motorcycle above 150 cc needs two years of previous licence (*licencia de conducir*) unless you are over 21 (art 16 A, reg art 16).
- **Why:** a person below the age limit cannot validly hold the licence, and letting an underage or unlicensed person drive is itself forbidden (art 48 b).
- **How:** at 17 you can obtain B.1 with a parent's authorisation; some provinces set 18 (not verified province by province: ask your licensing office).
- **Verify:** argentina.gob.ar "Ley simple: Licencia Nacional de Conducir" (ages section) and your municipality.
- **Don't:** let a 16-year-old drive a car "on a quiet road": there is no learner permit that allows it (VEH-LEY-7).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 11, 16, 17; Decreto 196/2025 art 1 (reg 13 c.1); https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/159591 (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: which provinces require 18 for class B.

### VEH-LEY-7: Getting a first licence: requirements, and there is no learner permit
- **Rule:** the issuing authority asks for: reading ability (and writing for professional classes), a sworn declaration of medical conditions, a compulsory theory-practice road-safety course at an authorised school, a medical psycho-physical examination (physical, visual, hearing, psychological), a theory exam (education and civic ethics, driving, signs, law), a practical exam on faults of safety elements and a driving test (art 14 a). If you fail an exam you wait 30 days (ANSV page). Driving schools must have a licensed instructor, vehicles of the class taught, insurance for teaching damage, and cannot accept a student younger than the minimum age by more than six months (art 12). The licence (*licencia de conducir*) is applied for at the issuing centre of your domicile (the address in your DNI).
- **Why:** Argentine law has no national "learner's permit" that lets you practice on the public road with a relative; practising with an unlicensed driver at the wheel is driving without a licence (art 77 d).
- **How:** book the course and exams through your municipality's licensing centre (in CABA: the City's online licence service; in PBA the municipality, art 8 Ley 13.927). Practise in a driving-school car with an instructor or on private land.
- **Verify:** argentina.gob.ar "Ley simple: Licencia Nacional de Conducir" (requirements and ages), plus the municipal site.
- **Don't:** teach a minor on public roads; the unlicensed driver and the person who allowed it are both exposed (art 48 b, reg 48 b.1).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 12, 14, 77 d; Decreto 196/2025 art 2 (reg 14); https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/159591 (read 2026-11-15); Ley 13.927 PBA art 8. Not verified in a primary source: whether any province allows supervised practice on public roads.

### VEH-LEY-8: First-time drivers: the beginner sign and the six-month limits
- **Rule:** during the first six months a new driver must show the beginner sign (*cartel de principiante*) visible in front and at the back of the vehicle, and may not drive in "central zones", motorways (*autopista*) or semi-motorways; the restriction must be written on the back of the sign (art 13 d; reg art 13 d). If the driver commits grave faults within the first two years the licence (*licencia de conducir*) is suspended and the theory-practice exam must be taken again (reg art 13 d).
- **Why:** protect new drivers and others; "zona céntrica" is defined by each local authority (reg art 48 e).
- **How:** get the sign at the licensing office or a road-safety shop, stick it on the windscreen and rear window, and plan routes that avoid motorways for six months.
- **Verify:** date of issue of the licence in Mi Argentina; the date of the first issue, not of a renewal, counts.
- **Don't:** drive on a motorway "just this once" during the six months; in CABA the novice also has a 0.0 g/l alcohol limit (Ley 2148 art 5.4.4).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 13 d; Decreto 196/2025 art 1 (reg art 13 d); https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/159591 (read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA art 5.4.4 (juristeca h-tit-5, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-9: Keep the licence valid: renewal terms and medical certificate
- **Rule:** the licence (*licencia de conducir*) is granted for the maximum the law sets (up to 5 years by art 13 c; the decree grants the maximum to people up to 65), renewed with a psycho-physical certificate from a doctor or health centre registered with the ANSV (reg art 13 c). After 65 the certificate is needed every 3 years for classes A, B, G and every 2 years for C, D, E; after 70 every year (reg art 13 e). The physical card has no expiry date and is valid while the digital licence is valid (reg art 13 b). A licence expires 90 days after an unreported change of data (reg art 18) and an expired licence or one with age-limit problems can be retained (art 72 b).
- **Why:** driving with an expired licence is driving without a valid licence (art 77 d); the licence is retained (art 72 b.1).
- **How:** Mi Argentina sends alerts a month before; renew online or at the licensing office with the certificate. Report a change of address within the 90 days to avoid loss of validity (reg art 18).
- **Verify:** licence status in Mi Argentina (it shows "vigente", "retenida" or "inhabilitada").
- **Don't:** assume the old card "never expires" if you are over 65: the medical term still counts, shown in the digital licence.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 13 c, 72 b, 77 d; Decreto 196/2025 art 1 (reg art 13) and art 4 (reg art 18); argentina.gob.ar/node/159591 and Mi Argentina FAQ https://www.argentina.gob.ar/miargentina/servicios/licencia/preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15). Renewal fees and local procedures are not covered: see ownership-and-paperwork.md.

### VEH-LEY-10: Use the digital licence (Mi Argentina) correctly
- **Rule:** since Decreto 196/2025 the national licence (*licencia de conducir*) is issued in digital form and the physical card is a valid optional copy; both have the same legal validity (reg art 13 b). The digital licence "is valid to drive in all the national territory without presenting the physical one". Officers check it with the app ValidAR through a QR code that refreshes every 24 hours (Mi Argentina FAQ).
- **Why:** you can be stopped without the card in your wallet; but a phone with no battery or no recent connection can leave you without a valid document.
- **How:** (1) install Mi Argentina, create the account, validate identity, subscribe to the licence service; (2) open the licence once every 24 h with a connection so the QR stays valid (the QR is valid for 24 hours offline); (3) keep the phone charged and also keep the physical card if you have it; (4) a screenshot is not a substitute (the QR expires); (5) the app shows "retenida" or "inhabilitada" if the authority retained or disqualified you.
- **Verify:** the app shows your licence only if the issuing jurisdiction is adhered to the Licencia Nacional de Conducir and you subscribed to the service (FAQ answer 9); if it does not appear, ask your licensing office. A November 2025 press report said some jurisdictions (including Buenos Aires province and Formosa) were not yet issuing it digitally; not verified in a primary source, check your own licence.
- **Don't:** hand your phone to the officer: show the screen and let him scan; and don't rely only on the digital copy abroad (not covered).
- **Source:** Decreto 196/2025 art 1 (reg art 13 b) and art 18 (reg art 72, digital suspension mechanism); Mi Argentina FAQ https://www.argentina.gob.ar/miargentina/servicios/licencia/preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15); Ley 13.927 PBA art 8 (digital licence allowed). Not verified in a primary source: adhesion status of each jurisdiction.

### VEH-LEY-11: Never let an unlicensed person drive your car
- **Rule:** it is forbidden to give or allow the driving to people without the licence (*licencia de conducir*) (art 48 b). The prohibition includes the owner's employees and relatives, and the owner cannot claim not to have known; allowing it is also when the owner or the authority knows and does not prevent it (reg art 48 b.1-b.2, text from Decreto 32/2018).
- **Why:** the owner shares the fault and the vehicle can be held at the roadside (art 72 c.2); insurance may also refuse cover for an unlicensed driver (see insurance.md).
- **How:** check the licence class and expiry of whoever borrows the car; with a digital licence ask to see the app.
- **Verify:** Mi Argentina licence status of the borrower (ask to see the screen).
- **Don't:** lend the keys to "a friend who drives well" without a licence, or to a minor.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 48 b, 72 c.2; regulation art 48 b.1-b.2 as in Decreto 32/2018, https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-32-2018-305742/texto (read 2026-11-15; Decreto 196/2025 did not replace reg art 48).

## Documents and paperwork to carry

### VEH-LEY-12: Carry licence, vehicle card, insurance proof and inspection proof
- **Rule:** to drive a car you must have: the licence (*licencia de conducir*) for that vehicle type (art 40 a), the vehicle identification card (*cédula*, art 40 b), the proof of valid compulsory insurance (*seguro obligatorio*) (art 40 c, may be shown on paper or digitally) and, under the regulation, proof of the technical inspection (*revisión técnica obligatoria, RTO/VTV*) (RTO/VTV) (reg art 37 as replaced by Decreto 196/2025: "the only documents that can be demanded are: licence, vehicle identification and inspection proof; they may be shown digitally or on paper"); reg art 40 (also replaced in 2025) lists licence, compulsory insurance and vehicle card as the circulation requirements. CABA's code also asks for the ID document and the inspection certificate (Ley 2148 art 5.2.2: DNI, licence, vehicle card, insurance, VTV certificate; art 6.1.2 repeats the list and adds the reflective vest (*chaleco reflectante*)) and says a stolen or lost vehicle card can be replaced by the police report plus the title for 15 days (art 6.1.2 b).
- **Why:** missing documentation is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 e, f, x, y); lack of the items of reg art 40 stops you from continuing to drive until fixed (reg art 40 first paragraph).
- **How:** one folder in the phone (Mi Argentina licence and vehicle card, insurer app or email with the policy proof and the digital inspection sticker (*oblea / identificación de la revisión*) or certificate) and a printed copy in the glovebox; DNI in the wallet, especially in CABA.
- **Verify:** open each document once a month; check the dates on the insurance proof and the inspection.
- **Don't:** drive "because the policy is paid" without the proof in hand; the law asks for the proof.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 40 a-c and 77 e, f, x, y (biblioteca.afip.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Decreto 196/2025 art 14 (reg 37) and art 16 (reg 40) (argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-196-2025-410682/texto, read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA arts 5.2.2 and 6.1.2 (juristeca h-tit-5 and h-tit-6, read 2026-11-15). The two regulation articles list slightly different items; carrying all of them covers both.

### VEH-LEY-13: Digital vehicle card (cédula): valid, with no expiry; the blue card is gone
- **Rule:** the vehicle identification cards are delivered in digital form with the same validity as the physical ones; they do not expire while the owner does not change (DNU 70/2023 arts 361-362, applied by Disposición DNRPA 29/2024). The card for an authorised driver (*cédula azul*) was abolished by the same Disposición because the green card no longer expires, and the owner's authorisation can be shown by handing over the card. The digital card in Mi Argentina has the same validity as the physical one while it is valid (Disposición Conjunta 1/2019; Mi Argentina FAQ).
- **Why:** a driver who is not the owner can show the owner's card (digital or paper) as authorisation; many people still believe the blue card is mandatory.
- **How:** the owner opens Mi Argentina > Mis vehículos and shows the QR; a non-owner driver should carry the owner's digital or paper card (or the owner's authorisation in the app). A new card is issued when ownership, address, engine, chassis or plates (*chapas patente*) change (Disposición art 1 as changed).
- **Verify:** the cards appear in Mi Argentina after identity validation; they work offline once loaded.
- **Don't:** take a screenshot of the card to show it: it is designed to show a live QR.
- **Source:** Disposición DNRPA 29/2024 (BO aviso 307350), https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/307350/20250317 (read 2026-11-15); Mi Argentina FAQ cédula digital https://www.argentina.gob.ar/miargentina/servicios/cedulas-de-tu-vehiculo-digital/preguntas-frecuentes (read 2026-11-15). Registry procedures: ownership-and-paperwork.md.

### VEH-LEY-14: Insurance proof: the compulsory cover must be in force and shown
- **Rule:** every motor vehicle, trailer and motorcycle must have insurance covering damage to third parties, transported or not (art 68); the proof in force must be carried and may be shown on paper or on a digital device (art 40 c, text of Ley 27.510). Driving without compulsory insurance (*seguro obligatorio*) is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 f, y) and the insurer pays the sanatorium or funeral costs of third parties at once (art 68).
- **Why:** it is both a roadside requirement and your protection if you hit someone.
- **How:** download the proof from your insurer's app, keep a PDF in the phone and a paper copy. Amounts, types of cover and what to do after a claim are in insurance.md.
- **Verify:** the policy start and end dates and the plate on the proof.
- **Don't:** use the proof of a different vehicle or an old month's proof.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 40 c, 68, 77 f, y (biblioteca.afip.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Decreto 196/2025 art 16 (reg 40 requires "seguro obligatorio vigente").

### VEH-LEY-15: Technical inspection (VTV/RTO): obligation and validity terms
- **Rule:** every motor vehicle, trailer and semi-trailer on public roads is subject to periodic technical inspection (*revisión técnica obligatoria, RTO/VTV*) (art 34). Under reg art 34 as replaced by Decreto 196/2025: private zero-km vehicles have 60 months from first registration before the first inspection; a periodic inspection of a private vehicle is valid 24 months if the vehicle is up to 10 years old since first registration and 12 months if older; jurisdictions may set longer terms except for non-private vehicles (maximum 12 months); the workshop issues a certificate (CRT) and a windscreen sticker (*oblea / identificación de la revisión*) or a digital certificate with the same validity; vehicles with natural gas (GNC) must show the ENARGAS compliance; a roadside quick check (*revisión rápida y aleatoria*) is free. PBA did not adhere to the new national terms and keeps the annual inspection for private vehicles from 2 years old (PBA minister, March 2026, press). A 1994 Gol is more than 10 years old: annual under the national baseline and in PBA.
- **Why:** driving without proof of inspection is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 x) and the insurer or the authority may use it against you.
- **How:** look at the date on the sticker or in Mi Argentina and book the appointment 30 days before it expires; for the process, fees and what is checked see ownership-and-paperwork.md and car-maintenance-basics.md. CABA requires the certificate (Ley 2148 art 5.2.2 e); its term should be checked on the City's VTV site.
- **Verify:** certificate date; in PBA the VTV portal of the province.
- **Don't:** assume the "5 years for zero km" or "every 2 years" figures apply in PBA; and don't confuse the national 24-month term with PBA's annual one.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 34 and 77 x; Decreto 196/2025 art 12 (reg 34) (argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-196-2025-410682/texto, read 2026-11-15); Decreto 139/2026 (BO 2026-03-09, confirms the reform stays). Not verified in a primary source: PBA's annual-from-2-years rule (La Nación, 2026-03-14, secondary; Ley 13.927 art 16 delegates to regulation) and CABA's term.

### VEH-LEY-16: What happens if you drive with an expired or missing VTV
- **Rule:** it is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 x). The regulation says vehicles that cannot prove the inspection are summoned to do it and "may not leave the jurisdiction where they are registered" until they do (reg art 34 items 7-8), without prejudice to penalties. In PBA the licence (*licencia de conducir*) is retained and replaced by a citation slip (*boleta de citación del inculpado*) valid 30 days (extendable to a maximum of 90 days), within which you must pay or defend yourself; after paying you must also prove you did the inspection (Ley 13.927 art 38).
- **Why:** it can cost you the use of the licence for some weeks and, in an accident, gives the insurer or the other party an argument.
- **How:** if stopped without VTV: get the infraction record (*acta de infracción*) and a copy; do the inspection immediately (the 60-day re-inspection rule for "conditional" results is in reg art 34 item 17); present the new certificate with your defence or payment (see fines-and-points.md).
- **Verify:** the article cited in the record (art 77 x of Ley 24.449) and the date; in PBA the date of the citation slip.
- **Don't:** keep driving on the 30-day slip after it expired (after 90 days the retained licence is destroyed and the right to drive lapses, Ley 13.927 art 38).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 77 x; Decreto 196/2025 art 12 (reg 34 items 7, 8, 17); Ley 13.927 PBA art 38 (https://normas.gba.gob.ar/documentos/0YqDnfd0.html, read 2026-11-15). Fine amounts: fines-and-points.md.

### VEH-LEY-17: Plates must be fitted, legible and unmodified
- **Rule:** vehicles in categories M and N (cars, vans, trucks, buses) carry two plates (*chapas patente*), front and rear; trailers carry one at the rear; plates must be legible, of the standard type and without accessories (art 40 d; reg art 40 as replaced). Only identification decals (official or private) are allowed on the glass (reg art 40, referring to art 48 q).
- **Why:** driving without the regulation plates is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 f); the Gol of 1994 may still carry the old two-letter plates: if they are the ones the registry gave, they are valid; if they were replaced the card shows the new plate.
- **How:** compare the plates with the vehicle card (*cédula de identificación del automotor*); replace damaged plates at the registry (see ownership-and-paperwork.md).
- **Verify:** plate characters match the cédula.
- **Don't:** cover the plate with a frame, cover or tow-ball; stick anything on the windscreen except official decals in the lower part.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 40 d, 77 f; Decreto 196/2025 art 16 (reg 40), argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-196-2025-410682/texto (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: whether the old plates of a 1994 car remain valid without replacement (registry rule).

### VEH-LEY-18: An officer can only ask for the listed documents and must give them back
- **Rule:** at the officer's request you must present the licence (*licencia de conducir*) and "the other documentation required"; it must be returned immediately after checking, and may not be retained except in the cases the law provides (art 37). The regulation lists the only documents that may be demanded: licence, vehicle identification and inspection proof (reg art 37 as replaced by Decreto 196/2025; see VEH-LEY-12 for the extra CABA items). Licence retention is allowed only for the causes in art 72 b (VEH-LEY-47).
- **Why:** officers sometimes ask for papers no longer required (blue card, "libre deuda", paid-tax receipts) or keep the licence without a legal cause.
- **How:** show the documents, ask for the return, and if they are kept ask for a receipt that names the legal cause; write down name, unit and place.
- **Verify:** art 37 of the law and art 72 b to check the cause cited.
- **Don't:** hand over the documents to anyone who is not in uniform or identified, or leave the original with the officer; show the screen of the phone instead of handing over the phone.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 37, 72 b; Decreto 196/2025 art 14 (reg 37) (read 2026-11-15, texts as in VEH-LEY-12).

## Mandatory equipment and vehicle condition

### VEH-LEY-19: Carry a valid fire extinguisher
- **Rule:** cars and all motor vehicles except motorcycles must have a fire extinguisher (*matafuego*) (art 40 f). It must be built, maintained and its charge checked periodically according to the relevant IRAM standards or equivalent international standards (reg art 29 A 6.2 a, text of Decreto 196/2025); it must be placed where the manufacturer indicates (Decreto 32/2018 text, reg art 40 f.1).
- **Why:** a charge that expired is not an extinguisher and may fail at the inspection.
- **How:** buy one with a gauge and a label with the charge date; fix it in the bracket within reach of the driver; recharge it at an authorised firm before the date. For the 1994 Gol the engine fire is the main risk: see car-maintenance-basics.md.
- **Verify:** pressure gauge in the green zone and a valid charge label; the gauge can be read from the driver's seat.
- **Don't:** leave it rolling loose in the boot (it must be fixed), or use a toy-sized can or an expired one.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 40 f; Decreto 196/2025 art 10 (reg 29 A 6.2 a), https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-196-2025-410682/texto and Decreto 32/2018 text (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the exact size/class (for example 1 kg ABC) required for a private car; the old text referred to reg art 29 for category M1/N1.

### VEH-LEY-20: Carry portable warning triangles (*balizas*)
- **Rule:** vehicles except motorcycles must carry standard portable warning triangles (*balizas portátiles*) (art 40 f). The 2025 text says motor vehicles must carry them "in an accessible place" (reg art 29 A 6.2 c). The previous text (Decreto 32/2018) asked for at least two, triangular retro-reflective, side 0.4-0.5 m with at least 0.25 m² of red retro-reflective material and a stable base (IRAM 10.031); yellow flashing lamps visible from 500 m were also accepted.
- **Why:** it is the legal way to warn traffic when stopped on the road; it is also checked at the inspection.
- **How:** carry two, in the boot or under the seat, not under the luggage; in a breakdown switch on the hazard lights (*luces de emergencia*) and place the triangle at a suitable distance behind the car (CABA: at least one, on the side the traffic comes from on a one-way street, one on each side on a two-way street when the car is next to the centre line, Ley 2148 art 5.5.3; a common practice is about 50 m, adapt to the road speed).
- **Verify:** triangles with an IRAM label or a sign of homologation, no cracks.
- **Don't:** put them while standing on a motorway (*autopista*) lane: first leave the carriageway (see trip-preparation.md for the breakdown routine).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 40 f; Decreto 196/2025 art 10 (reg 29 A 6.2 c); Decreto 32/2018 reg 40 f.2 (replaced in 2025, quoted as the former text); Ley 2148 CABA art 5.5.3 (juristeca h-tit-5) (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the 50 m figure and whether two triangles are still required nationally after the 2025 text (the article now says only "accessible").

### VEH-LEY-21: Reflective vest: required in CABA, announced nationally
- **Rule:** in CABA you must carry a reflective vest (*chaleco reflectante*) in an accessible place inside the passenger compartment (Ley 2148 art 6.1.2 h). The national regulation lists the supply of a high-visibility vest in the vehicle among the future safety measures the authority may phase in with manufacturers (reg art 29, last paragraph of Decreto 196/2025), without making it mandatory yet in the text read. In PBA the driver and passenger of mopeds and motorcycles must wear a reflective vest with the plate number (Ley 13.927 art 48 as amended by Ley 15.143).
- **Why:** to be visible when getting out of a stopped car on the road, which is when most pedestrian-hit casualties happen.
- **How:** keep a vest in the cabin (not in the boot), put it on before leaving the car on a road at night or on a motorway (*autopista*); one per occupant is a good practice.
- **Verify:** check the CABA article and any new ANSV disposition; the vest should be reflective (EN 20471 type, not mandatory by the text).
- **Don't:** leave the vest in the boot: it is required "accessible in the cabin" in CABA.
- **Source:** Ley 2148 CABA art 6.1.2 h (juristeca h-tit-6, read 2026-11-15); Decreto 196/2025 art 10 (reg 29, last paragraphs); Ley 13.927 PBA art 48 (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: any ANSV disposition making the vest mandatory nationally after 2025.

### VEH-LEY-22: Wear the seat belt in every seat and keep the headrests
- **Rule:** cars must have standardised seat belts (*cinturón de seguridad*) and headrests (*apoyacabezas*) where the regulation says (art 30 a); all occupants must wear the belts of vehicles that must have them (art 40 k); driving without every occupant belted is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 t) and carrying more occupants than the number of belts is another (reg art 77 p). Headrests in used vehicles are required only if the original seat design allows them (reg art 29 A 6.1 of Decreto 196/2025). CABA requires belts for all occupants in all vehicles except motorcycles and bicycles (Ley 2148 art 6.1.3), and airbags do not replace the belt (art 6.1.3 c).
- **Why:** the belt is the base of passive safety; a belt on the rear seat protects the driver too, and a loose passenger is a projectile.
- **How:** check that each belt locks and retracts; an old car may have only lap belts in the back: wear them; replace frayed belts at a workshop with the same type.
- **Verify:** pull the belt sharply, it must lock; check the buckle clicks.
- **Don't:** put one belt over two people or put the belt behind the back; do not carry more people than belts (reg art 77 p).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 30 a, 40 k, 77 t; reg art 77 p (text in Decreto 32/2018 art 17, still in force); Decreto 196/2025 art 10 (reg 29 A 6.1); Ley 2148 CABA art 6.1.3 (juristeca h-tit-6, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-23: Children in the car: rear seat and child restraint
- **Rule:** under-10s must travel in the rear seat (art 40 g); driving with under-10s in the front seat is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 u). The regulation added in 2018 that they must travel "restrained to the rear seat with the child restraint (*sistema de retención infantil, SRI*) system (*SRI*) approved for their weight and size" (reg art 40 g.1, Decreto 32/2018) and the grave-fault text of reg art 77 u (not replaced in 2025) also covers travelling "without child restraint devices". In CABA: under-12s may not travel in the front seat and babies or children in arms are forbidden in the front (Ley 2148 art 5.2.4 c); a passenger under 12 and shorter than 1.50 m must use a homologated restraint for weight/height certified to IRAM 3680-1/2, FMVSS 213, UN R44/04, AS/NZS 1754 or NBR 14400 (art 6.1.3 b). Decreto 196/2025 replaced reg art 40 and the paragraph g.1 no longer appears in the new text.
- **Why:** crash forces on a child who is not in a suitable seat are often fatal; the age rule is a minimum, not a safe limit.
- **How:** infant seat rear-facing from birth, then a forward-facing seat, then a booster until the adult belt fits (about 1.45-1.50 m); always in the rear seat; tight installation (belt or ISOFIX); never in front with an active airbag.
- **Verify:** seat label with the standard (R44/R129, FMVSS 213 or IRAM 3680); manufacturer's weight/height range.
- **Don't:** rely on arms or on the adult belt for a small child; do not buy a second-hand seat that was in a crash.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 40 g, 77 u; Decreto 32/2018 (reg art 40 g.1, reg art 77 u), https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-32-2018-305742/texto; Decreto 196/2025 art 16 (reg 40 replaced); Ley 2148 CABA arts 5.2.4 c, 6.1.3 b (juristeca, read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: whether the SRI duty remains enforceable nationally by another instrument after the 2025 replacement of reg art 40 (reg art 77 u still mentions it), and the practical advice on seat types (general road-safety practice, not law).

### VEH-LEY-24: Motorcycles: approved helmet, glasses, reflective vest where required
- **Rule:** every occupant of a motorcycle must wear a standardised helmet (*casco*), and the driver must wear glasses if there is no windscreen (art 40 j); riding without the helmet correctly fastened by any occupant is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 s). Helmets must conform to UNECE R22-05 certification (reg art 29 I as replaced in 2025). In PBA the helmet must carry the plate number in reflective characters on both sides and driver and passenger wear a reflective vest (*chaleco reflectante*) with the plate (Ley 13.927 art 48, Ley 15.143); not wearing the helmet is a grave fault in PBA (art 48).
- **Why:** the helmet is the main protection for riders; PBA adds identification to deter crimes committed from motorcycles.
- **How:** buy a helmet with the ECE 22.05 (or newer 22.06) label, replace it after a hard impact (the label says so), fasten the strap.
- **Verify:** the ECE label inside the helmet; plate characters at least 3 cm high in PBA (Ley 13.927 art 48 / Decreto 32/2018 reg art 40 j.1.4).
- **Don't:** ride a moped with a passenger under the legal conditions (reg art 40 g.2 text in Decreto 32/2018: passenger weight limit 40 kg on mopeds, one passenger behind the rider on motorcycles).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 40 j, 77 s; Decreto 196/2025 art 10 (reg 29 I); Ley 13.927 PBA art 48 and art 77 reference (normas.gba.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Decreto 32/2018 reg art 40 j (read 2026-11-15). The newer R22-06 is mentioned as common practice; not verified in the decree.

### VEH-LEY-25: Tyres: 1.6 mm tread, no cuts, same type per axle
- **Rule:** driving with tyres (*neumáticos, cubiertas*) that have faults or without the legal tread depth is prohibited (art 48 l). Minimum tread depth is 1.6 mm in the central area for cars, 1 mm for motorcycles, 0.5 mm for mopeds (reg art 48 l.2; reg art 29 A 4.3). Tyres on the same axle must be of the same type, size and construction; a temporary spare is allowed while in emergency use with its stated pressure and speed (reg art 29 A 4.4); retreaded ("redibujados") tyres are forbidden except where the standard allows; cut or damaged tyres are forbidden; remoulded tyres may not be used on both axles of motorcycles (reg art 29 A 4.5-4.7).
- **Why:** worn tyres lengthen braking and aquaplane; "faulty tyre" is a ground to stop you (art 72 c.1).
- **How:** check the tread wear indicators moulded in the grooves, the sidewalls for bulges, and the age (DOT code, four digits = week/year; a tyre older than about 6-10 years is aged: general practice, not in the law).
- **Verify:** use a depth gauge (inspection stations have one) and read the wear indicators moulded in the grooves.
- **Don't:** mix a different tyre on one side of the axle or fit a temporary spare as a permanent wheel. The law does not list a spare wheel among the mandatory items; carry one and the jack anyway (not verified in a primary source for the inspection checklist, Anexo J).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 48 l, 72 c.1; Decreto 196/2025 art 10 (reg 29 A 4.1-4.12); reg art 48 l.1-l.4 in Decreto 32/2018 (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: tyre age limits and the spare wheel at the inspection (Anexo J).

### VEH-LEY-26: Lights fitted must be the legal ones; extra lights are limited
- **Rule:** cars must have headlights (white or yellow, high and low beam), position lamps (front white or yellow, rear red), amber turn signals front and rear, rear red brake lights (they light before the brake acts), a rear plate lamp, white reversing light, hazard lights (*luces de emergencia*) and a flash-to-pass system (art 31). Any other lights are forbidden except up to two fog lamps and, only on dirt roads, spotlights (art 31 last paragraph). Imported vehicles complying with American standards may have red rear turn signals (art 31 c).
- **Why:** lights are checked at the inspection and at roadside; illegal ones can lead to retention (art 72 c.1).
- **How:** check every bulb once a month with a helper; carry spare bulbs and fuses for the car (see car-maintenance-basics.md and vw-gol-1994-repairs.md for the Gol).
- **Verify:** the headlight aim at the inspection (optical aligner with lux meter, reg art 34 item 15).
- **Don't:** fit blue or coloured headlights, LED bars without standard, or tint the light covers.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 31, 72 c.1; Decreto 196/2025 art 12 (reg 34 item 15) (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-27: Do not remove or alter safety systems, exhaust noise or emissions
- **Rule:** the safety characteristics of vehicles released to traffic cannot be changed except as the regulation allows (art 34); the vehicle must have its original safety systems in good working order, at the risk of retention (art 40 i, art 72 c.1); gases, smoke, noise or radiation above the limits are prohibited (art 48 w); the horn (*bocina*) and siren must be authorised and used only in danger or on rural roads (art 48 v); wheels and parts outside the bumper that are dangerous are prohibited (art 48 y); cargo that hides lights or exceeds the vehicle limits is prohibited (art 48 q).
- **Why:** modifications void the inspection and can void the insurance; they are also a ground for retention.
- **How:** before modifying (exhaust, suspension, lowering, wheels, bull bars, roof racks, gas conversion) ask a workshop registered for the specialty and, for GNC, the ENARGAS rules (GNC cars must show compliance to get the VTV, reg art 34 item 13).
- **Verify:** the VTV certificate lists the modification if it was approved; keep the paperwork.
- **Don't:** use a "straight-through" exhaust, remove the catalytic converter or mufflers, or fit bull bars that project beyond the bumper.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 34, 40 i, 48 q, v, w, y, 72 c.1; Decreto 196/2025 art 12 (reg 34 item 13) (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-28: Do not repair the car in the street (except emergency fixes)
- **Rule:** repairs in urban areas are prohibited except "arreglos de circunstancia" in any type of vehicle (art 48 r); obstructing the carriageway or the shoulder in any way is also prohibited (art 48 t). The regulation says "Sin reglamentar" for item r (reg art 48 r), so the law's wording applies. A vehicle left abandoned or broken and not repaired or removed at once can be towed (*acarreo* or *remoción*) at the owner's cost (art 72 c.5).
- **Why:** DIY repairs on the street block traffic and are dangerous; this matters for people who repair their own cars.
- **How:** move the car to a garage or a private place; for a flat tyre (*cubierta*) or a dead battery do the quick fix on the spot only if you are safe from traffic (hazard lights (*luces de emergencia*), triangles, hand brake, wheel chocks), and never lie under a car on the road. See car-maintenance-basics.md for safe repairs.
- **Verify:** local rules may be stricter (for example sidewalk and parking restrictions in the municipality).
- **Don't:** work under a car held only by a jack on a slope, or leave a dismantled car on the street overnight.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 48 r, t and 72 c.5; reg art 48 r (Decreto 32/2018 text, "Sin reglamentar") (read 2026-11-15). The definition of "arreglos de circunstancia" is not found in the text read: ask the local authority.

## Rules of the road

### VEH-LEY-29: Maximum speed limits by road type (art 51)
- **Rule:** maximum speeds: urban street 40 km/h; avenue 60 km/h; urban roads with coordinated traffic lights (*semáforo*), for motorcycles and cars only, the coordination speed; rural road 110 km/h for motorcycles, cars and vans, 90 km/h for minibuses, buses and motorised homes, 80 km/h for trucks and cars towing a motorhome and for dangerous goods; semi-motorway (*autopista*) same as rural but 120 km/h for motorcycles and cars; motorway 130 km/h for motorcycles and cars (buses 100 km/h, others as rural); special limits: urban crossroads without lights: precautionary speed never above 30 km/h; level crossing (*paso a nivel*) without barrier or lights: not above 20 km/h and only after making sure no train is coming; near schools, sports grounds and crowds while open: precautionary speed not above 20 km/h; roads that cross urban areas: 60 km/h unless signed otherwise (art 51).
- **Why:** speed over the limit by more than 10% is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 n); precautionary speed (art 50) applies even below the limit (VEH-LEY-31).
- **How:** unsigned limits are these; a speed limit sign overrides them in that stretch (art 52 b); minimum speeds are half of the maximum in urban areas and motorways and 40 km/h on roads and semi-motorways (art 52 a).
- **Verify:** the speedometer of an old car can be off: check with a GPS app, especially in PBA and CABA where automatic cameras apply.
- **Don't:** assume the limit is signed everywhere: in the absence of a sign the table above applies.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 51, 52, 77 n (biblioteca.afip.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15). Note: some provinces and municipalities have different limits; check yours.

### VEH-LEY-30: Speed limits in CABA and PBA: local differences
- **Rule:** CABA (Ley 2148 art 6.2.2): fast roads such as Av. Cantilo, Lugones, the central lanes of Gral. Paz up to Autopista Pascual Palazzo, Autopista 25 de Mayo: 100 km/h for all except cargo and passenger vehicles over 3,500 kg, with 80 km/h on other Gral. Paz sections and 60 km/h on heavy-traffic lanes; avenues 60 km/h, but 70 km/h on Figueroa Alcorta, del Libertador, 27 de Febrero, Quiroga and Costanera, and 40 km/h on Corrientes between Junín and Libertad; streets and fast-road service roads 40 km/h; passages and "calles de convivencia" 20 km/h. Special CABA limits (6.2.3): street crossings without light 30 km/h, avenue crossings without light 40 km/h, level crossings (*paso a nivel*) 20 km/h, school zones at entry and exit times the minimum of the road type, pedestrian streets at walking pace, urban buses and heavy cargo 60 km/h on fast roads and 50 km/h on avenues, electric-assist bikes and personal mobility devices 25 km/h. PBA: follows art 51 by adhesion, and speed cameras (*cinemómetro, fotomulta*) must be signed 500 m ahead where the limit is lower than the legal maximum (Ley 13.927 art 28 bis, see road-signs.md).
- **Why:** the City has lower or different numbers on key roads; CABA cameras ticket accordingly.
- **How:** read the speed signs on Gral. Paz and Lugones (they vary by lane and section) and slow at pedestrian streets.
- **Verify:** Juristeca/Boletín Oficial CABA text of art 6.2.2 for later changes (Ley 6.399 of 2021 is the last one shown).
- **Don't:** apply the national 60/40 numbers on the City's 70 km/h avenues: the City's figures prevail there.
- **Source:** Ley 2148 CABA arts 6.2.2-6.2.3 (https://juristeca.jusbaires.gob.ar/compilacion-normativa-juristeca/ley-2148/h-tit-6/, consolidated to 2024-02-29, read 2026-11-15); Ley 13.927 PBA art 28 bis (normas.gba.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: changes to CABA speed limits after the consolidation date.

### VEH-LEY-31: Precautionary speed and a safe following distance
- **Rule:** the driver must always travel at a speed that keeps full control of the vehicle, taking into account health, vehicle and load, visibility, road conditions, weather and traffic density; if that is not possible he must leave the road or stop (art 50). The following distance must be prudent for the speed (art 48 g); the regulation says at least a separation of 2 seconds, except long cargo units (over 20.50 m) on two-way roads, which must keep 100 m (reg art 48 g). In rural areas, buses and trucks must keep at least 100 m between them (art 48 n). Reversing is allowed only to park or to leave a garage or dead end (*calle sin salida*) (art 48 h).
- **Why:** the 2-second rule gives time to brake on a dry road; in wet or fog double or triple it.
- **How:** pick a fixed point on the road; when the car ahead passes it count "one thousand and one, one thousand and two"; if you pass it before 2 s, drop back.
- **Verify:** fog with the fog marks (see road-signs.md VEH-SEN-30) and at night.
- **Don't:** tailgate to "pressure" the car in front; do not brake abruptly or weave without reason (art 48 d).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 48 d, g, h, n, 50; reg art 48 g (Decreto 32/2018, text in force, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-32: Use headlights correctly (low beam on national routes day and night)
- **Rule:** on national routes low beams or daytime running lights must be on all the time, day and night, regardless of visibility, except when high beam applies and at level crossings (*paso a nivel*) (art 47 a). High beam is compulsory only in rural zones and motorways (*autopista*) when natural light or visibility is insufficient (art 47 b); low beams, position and plate lights must be used when natural light is insufficient or visibility or traffic requires it (art 47 c); flashing is for crossings and to warn overtaking (*adelantamiento*) (art 47 d); hazard lights (*luces de emergencia*) to indicate stopping at tolls, dangerous zones or risky manoeuvres (art 47 e); brake, turn and reverse lights must work even in daylight (art 47 g). New zero-km models must have automatic low beams or DRL (art 47 h). CABA: low beams are compulsory 24 hours on Av. Cantilo, Lugones, Gral. Paz and all motorways (Ley 2148 art 6.3.1 a).
- **Why:** a car with lights on is seen earlier; on routes the fine is for not having them on.
- **How:** on a route switch on low beams when you leave the town, even at noon; a 1994 Gol has no DRL: turn the lights on by hand (see vw-gol-1994-repairs.md for the switch/relay).
- **Verify:** the dashboard pilot lights; walk around the car with a helper.
- **Don't:** use fog lights as daytime lights, or use high beams when you meet or follow another vehicle (dazzle).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 47 (biblioteca.afip.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA art 6.3.1 (juristeca h-tit-6, read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: whether PBA provincial roads require continuous low beams (no separate rule found in Ley 13.927).

### VEH-LEY-33: Right of way at crossroads: the vehicle coming from your right goes first (national rule)
- **Rule:** every driver must give way (*ceda el paso*) at crossroads to the vehicle crossing from the right; that priority is "absolute" and is only lost against (a) specific signs saying otherwise, (b) rail vehicles, (c) public emergency vehicles on duty, (d) vehicles on a semi-motorway (*autopista*) (you must stop before entering or crossing it), (e) pedestrians lawfully crossing on a crossing or marked danger zone (stop if you endanger them), (f) roundabout (*rotonda*) rules, and (g) in any case when you come from a dirt road onto a paved one, you pass beside the railway and the other vehicle leaves a level crossing (*paso a nivel*), you have stopped or are about to turn into another road, you lead animals or animal-drawn vehicles. If several exceptions occur together, the order of the article decides; in any other manoeuvre the priority belongs to who keeps his right; on steep narrow slopes the descending vehicle must reverse unless it carries a trailer and the ascending one does not (art 41).
- **Why:** unmarked T crossings still give priority to the right (the idea that "the road that continues has priority" is not in art 41, per press analysis of 2026).
- **How:** slow down, look right first, give way to the right; at a crossing with a STOP or CEDA EL PASO sign follow the sign (VEH-SEN-8 and VEH-SEN-9).
- **Verify:** the article; in PBA the new rule of VEH-LEY-34 applies to avenues and roads of higher rank.
- **Don't:** assume that the main avenue always wins nationally: by Ley 24.449 only a sign, a semi-motorway or the other listed exceptions remove the priority of the right (but see PBA).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 41 (biblioteca.afip.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); press analysis (Los Andes, 2026-06-30) used only as secondary support for the T-crossing remark.

### VEH-LEY-34: In PBA, avenues and roads of higher rank now have priority (Ley 15.657)
- **Rule:** Ley 15.657 added art 28 quater to Ley 13.927: the driver arriving at a crossing must give way (*ceda el paso*) to the vehicle from his right, but this priority is lost in these cases: specific signs; rail vehicles; public emergency vehicles giving warning signals; vehicles on a road of higher rank (motorways (*autopista*), semi-motorways, routes, highways and avenues; you must stop before entering or crossing); pedestrians on the crossing; when entering a roundabout (*rotonda*); on dirt roads towards paved roads; when a vehicle has stopped completely before entering the crossing and another arrives in motion; when turning into a transverse road; and in slow queues at an intersection the passage is alternated one vehicle at a time per transverse road. If several apply the order is the one of the article; if none applies the priority stays with the right.
- **Why:** it ends the doubt about whether an avenue has to give way to a side street coming from its right, but the drivers on the side street must now stop.
- **How:** in PBA, when you come from a street to an avenue, slow and give way even if the avenue traffic comes from your left; at slow queues wait your turn alternately.
- **Verify:** the law was published in Boletín Oficial 30353 on 2026-10-30; a legal commentary (secondary, Estudio Vilaplana, 2026-10-30) computed that it applies from 2026-11-08 (8 days after publication) because it has no special start date. Check the signs and any provincial regulation.
- **Don't:** apply this rule in CABA or other provinces without checking their codes.
- **Source:** Ley 13.927 PBA art 28 quater as inserted by Ley 15.657 (BO 30353 of 2026-10-30), https://normas.gba.gob.ar/ar-b/ley/2026/15657/632161 and updated text https://normas.gba.gob.ar/documentos/0YqDnfd0.html (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the entry-into-force date (secondary commentary, https://estudiovilaplana.com.ar/2026/10/11/prioridad-paso-avenidas-reforma-buenos-aires-2026/).

### VEH-LEY-35: Pedestrians and crossings: stop and never stop on the crossing
- **Rule:** pedestrians lawfully crossing on the crossing (*senda peatonal*) or in a danger zone signed as such have priority and the driver must stop if he endangers them (art 41 e); if the crossing is not painted, the imaginary prolongation of the pavement is the crossing (art 5 t). Vehicles must not park or stop on it (Anexo L H.5; art 49 b.3), and must stop at the stop line (*línea de detención*) before the crossing on a red light (art 44 a.2). The pedestrian has the benefit of doubt in accident presumptions as long as he has not seriously broken the rules (art 64). Pedestrians must use the crossing, and the overpass if there is one (art 38).
- **Why:** pedestrian priority is absolute at the crossing; in an accident the driver is presumed at fault if he lacked priority or committed an infraction (art 64).
- **How:** slow down early at crossings, check the left and right side of a car or bus that has stopped, and wait until the pedestrian is fully across; at a crossing with a traffic light (*semáforo*) the light decides.
- **Verify:** painted lines or stripes at crossings; Anexo L H.5 describes them (road-signs.md VEH-SEN-27).
- **Don't:** overtake a vehicle that is stopped at a pedestrian crossing.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 5 t, 38, 41 e, 44, 49 b.3, 64 (biblioteca.afip.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Anexo L H.5 in Decreto 196/2025 (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-36: Overtaking: only on the left, with visibility and signals
- **Rule:** overtaking (*adelantamiento*) is done on the left: check that the road is free on the left for a safe distance and nobody behind you is overtaking you; do not start near a junction, curve, bridge, hilltop or dangerous place; warn the car in front with flashes of the headlights (or the horn (*bocina*) on rural roads) and use the left indicator until the lateral move ends; overtake quickly and return to the right with the right indicator, without disturbing the overtaken car (art 42 a-d). The overtaken driver must keep right and may reduce speed (art 42 e); a left indicator on the car ahead means "do not overtake" (art 42 f). Overtaking on the right is exceptional: when the car ahead has signalled left or stop on its left, or in a traffic jam where the left lane is slower (art 42 h). Changing lanes or overtaking in curves, crossroads and dangerous zones is forbidden (art 48 j). Overtaking without respecting the requirements is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 v).
- **Why:** the highest-risk manoeuvre on two-lane roads.
- **How:** drop back, check mirror, signal, accelerate in the left lane, return only when you see the overtaken vehicle in your mirror; abort if a car comes the other way.
- **Verify:** continuous or double yellow lines forbid it (road-signs.md VEH-SEN-26); a "No overtaking" sign R.6 forbids it.
- **Don't:** overtake on a hill crest, in fog, or when the overtaken car is itself overtaking a vehicle.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 42, 48 j, 77 v (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-37: Turns, lane use and left turns on two-way roads
- **Rule:** to turn, respect the signs, signal in advance with the indicator until the end of the junction, move 30 m before to the side nearest to the turn, slow down gradually and turn at moderate speed; reinforce with a hand signal when turning into a minor road or a private property (art 43 a-d). On roads with more than two lanes per direction: use middle lanes only if no free lane on the right, stay in the lane's centre, signal lane changes, do not drive below the operating speed of your lane, and move right when someone behind signals he is overtaking (*adelantamiento*) (art 45). On two-way roads with traffic lights (*semáforo*), left turns are not allowed unless a signal permits it (art 44 f). Do not block a junction when the other side has no space (art 44 e; art 48 f).
- **Why:** many CABA tickets are for left turns at lights and for blocking junctions.
- **How:** in CABA the left turn on a two-way street without a light, or with a turn signal, is made from the lane nearest the centre line, 30 m before, without crossing the axis (Ley 2148 art 6.1.14 b); look for a "permitted left turn" sign (Anexo L I.21) or the left-turn arrow light.
- **Verify:** signs at the junction and the arrows on the pavement.
- **Don't:** turn from the wrong lane, "cut" the corner or turn without the indicator.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 43, 44 e-f, 45, 48 f (read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA art 6.1.14 (juristeca h-tit-6, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-38: Roundabouts: the vehicle inside has priority
- **Rule:** in a roundabout (*rotonda*) the circulation is continuous without stopping, keeping the central non-driveable area on your left (counter-clockwise); the vehicle already in the roundabout has priority over the one trying to enter and must give way (*ceda el paso*) to the one exiting, unless a sign says otherwise (art 43 e). Roundabout rules are an exception to the right-hand priority (*prioridad de paso*) (art 41 f). PBA repeats: entering a roundabout you give way to those already circulating (Ley 13.927 art 28 quater f).
- **Why:** if you stop inside you get hit from behind; the priority is of the one inside.
- **How:** slow before the entry, look left, enter when the lane is free, use the right indicator before your exit; the warning sign P.21 shows the circle of three arrows (road-signs.md).
- **Verify:** signs at the entry; some roundabouts have traffic lights (*semáforo*).
- **Don't:** stop inside the roundabout or enter fast expecting the others to give way.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 41 f, 43 e (read 2026-11-15); Ley 13.927 PBA art 28 quater f (normas.gba.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Anexo L P.21 (Decreto 196/2025).

### VEH-LEY-39: Traffic lights: green, amber, red and the junction rule
- **Rule:** green: go on; red: stop before the stop line (*línea de detención*) or the pedestrian crossing (*senda peatonal*) and make no movement; amber: stop if you estimate you will not clear the junction before the red (or clear it at once); flashing amber: cross with caution; flashing red: stop and move off only when there is no risk; at a level crossing (*paso a nivel*) the start of the descent of the barrier equals amber (art 44 a). On signalised roads the normal crossing rules do not apply (art 44 c) and the maximum speed is the signed speed for the coordinated greens (art 44 d). Details of arrows, pedestrian lights and special signals are in road-signs.md (VEH-SEN-31 to VEH-SEN-35).
- **Why:** running a red light is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 o).
- **How:** at a light, brake at amber if you can stop safely; never accelerate to "beat the red".
- **Verify:** the stop line and the light for your lane.
- **Don't:** enter the junction if the exit is blocked, even on green (art 44 e).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 44, 77 o (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-40: Motorways: left lane to overtake, no stopping, no slow vehicles
- **Rule:** on motorways (*autopistas*) the far-left lane is for maximum speed and overtaking (*adelantamiento*); pedestrians, human-powered vehicles, animal-drawn vehicles, mopeds and special machinery may not use them; it is forbidden to park or stop (even to drop passengers) except in the bays built for it; vehicles towed after an accident or breakdown must leave at the first exit (art 46). On semi-motorways b, c and d apply (art 46). Beginners may not drive on them during the first six months (art 13 d). When you must stop use the emergency bay, exit the car by the side away from traffic and stay behind the barrier (general safety practice; see trip-preparation.md).
- **Why:** high speed and low visibility to stopped vehicles; vehicles stopped on a motorway cause the most deadly collisions.
- **How:** enter the motorway by the acceleration lane, move to the left only to overtake and return right; in CABA the left lane use is the same (Ley 2148 art 6.1.9).
- **Verify:** signs at the entry; see road-signs.md for I.12 and I.13 (start and end of motorway).
- **Don't:** drive in the left lane at low speed or stop on the lane to wait for someone.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 13 d, 46 (read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA art 6.1.9 (juristeca h-tit-6, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-LEY-41: Level crossings: never cross if a train may be coming
- **Rule:** rail vehicles always have priority (art 41 b). Do not cross if you perceive a train, if warning signals are being made, if the barriers are down or moving, or if your exit is not clear; do not stop on the rails or within 5 m of them when there are no barriers; do not stay where you can block the barriers (art 48 k). The start of the barrier's descent equals an amber light (art 44 a.6). At a crossing without barrier or lights the speed must not exceed 20 km/h and only after making sure no train comes (art 51 e.2). When the crossing is closed stop so you do not obstruct traffic parallel to the tracks; if the barrier is out of order, look both ways in the visibility triangle before the tracks (reg art 48 k.1-k.2, Decreto 32/2018). Use the flash of the headlights at crossings (art 47 d).
- **Why:** a car cannot stop a train; this is the most severe class of collisions.
- **How:** stop before the stop line (*línea de detención*) or the St. Andrew's cross (road-signs.md VEH-SEN-35 explains the signs), look and listen both ways, check the exit is free and cross without stopping or changing gear; if your car stalls on the track, get everybody out and away from the track in the direction the train comes from (to avoid debris), then call 911.
- **Verify:** signs P.1, P.3, R.30 and markings H.13 (VEH-SEN-19, VEH-SEN-35).
- **Don't:** follow the car in front into a queue that stops on the tracks; do not zigzag between half-lowered barriers.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 41 b, 44 a.6, 47 d, 48 k, 51 e.2 (read 2026-11-15); reg art 48 k in Decreto 32/2018 (read 2026-11-15); Anexo L P.1, P.3, R.30, H.13 (Decreto 196/2025). Not verified in a primary source: the advice on where to go if the car stalls (general rail-safety guidance, not in the text).

### VEH-LEY-42: Phone, headphones and screens: hands-free only, and not even that for video
- **Rule:** it is prohibited to drive using headphones and communication systems of continuous manual operation (art 48 x), and it is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 q). The regulation extends the grave fault to screens or video monitors in the driver's compartment and to any other element that causes distraction or requires the driver's sensory attention (reg art 77 q, Decreto 32/2018 text). The regulation also says that on conventional vehicles the driver must drive with both hands on the wheel except when operating other controls (reg art 39, Decreto 196/2025). CABA repeats the prohibition of headphones and continuous manual systems (Ley 2148 art 5.2.4 e).
- **Why:** it is a grave fault because it is among the leading causes of crashes.
- **How:** mount the phone on a holder, set the route before moving off, use voice commands, and stop in a safe place for calls or messages; if the car has no hands-free system, do not answer.
- **Verify:** the legal wording is "communication systems of manual continuous operation": a call held in the hand is covered.
- **Don't:** hold the phone at a red light "just for a second" or use earbuds in both ears.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 48 x, 77 q; Decreto 196/2025 art 15 (reg 39); reg art 77 q in Decreto 32/2018 art 17 (read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA art 5.2.4 e (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: whether a phone mounted and used with voice commands is accepted in all jurisdictions (the text does not mention it).

### VEH-LEY-43: Alcohol and drugs: limits (zero nationally and in PBA, 0.5 g/l in CABA)
- **Rule:** nationally it is forbidden to drive any kind of vehicle with an alcohol level above zero (0) milligrams per litre of blood, and to drive with physical or mental impairment or after taking drugs or medicines that reduce driving ability (art 48 a, text of Ley 27.714). PBA: forbidden to drive any motor vehicle with alcohol above 0 (Ley 13.927 art 28 ter, Ley 15.402), with disqualification of 3 to 18 months depending on the level and 18 months for refusing the test (art 39 bis). CABA keeps its own limits: more than 0.5 g/l for any vehicle, more than 0.2 g/l for motorcycles and 0.0 for professional, school and novice drivers (Ley 2148 art 5.4.4). Driving intoxicated is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 m). The national regulation (Decreto 32/2018 text, reg art 48 a.1.2.1) still mentions 0.5 g/l and 1 g/l as thresholds for towing and fault: it pre-dates Ley 27.714 and the law prevails.
- **Why:** a different limit in each jurisdiction; the only safe rule is zero.
- **How:** if you drink, do not drive; also check the leaflet of medicines for drowsiness and use a designated driver or taxi.
- **Verify:** breath test procedure and thresholds of the jurisdiction where you are (VEH-LEY-49).
- **Don't:** estimate by "one beer is fine": a 0.5 g/l allowance only exists in CABA.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 48 a, 77 m (biblioteca.afip.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Ley 13.927 PBA arts 28 ter, 39, 39 bis (normas.gba.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA art 5.4.4 (juristeca h-tit-5, read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: whether the national regulation art 48 / 73 was updated after Ley 27.714 and how "0 mg/l of blood" is measured in practice.

### VEH-LEY-44: Parking and stopping: where it is forbidden
- **Rule:** in urban areas park parallel to the kerb (*cordón*) leaving at least 50 cm between vehicles (art 49 a); do not park where safety, visibility or traffic flow is affected or the signs are hidden; at corners between the ideal vertex and the line of the prolonged building line (*ochava*); on pedestrian or bicycle crossings, pavements, rails, on the carriageway (double parking) and within 10 m before and after a bus stop; in front of the door of hospitals, schools and public services within 10 m on each side; in front of cinemas and theatres while open; at garage entrances with the sign; for more than 5 days or what the local authority sets; buses, motorhomes and trucks only in signed places (art 49 b); no reserved spaces without signs and permit (art 49 c). In rural zones park as far as possible from the carriageway and shoulder (art 49). Parking on a motorway (*autopista*) shoulder or stopping without emergency is prohibited (art 48 i). The kerb colour rule (Anexo L H.15): red kerb = no parking or stopping, yellow kerb = no parking but you can stop (road-signs.md VEH-SEN-29).
- **Why:** badly parked cars blocking traffic or emergency spaces can be towed (*acarreo*) at the owner's cost (art 72 c.5).
- **How:** look for signs on both sides, the kerb colour and the time limit; for "estacionamiento medido" read the local meter rules.
- **Verify:** a "No estacionar" sign (R.8) and "No estacionar ni detenerse" (R.9): the second includes stopping (road-signs.md VEH-SEN-12).
- **Don't:** leave the car "just two minutes" in a bus stop or in front of a garage; leave nobody inside.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 48 i, 49, 72 c.5 (read 2026-11-15); Anexo L R.8, R.9, H.15 (Decreto 196/2025, read 2026-11-15). Local parking rules and towing fees (CABA, municipalities) are not covered: not verified in a primary source.

### VEH-LEY-45: Hands on the wheel and other basic prohibitions
- **Rule:** on conventional vehicles the driver must drive with both hands on the wheel except when operating other controls (reg art 39 as replaced by Decreto 196/2025, which also sets rules for automated vehicles up to level 5, approved experimentally by the ANSV). It is also prohibited to drive on the wrong side (art 48 c, w), to slow down abruptly or zigzag without reason (art 48 d), to reverse except to park or leave a garage or dead end (*calle sin salida*) (art 48 h), to stop on the carriageway irregularly (art 48 i), to carry things that hide lights or protrude (art 48 q), to use the horn (*bocina*) except in danger or rural areas (art 48 v) and, in moving vehicles, to smoke or put arms out (the law lists it for transport vehicles, check the article).
- **Why:** in a crash a driver without control of the wheel is held at fault (art 64).
- **How:** adjust seat and mirrors before moving, do not eat or handle devices, use the horn only to prevent danger.
- **Verify:** wheel position at 9 and 3.
- **Don't:** race or do "sport" manoeuvres on public roads: unauthorised speed or skill competitions are sanctioned (see fines-and-points.md).
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 48, 64; Decreto 196/2025 art 15 (reg art 39) (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the smoking/arms rule applies to which vehicles (appears in a transport article).

## Police checks and accidents

### VEH-LEY-46: At a police check: what the officer may and may not do
- **Rule:** the authority must act on its own or after a complaint, identify himself and state his unit, and use the regulation form giving you a copy (art 70 a); your receipt of the copy of the record serves as a summons to appear before the judge in the place and period it states, never less than five days (art 69 e). He may ask for your licence (*licencia de conducir*) and the documents of VEH-LEY-12 and must return them at once (art 37), may stop you for checks (random roadside technical check, art 34), may retain the licence only in the cases of art 72 b (VEH-LEY-47), may hold the vehicle in the cases of art 72 c (VEH-LEY-48) and the documents of the vehicle only in the cases of art 72 e (adulterated, not matching the facts, etc.). He may not retain documents for other causes or demand papers not required (VEH-LEY-18). Fines are settled through the procedure of the record, not by paying the officer: the law forbids the State from giving gratifications to those who find infractions (art 69 g), so never offer or accept an informal payment. In PBA, controls must be signed and set up at a safe distance (Ley 13.927 art 28).
- **Why:** you can comply and still protect your rights.
- **How:** stay calm, keep the engine off, show documents, ask for the copy of the record; if you disagree write "no conforme" and sign, then file the defence within the term (fines-and-points.md).
- **Verify:** the record shows the article violated, place, date, time, officer and unit.
- **Don't:** offer money, argue, or sign blank forms.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 34, 37, 69 e, g, 70 a, 72 (read 2026-11-15); Ley 13.927 PBA art 28 (normas.gba.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15). 

### VEH-LEY-47: Licence retention: only for the causes in art 72 b (and the PBA 30-day slip)
- **Rule:** the authority must retain the licence (*licencia de conducir*) when it is expired, lapsed for unreported change of data, outside the age limits, adulterated or obtained in evident breach of the requirements, when the holder's psycho-physical fitness is evidently reduced (then art 19 applies) or when the holder is disqualified or suspended (art 72 b.1-6). For digital licences the Authority must design a mechanism that equals suspension to the retention of the physical card (reg art 72 as replaced). In PBA, for the grave faults of art 77 m, n, o, s, w, x and y of Ley 24.449, the licence is retained and replaced by a "citation slip (*boleta de citación del inculpado*)" that lets you drive 30 days (up to 90 with one extension); you must appear within 30 days to pay or defend yourself; if you do not appear in 90 days the retained licence is destroyed and the right to drive lapses until a new licence is issued; the licence is returned on payment or compliance with the decision (Ley 13.927 art 38).
In CABA, retention causes include (Ley 2148 art 5.6.1 b): expired or lapsed licence, alcohol above the limit, speeding more than 20 km/h over the limit, crossing a junction that was entered on red, riding a motorcycle without a correctly fastened helmet, using the exclusive Metrobus lane or the cycle path without authorisation, crossing a level crossing with the barriers down, and driving the wrong way or on the pavement; in the slip cases the *boleta de citación* lets you drive for up to 40 calendar days (art 5.6.2).
- **Why:** the slip is how PBA and CABA reconcile retention with the right to defence.
- **How:** if the licence is retained in PBA, keep the slip with you as the driving document, put the deadline in your calendar, and pay or defend within 30 days; the Mi Argentina app shows "retenida".
- **Verify:** the date on the slip and the cause box.
- **Don't:** keep driving after 90 days or throw away the slip.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 72 b (read 2026-11-15); Decreto 196/2025 art 18 (reg 72); Ley 13.927 PBA arts 38 and 38 bis (normas.gba.gob.ar, read 2026-11-15); Ley 2148 CABA arts 5.6.1-5.6.2 (juristeca h-tit-5, read 2026-11-15, the shorter slip terms for some causes were not copied).

### VEH-LEY-48: When the car can be stopped, held or towed
- **Rule:** the authority can retain the vehicle (art 72 c) when: it does not meet regulation safety requirements (provisional act: it is annulled if you show within 3 days that the fault was fixed; the retention lasts only the time to make the record unless the defect puts traffic safety in real danger, then until repaired) (c.1); it is driven by an unlicensed, disqualified or underage person (c.2: after the record it can be released under a licensed driver, otherwise it is towed (*acarreo*) and delivered to the owner after paying transport costs); it exceeds weight or dimension limits; it carries more occupants than its capacity (c.7: the vehicle may continue after the excess people get out); it is badly parked and obstructs or occupies emergency, bus or taxi spaces, is abandoned or broken and not repaired (c.5: towed, costs paid before pickup). Under the regulation (art 72 c, Decreto 196/2025) you can be stopped from continuing for missing or illegitimate documents "in the cases listed in art 40" and the vehicle can be removed from the road only if it cannot be driven by a licensed person because the driver is in the grave fault (*falta grave*) of art 77 m (intoxication); the costs of removal are charged to the registered owner or holder.
- **Why:** knowing the exact causes helps to refuse an unlawful tow.
- **How:** ask the cause in writing, take photos of where the car was, and ask where it is going; pay and recover it with the title card, ID and insurance; claim later if the cause was unlawful (fines-and-points.md).
- **Verify:** whether the cause is in art 72 c and whether the record names it.
- **Don't:** let a tow-truck driver (not an officer) decide; ask for the officer's record.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 72 c (read 2026-11-15); Decreto 196/2025 art 18 (reg 72 c). Tow fees and CABA/PBA depot procedures: not verified in a primary source.

### VEH-LEY-49: The alcohol test: you must take it, how it is done, what refusal means
- **Rule:** drivers must take the tests for detecting alcohol or other intoxication; in PBA anybody driving a motor vehicle must (Ley 13.927 art 39) and refusal or resistance is a grave fault (*falta grave*) with retention of the licence (*licencia de conducir*) and up to 18 months of disqualification; in CABA the driver must take the test and refusal is presumed a positive result, the agent must forbid him to continue driving and order the removal of the vehicle (Ley 2148 arts 5.4.2, 5.4.7). Nationally, the authority retains an in-flagrante intoxicated driver (reg art 72 a.1: evident intoxication, immediate retention not taking more than 30 minutes, with a record) for the time needed to recover the normal state, not more than 12 hours (art 72 a.1), based on a medical certificate or approved device. In an accident or at the driver's request the authority must take the test as soon as possible and ensure its proof (Ley 13.927 art 39).
- **Why:** the result must be reliable; the instruments must be certified and calibrated and the data must not be alterable by hand (Ley 13.927 art 28 ter; Ley 2148 art 5.4.3).
- **How:** blow as instructed; ask for the printed result; ask for a second test or a blood test where the law provides it (your right to counter-test is in the local regulation: not verified here); write down the device and time.
- **Verify:** the record shows the instrument, the result, the time and the legal cause.
- **Don't:** refuse (the penalty is as severe as the highest level) or drive off before the record is made.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art 72 a.1; Decreto 196/2025 art 18 (reg 72 a.1); Ley 13.927 PBA arts 28 ter, 39, 39 bis; Ley 2148 CABA arts 5.4.2-5.4.7 (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the right to a counter-test.

### VEH-LEY-50: After a crash: stop, secure the scene, exchange data, call for help
- **Rule:** all participants must stop immediately (art 65 a), in a safe place and without creating new risks (reg art 65 a as replaced by Decreto 196/2025); give the licence (*licencia de conducir*) and compulsory insurance (*seguro obligatorio*) data to the other party and the authority, and if they are not there attach those data firmly to the damaged vehicle (art 65 b); report the event to any authority (art 65 c); appear and give testimony when summoned (art 65 d). A traffic accident is any event that causes damage to persons or things as a consequence of circulation (art 64); the one who lacked priority or committed an infraction related to the cause is presumed responsible, without prejudice to the responsibility of those who could have avoided it (art 64). Not stopping or not giving the data is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 g, h). CABA adds the duty to ask for help for victims, not to alter the scene or the marks (Ley 2148 art 5.5.1).
- **Why:** leaving the scene is itself a grave fault and can have penal consequences, and losing the other party's data blocks the insurance claim.
- **How:** (1) hazard lights (*luces de emergencia*), engine off, triangles if safe, vest on; (2) check for injured: call 911 (national emergency line; 107 for medical emergencies in many jurisdictions); do not move seriously injured people unless there is fire danger; (3) take photos of positions, plates (*chapas patente*), damage, signs, skid marks before moving; (4) exchange name, licence, plate, insurer and policy proof (not insurance policy numbers on social media); (5) call your insurer (see insurance.md for deadlines).
- **Verify:** the other driver's licence status in the app and the plate on the card.
- **Don't:** admit fault in writing at the scene, accept cash "to settle" for injuries without advice, or move the vehicles if somebody is injured.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 64, 65, 77 g, h; Decreto 196/2025 art 17 (reg 65); Ley 2148 CABA art 5.5.1 (juristeca h-tit-5) (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the safety steps (general good practice).

### VEH-LEY-51: Accident record, report and the black-box data
- **Rule:** the authority makes an *acta de choque* with the data it verifies and the parties' statements, giving them the original and a copy (art 66 a); the insurer receives the claim on the basis of this record (art 68, fourth paragraph); for accidents with penal proceedings the authority makes the accident record sheet for statistics (art 66 b). The report, statement or crash form is made on the form established in Annex U where applicable (reg art 65 c as replaced in 2025). The investigating authority establishes the cause of the accident and not responsibilities (reg art 65 d). If the vehicle has an event data recorder, you must say so; when there are victims the authority seizes the support and the driver or companion must hand it over; in other cases you may deliver it voluntarily against a detailed receipt to preserve it as evidence (reg art 65 d).
- **Why:** the record is the base for the claim; the data recorder can help or hurt.
- **How:** ask the police or traffic authority for the *acta de choque* where they attended; if nobody came, complete the amicable form of your insurer and report to the police station or online where available; keep photos and witness contacts.
- **Verify:** the copy of the record has the plates (*chapas patente*), names, date and time; compare with your photos.
- **Don't:** hand over the data recorder without a receipt or let the other side's tow truck take the car before you have photos.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 66 a-b, 68; Decreto 196/2025 art 17 (reg 65 c-d) (read 2026-11-15). The insurer's deadlines and the amicable form are in insurance.md; the Anexo U form was not read (not verified in a primary source).

### VEH-LEY-52: Never leave the scene of an accident or flee a check
- **Rule:** fleeing after being involved in an accident or after committing certain serious infractions allows the authority to retain the driver for the time needed to draw up the police record, not more than 12 hours (art 72 a.2); fleeing, or refusing to give documents or information when obliged, is a grave fault (*falta grave*) (art 77 g), as is failing to do what the law requires after an accident (art 77 h). Where there are injured people, other penal rules may apply (the Penal Code; not covered here).
- **Why:** fleeing converts a property-damage matter into a criminal one and voids insurance cover in many policies (see insurance.md).
- **How:** if you feel threatened at the scene, stay in the car locked and call 911 for assistance; drive to the nearest police station only if told by the authority.
- **Verify:** ask the police to log the circumstances if you left to a safe place.
- **Don't:** drive away "to avoid a fight" without telling the police; call 911 from a safe place instead.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts 72 a.2, 77 g, h (read 2026-11-15). Penal consequences not verified in a primary source.
