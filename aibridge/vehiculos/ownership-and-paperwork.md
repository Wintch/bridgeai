# Owning and papering a vehicle in Argentina

For owners and buyers of private cars, many with old cars. Procedures, fees and tax rules change often and differ by province and municipality; every figure is a dated example with the official place to check, and several items below could only be confirmed in news or in summaries, which the Source line says. **Information, not legal advice**: for a purchase of real value, a registry clerk (*gestor* or *escribano*) or a lawyer should read the documents. Never hand over originals (title, cédula, ID) to a stranger.

## The registry and the cards

### VEH-DOC-1: Know which registry office holds your car and its file
- **Rule:** every car is registered in the national automotive property registry (*Registro Nacional de la Propiedad del Automotor*, run by the *DNRPA*) and its file (*legajo*) is kept at one local branch (*registro seccional*), the one of the vehicle's registered location (*radicación*). Procedures that change the registry record happen there.
- **Why:** you need the right branch for a transfer, a duplicate of a document or an address change, and the branch decides where tax and fines are tied.
- **How:** (1) look at the title or the cédula: the branch is printed on it; (2) the official service "Dónde está radicado un vehículo" (argentina.gob.ar/consultar-donde-esta-radicado-un-vehiculo) gives it from the plate; (3) the file can be consulted at the branch (service "Consultar el legajo del automotor"); (4) the registry's online portal is run by the Ministerio de Justicia (jus.gob.ar and argentina.gob.ar/justicia/registro-automotor).
- **Verify:** the branch number and city on the cédula and the online answer match.
- **Don't:** go to a random branch for a procedure that must be filed at the branch of registration (some can be filed at another branch: ask).
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar/tema/automotor (list of registry services, read 2026-11-15); Régimen Jurídico del Automotor Decreto-Ley 6582/58 (cited as the governing law in DNRPA Disp. 161/2026, read 2026-11-15; the Decreto-Ley itself not read).

### VEH-DOC-2: Carry the documents the law asks for
- **Rule:** to drive you must carry: a licence valid for the vehicle type (art. 40 a), the vehicle's registration card, expired or not (*cédula*, art. 40 b), the proof of valid insurance (art. 40 c), the plates on the car (art. 40 d), a fire extinguisher and portable warning triangles (art. 40 f). Since Decreto 196/2025 the documents may be shown in digital or paper form, and the inspection certificate is also a document to carry.
- **Why:** missing documents are an infraction (art. 77 e) and can lead to retention of the vehicle or licence (art. 72).
- **How:** (1) licence and cédula in the Mi Argentina app and on paper; (2) proof of insurance on phone and paper (insurance.md); (3) inspection (VTV) certificate or sticker in date; (4) fire extinguisher (*matafuego*) with valid date and two triangles (*balizas*); (5) if you drive someone else's car, the owner's authorisation (VEH-DOC-4); (6) keep the title at home, not in the car.
- **Verify:** open each digital document at home with the phone offline to see whether it still shows; check dates of the extinguisher and sticker.
- **Don't:** leave the title in the glovebox: if the car is stolen the thief has the title.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts. 40, 72, 77 (text read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: Decreto 196/2025 art. 14 (art. 37 text, digital or paper documents), summarised in a legal-news note and a Boletín Oficial summary page, read 2026-11-15; text not read.

### VEH-DOC-3: Use the registration card (cédula verde), on paper or in Mi Argentina
- **Rule:** the owner's registration card (*cédula verde*, formally *cédula de identificación del automotor*) does not expire for the owner. A digital version in the Mi Argentina app (*cédula digital*) has the same validity as the physical one (Disposición Conjunta 1/2019, per the official page). The code on the digital card refreshes every 24 hours and lets an inspector check its status.
- **Why:** the card proves the car's identity, the owner and the registry data; the app makes it hard to lose.
- **How:** (1) physical card required first; (2) install Mi Argentina, create the account and validate identity; (3) open "Mis vehículos" and the cédula; (4) show the screen and the code when asked; (5) keep the physical card as backup.
- **Verify:** the plate, owner name and data on the app match the physical card.
- **Don't:** rely on a screenshot: show the live screen with the refreshing code.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar/miargentina/servicios/cedulas-de-tu-vehiculo-digital (read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar service "Obtener un duplicado de la cédula del vehículo" (no expiry for the owner; read 2026-11-15); Ley 24.449 art. 40 b (the card is required "vencida o no"; read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-DOC-4: Authorising another driver: the blue card (cédula azul) and what replaced it
- **Rule:** the paper blue card (*cédula azul*, the card for an authorised driver) was repealed in 2024 (DNRPA Disposición 29/2024, per the official news item "La cédula azul ya no es documento exigible"): blue cards already issued stay valid until revoked by whoever requested them. The owner now authorises another person from the Mi Argentina app: "Mis vehículos", choose the plate, "Habilitar a conducir" on the current cédula, enter the person's name and CUIT, CUIL or CDI (Disposición 343/2024). The authorised person must have the "Cédula automotor" service enabled in his or her own profile to see the vehicle's green card.
- **Why:** driving someone else's car without proper authorisation can void insurance arguments and cause problems at checks; the authorisation does not change the owner's civil liability for damage.
- **How:** (1) the owner opens Mi Argentina and authorises the driver; (2) to revoke, open the list of authorised persons and deactivate; (3) for company fleets there is a separate registry of authorised drivers (RAC); (4) old blue cards: revoke those you no longer want (official service "Revocar o dar de baja la cédula para autorizado a conducir"); (5) if a person without the app must drive, ask the registry whether the physical route (service "Tramitar una cédula para autorizado a conducir") is still available: the official page still described it when read.
- **Verify:** the authorised driver sees the vehicle's cédula in his or her own app.
- **Don't:** lend the car on the idea that "the blue card is gone, so anyone can drive": the owner stays civilly liable (VEH-DOC-2 and insurance.md).
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar/noticias/la-cedula-azul-ya-no-es-documento-exigible-para-la-circulacion-de-vehiculos-en-el-pais and Disposición 343/2024 (Boletín Oficial 2024-11-27), both located in a search on 2026-11-15; the 343/2024 text was not read, only secondary and official summaries. Not verified in a primary source: whether the physical route is still offered.

### VEH-DOC-5: Replace a lost, stolen or damaged cédula
- **Rule:** if the cédula is lost, stolen or so deteriorated that it looks altered, request a duplicate (*duplicado de cédula*) at the registry branch where the car is registered; it takes 24 hours and you collect the new one the next day.
- **Why:** the card is needed to drive and to transfer the car.
- **How:** (1) either complete the online request on the DNRPA site ("duplicado de cédula") and pay fees online, then bring documents to the branch and sign the printed request (Solicitud Tipo TP); or go in person, pay at the branch, fill Form 02; (2) bring ID (DNI; the "documento en trámite" receipt does not prove identity) and the title; (3) if someone else does it, a power of attorney and Form 59; (4) if a physical verification of the vehicle is required, do it at an authorised plant first; (5) the fee depends on car or motorcycle: see the DNRPA fee schedule (amounts not listed on the service page).
- **Verify:** the new card is in your hands and in the app.
- **Don't:** drive for months with a photocopy: the card is a required document.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Obtener un duplicado de la cédula del vehículo" (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-DOC-6: Guard the title (título del automotor) and replace it correctly if lost
- **Rule:** the title of the vehicle (*título del automotor*) proves ownership; it has no expiry. If lost or stolen, request a duplicate (*duplicado de título*); the duplicate requires a physical verification of the vehicle for loss or theft (not for deterioration). The verification form (Solicitud Tipo 12) is valid 150 business days.
- **Why:** you need the title to sell the car; a stolen title is a tool for fraud.
- **How:** (1) do the physical verification at an authorised plant (loss or theft); (2) either do the online request ("duplicado de título"), pay fees online and finish at the branch, or in person pay and fill Form 02 (and Form 59 if a third party does it); (3) collect the duplicate the next day or download it from the site; (4) bring ID and, for a damaged title, the damaged original.
- **Verify:** the duplicate carries your name and the right plate; the registry confirms the file shows the duplicate.
- **Don't:** accept a seller's "I lost the title, the photocopy is enough": a duplicate must be issued to the owner first.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Obtener duplicado del título del automotor" (read 2026-11-15); the service page does not mention a police report as a requirement, but a police report is useful when the title is stolen.

### VEH-DOC-7: Plates (chapa patente): keep them legible and replace lost ones through the registry
- **Rule:** the plates must be fitted as the regulation says, legible and standard, without additions (Ley 24.449 art. 40 d). Cars (category M) and light trucks (category N) carry front and rear plates; trailers (category O) rear only (Decreto 196/2025 as reported). Lost or stolen plates are replaced through the registry service "Obtener un duplicado de la chapa patente del automotor".
- **Why:** a car without legible plates is an infraction and attracts fines on the wrong car; stolen plates are used for cloning.
- **How:** (1) if a plate is stolen file a police report; (2) request the duplicate at the branch; (3) never drive without both plates; (4) keep a photo of the plate numbers.
- **Verify:** both plates match the cédula; no stickers or frames cover the characters.
- **Don't:** use a handmade plate.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 40 d (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: Decreto 196/2025 plate rule (legal-news summary, read 2026-11-15); the duplicate-plate service page (listed on argentina.gob.ar/tema/automotor, not opened).

## Buying and selling

### VEH-DOC-8: Order a vehicle-status report (informe de dominio) before paying
- **Rule:** before buying, request the vehicle-status report (*informe de dominio*) from the registry: it shows registration details, theft reports, seizures (*embargos*), pledges (*prendas*), the owner's inhibitions and the vehicle's characteristics (make, model, engine and chassis numbers, first registration year, use).
- **Why:** a car with a seizure, a pledge or a theft report cannot be safely bought.
- **How:** (1) online: ask for "Informes de dominio e históricos" at the Ministerio de Justicia site (jus.gob.ar), pay online, receive the report by email within 24 hours; (2) in person: Form 02 at the vehicle's branch or Form 57 at another branch: 48 hours or up to 7 business days; (3) for the same day, the urgent version; (4) the historical report lists past owners; (5) any adult with an ID can ask for it, but the "certificado de dominio" can only be requested by the owner or a court (the buyer asks the seller for it).
- **Verify:** the report's owner, plate, engine and chassis match the title and the car.
- **Don't:** rely on a photo of a report from the seller; order your own.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Solicitar un informe de dominio del automotor" (read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar service "Hacer la transferencia de dominio de un auto, moto o maquinaria" (who can request which report; read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-DOC-9: Do the physical police verification (verificación policial), which is not the VTV
- **Rule:** for a transfer, the car must go through a physical verification at an authorised plant (*verificación física policial*), which checks that the engine and chassis numbers match the documents. It is not the technical inspection (VTV). It produces the Solicitud Tipo 12 verification, also emailed.
- **Why:** it detects altered or cloned numbers; it is done after the vehicle-status report.
- **How:** (1) get the status report first; (2) book at an authorised plant (the official page speaks of plants of the Federal Police; check the DNRPA site for the plants in your area, their schedule and fee); (3) bring the car, the title and the cédula; (4) keep the Solicitud Tipo 12 for the branch.
- **Verify:** the verification shows no discrepancy with the title.
- **Don't:** buy a car whose numbers do not match the documents, even for a "good price".
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Hacer la transferencia de dominio de un auto, moto o maquinaria" (step 2; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: whether a verification is required at every transfer (rules on when it is required were amended over time; check the DNRPA digest).

### VEH-DOC-10: Check an old car before buying: a short list
- **Rule:** before paying for an old car check: status report; the numbers on the car against the title (engine, chassis); the seller's ID matches the title; whether the car has pledges, seizures or fines; the inspection certificate and its date; the insurance and who pays the next period; whether the car's registered branch is far away.
- **Why:** most bad deals with old cars are about papers, not about the engine.
- **How:** (1) ask for the title, the cédula (all of them: green and blue) and the seller's ID; (2) compare the seller's name with the title: if the title is in another name, do not buy without a sworn authorisation by the registered owner and a lawyer's advice; (3) check the plate in the fines databases of every jurisdiction where the car lived (fines-and-points.md); (4) check the payments of the patente tax (VEH-DOC-13); (5) have the car checked mechanically (car-maintenance-basics.md); (6) see VEH-DOC-11 for the transfer.
- **Verify:** all checks written on one sheet and signed copies of the documents kept.
- **Don't:** accept "the papers come later" or a car "sold by a friend of the owner".
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Hacer la transferencia de dominio de un auto, moto o maquinaria" (documents: title, all cédulas; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: market practice items (sworn authorisation, mechanical check) are common advice and not rules.

### VEH-DOC-11: Transfer the title step by step (formulario 08)
- **Rule:** a transfer by sale, succession or donation is registered at a branch by both parties. Complete it within 30 days of signing the transfer form (*formulario 08*); the registry takes about 48 hours if there are no observations.
- **Why:** until the transfer is registered, the seller remains the registered owner and responsible; after the 30 days, if a sale was reported, a circulation ban is decreed.
- **How:** (1) status report (VEH-DOC-8); (2) physical verification (VEH-DOC-9); (3) choose a branch (the current one or one closer to the buyer's address or garage); (4) fill Form 08 online to pre-load data (optional but faster); (5) seller and buyer go to the branch with: ID of both (and of spouses if the asset is joint property), the buyer's CUIT, CUIL or CDI, the title (or CAT), all the cédulas, Form 08, the verification; if the car is pledged or leased, proof of notification to the creditor; (6) pay fees at the branch and complete Form 13; (7) receive the transfer receipt. If the price exceeds ARS 145,494,290 (example as of 2026-11-15; check the registry page), the buyer must be registered with the UIF (financial intelligence unit) to verify the origin of the money.
- **Verify:** the receipt or new title and cédula are in the buyer's name; the seller gets the registry's confirmation.
- **Don't:** sign Form 08 and hand over the car without going to the branch within the deadline; do not accept "I will transfer it later" from a buyer.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Hacer la transferencia de dominio de un auto, moto o maquinaria" (read 2026-11-15). The page states "up to 30 days" in one section and "30 business days" in another: confirm with the branch.

### VEH-DOC-12: Budget the transfer cost, with dated figures
- **Rule:** the registry fee for a transfer is a percentage of the vehicle's value: 1.5% of the total value if of national origin, 2% if imported (official page). It includes signature certification per person, title, cédula and authorised-driver cards, tax registration and de-registration, debt, fines and infractions request, and Forms 13 and 08. Provincial stamp taxes and other charges may add to it.
- **Why:** old cars have a low value, so the fee is small, but stamp taxes and debts can change the total.
- **How:** (1) use the registry's cost estimator (DNRPA site) with the vehicle's valuation; (2) ask the branch what it charges as minimums; (3) agree in the sale contract who pays which fees; (4) example: for a car valued (invented) at ARS 6,000,000 and national origin, 1.5% is ARS 90,000, plus stamp tax and extra items: check the real quote.
- **Verify:** the branch's printed fee breakdown.
- **Don't:** pay a "gestor" without a written quote.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Hacer la transferencia de dominio de un auto, moto o maquinaria" (costs; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the amount of provincial stamp taxes (the page does not list them).

### VEH-DOC-13: Clear the patente and fines debts (libre deuda) before selling or buying
- **Rule:** the transfer includes a request of debts, fines and infractions (*solicitud de deuda, multa e infracción*), and both sides should know what is owed before signing. Ask the seller for a clean patente tax statement (*libre deuda de patentes*) from the authority that charges the tax for that car (VEH-DOC-19) and check fines in every jurisdiction where it ran.
- **Why:** a debt can follow the car; a seller with debts is a seller who may not finish the transfer.
- **How:** (1) PBA: ask ARBA for the debt statement (for models 2016 and newer) or the municipality (for models 1990 to 2015); (2) CABA: ask AGIP; (3) fines: see fines-and-points.md (national, PBA and CABA consultations); (4) agree in the contract that debts to the sale date are the seller's and that an amount is retained until the statements arrive.
- **Verify:** statements dated within a few days of the sale and printed.
- **Don't:** assume "the registry cleans it".
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Hacer la transferencia de dominio de un auto, moto o maquinaria" (the fee covers the debt and fines request; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: whether tax debt blocks the transfer or travels with the car in each province and municipality.

### VEH-DOC-14: Sellers: file the sale report (denuncia de venta) at once
- **Rule:** if you sell the car and the buyer does not transfer it with you at the branch, file a sale report (*denuncia de venta*) with the registry. It releases you from any civil liability for the vehicle from that moment, notifies the buyer that the transfer must be done within 30 business days, and if not done the registry emails you and the car is banned from circulating and can be seized. The report stays valid until you recover the car.
- **Why:** without the report the registered owner can be sued for the new owner's accidents and receive his or her fines and tax bills.
- **How:** (1) have: plate, your email, the buyer's name and ID (or company and CUIT), the buyer's address or a statement that you do not know it, place and date of delivery (approximate is allowed), any proof of sale; (2) file online on the DNRPA site ("Denuncia de venta"), pay with your own CBU (only for individuals with a single owner), and receive the certificate by email; (3) otherwise go to the branch with an appointment: more than one owner or a company must go in person; (4) keep the certificate; (5) it takes about 48 hours.
- **Verify:** the certificate in email; the registry confirms the file shows it.
- **Don't:** sell with only a signed Form 08 in your drawer and "wait for the buyer to call".
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Denunciar la venta de un automotor" (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the article of the Régimen Jurídico del Automotor (Decreto-Ley 6582/58) that frees the seller; the amounts of the fees (the page gives none).

### VEH-DOC-15: Sell an old car step by step
- **Rule:** follow an order: documents, price, contract, payment, transfer or sale report, insurance, patente, handover.
- **Why:** a 1990s car is often sold between private persons with old papers, and the mistakes that cost money are always in the order of the steps.
- **How:** (1) gather: title, all the cédulas, the keys, the inspection certificate, the receipts; replace any lost document (VEH-DOC-5, VEH-DOC-6); (2) check fines and tax debts and pay them or agree who pays (VEH-DOC-13); (3) order your own status report to show the buyer a clean file (VEH-DOC-8); (4) write a sale contract with the plate, the numbers, the price, the date and hour of delivery, who pays debts and fees, signatures and copies of IDs; (5) take payment by bank transfer or a certified instrument that clears before handover; avoid large cash sums; (6) go with the buyer to the branch the same day, sign Form 08 and complete the transfer (VEH-DOC-11); if that is not possible, file the denuncia de venta that day (VEH-DOC-14); (7) tell the insurer after handover and cancel the policy as in insurance.md; (8) tell the municipality or tax office of the sale; (9) keep copies of everything for years.
- **Verify:** at the end you hold the transfer receipt or the sale-report certificate, the signed contract and the proof of payment.
- **Don't:** hand over the car, the keys and the documents before the money is credited.
- **Source:** composed from VEH-DOC-8 to VEH-DOC-14 (their sources); step (5) and (8) are common practice, not verified in a primary source.

## Addresses

### VEH-DOC-16: Change the owner's address at the registry when you move
- **Rule:** if the owner's address changes, register the new one (*cambio de domicilio del titular*) at the branch where the car is registered. The official page states no deadline.
- **Why:** official notices (fines, inspection reminders, the registry's emails) go to the registered address.
- **How:** (1) semi-in-person: complete the online request on the DNRPA site ("Cambio de domicilio"), then go to the branch, pay and sign the printed form (Solicitud Tipo TP; Form 59 if a third party does it); or in person at the branch (Form 04, free if you certify the signature at the branch and file at the same time); (2) bring ID, title, cédula, and proof of the new address (an ID with the new address, or a utility bill, lease or deed in your name); (3) for a car with several owners, owners of more than 50% must consent; (4) the fee depends on car or motorcycle (DNRPA fee schedule).
- **Verify:** the branch confirms the processing; your Mi Argentina record shows the new address.
- **Don't:** assume the licence address update changed the vehicle record: they are separate.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Cambiar el domicilio del titular de un automotor" (read 2026-11-15). The page does not distinguish a change of address from a change of registered location (*radicación*) and does not mention penalties for not doing it.

### VEH-DOC-17: Keep addresses and notices straight
- **Rule:** the insurer's risk address (where the car is kept), the registry's address for the owner and the address the tax authorities use can all differ from where you live. Fix all three and check the plate regularly.
- **Why:** a fine notice sent to an old address can pass unseen and the defence deadline can expire.
- **How:** (1) update the registry (VEH-DOC-16) and tell the insurer the real place the car is kept (insurance.md); (2) check the plate for fines in every jurisdiction every couple of months (fines-and-points.md); (3) keep a copy of any notice you receive with the date you got it.
- **Verify:** a plate search shows no fine you did not know.
- **Don't:** wait for the letter.
- **Source:** earlier owner page of the sibling repository (section "Keep the addresses straight", carried over; read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar service "Cambiar el domicilio del titular de un automotor" (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-DOC-18: Moving the car to another city or province changes who charges the tax
- **Rule:** the car's registered location (*radicación*) determines which tax authority charges the patente and which registry branch holds its file. If you move to another jurisdiction, ask the registry about the procedure to move the car's registration and ask the tax office how the tax and fines will be handled.
- **Why:** a tax bill for the wrong jurisdiction or a car "registered" in a place you left can create debts and wrong notices.
- **How:** (1) ask the branch where the car is registered what the procedure and fees are; (2) the registry has a service "Dónde radicar un automotor" (argentina.gob.ar/propiedadautomotor/consultar-donde-radicar-un-automotor) that says where a car can be registered; (3) before moving, clear debts and fines of the old jurisdiction; (4) afterwards, check the plate in the new one.
- **Verify:** the new branch shows the car in its file; the tax authority has the car in its system.
- **Don't:** keep the old branch's address when you live elsewhere.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: the procedure and fee of a change of registered location (only the service names were read in argentina.gob.ar/tema/automotor, 2026-11-15; ask the registry).

## Vehicle tax (patente)

### VEH-DOC-19: Know who charges the patente on your car
- **Rule:** the vehicle tax (*impuesto a los automotores*, called *patente*) is charged by the province or the city of registration. In PBA, ARBA charges models of recent years and, by law each year, the tax of older model years is transferred to the municipalities. For 2026, Ley 15.558 art. 34 transfers to the municipalities the tax of model years 1990 to 2015: a 1994 car's patente is therefore charged by the municipality where the car is registered, not by ARBA. In CABA, AGIP charges it.
- **Why:** asking ARBA about a 1994 car sends you to the wrong office; each municipality has its own rules, discounts and exemptions.
- **How:** (1) PBA: model years 2016 to 2026 are in the ARBA scales (art. 33); ask your municipality for the model years 1990 to 2015; (2) the amount is computed from the vehicle's value or the formula in each municipality's rules; (3) check your municipality's tax office site or office for the bill, due dates and installments; (4) CABA: AGIP (buenosaires.gob.ar/agip). ARBA's 2026 patente is paid in monthly installments from March 2026 (news source).
- **Verify:** you hold a bill or a "no tax due" statement from the right authority.
- **Don't:** assume that being old means being free: the exemption depends on the jurisdiction (VEH-DOC-20, VEH-DOC-21).
- **Source:** Ley 15.558 (Ley Impositiva PBA 2026) arts. 33-34 (read at arba.gov.ar/archivos/Publicaciones/leyimpositiva2026.pdf, 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the monthly-installment regime of 2026 (news notes, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-DOC-20: Old-car exemption in PBA: ask your municipality; no provincial 25-year rule confirmed
- **Rule:** the exemption of old cars from the patente in the province of Buenos Aires depends on the municipality that charges it for model years 1990 to 2015 (VEH-DOC-19). Several news notes say that PBA exempts cars of 25 years or more or model year 2000 or earlier; these notes cite no rule and no official ARBA page confirmed it, so treat the rule as unconfirmed and ask the municipality.
- **Why:** an exemption you do not claim is not applied; an exemption you assume that does not exist leaves a debt.
- **How:** (1) call or visit the municipal tax office and ask: "Is a model-year 1994 car exempt or discounted? What do I have to file?"; (2) ask for the answer in writing; (3) if an exemption exists, ask whether it is automatic or needs a request; (4) the municipal fiscal ordinance is the legal basis; (5) the Código Fiscal of PBA (Ley 10.397) art. 243 lists the provincial exemptions of the automotive tax for the cars ARBA charges (ARBA states that its exemptions are regulated by that article, per a search result of 2026-11-15; the article text was not read).
- **Verify:** the written answer or the municipal bill shows zero or the discount.
- **Don't:** stop paying because a news note says "old cars are exempt".
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: age-based exemptions (news notes, some saying 25 years, some model year 2000 or earlier; one note says one PBA municipality applies 100%; none cites the rule; read 2026-11-15). Ley 15.558 art. 34 (read 2026-11-15) fixes only which model years are municipal.

### VEH-DOC-21: Old-car exemption in CABA: 25 years reported, a proposal for 20 years from 2027
- **Rule:** AGIP is reported to exempt from the patente cars with 25 years of age or more (cars of model year 2000 or earlier in 2026; sources differ on "25 or more" versus "more than 25"). A city proposal for the 2027 budget would lower the threshold to 20 years, subject to approval in the city budget law.
- **Why:** if the car's registered location is the City, a 1994 car should be exempt in 2026.
- **How:** (1) check your car's status in the AGIP vehicle tax page (buenosaires.gob.ar/agip, patentes); (2) if you are still charged, ask AGIP how to claim the exemption in writing; (3) the exemptions are in art. 433 of the Código Fiscal of the City.
- **Verify:** the AGIP bill shows zero for the car or the exemption code.
- **Don't:** assume the 2027 change applies in 2026.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: the threshold of 25 years (news notes of 2026, one quoting AGIP; the text of art. 433 was not read) and the 2027 proposal (iProfesional, 2026-09-24, depending on the budget law).

## Technical inspection (VTV)

### VEH-DOC-22: The framework: a national rule, applied by each province
- **Rule:** all motor vehicles that circulate on public roads must pass periodic technical inspection (*revisión técnica obligatoria*, *RTO*, or *VTV* in common usage) of safety and emission systems (Ley 24.449 art. 34). The regulation Decreto 779/95 was modified by Decreto 196/2025: reported rules are a 60-month grace after first registration for new private cars, a validity of 24 months for private vehicles up to 10 years old and 12 months for older ones (jurisdictions may set longer periods for private cars), and results "apto", "condicional" and "rechazado".
- **Why:** a province can be stricter or looser than the national framework in how it applies the rule; you need the rule of the province where the car is registered.
- **How:** (1) read your province's rule (VEH-DOC-23 for PBA, VEH-DOC-28 for CABA); (2) the national decree says a unique plate-searchable database must exist within a year of the decree to verify inspections; (3) never rely on a general figure without the province's page.
- **Verify:** the inspection sticker and the certificate show the date of validity.
- **Don't:** apply the national 24 or 12 months blindly to PBA or CABA.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 34 (read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the content of Decreto 196/2025 (legal-news summaries of 2025-03, read 2026-11-15; the text was not read).

### VEH-DOC-23: VTV in PBA: when and how often
- **Rule:** in the province of Buenos Aires, a private car's first inspection is due after 2 years from registration; then, according to the provincial site, annually between 1 and 20 years of age and every six months from 20 years. Each plate ending has a fixed month; you can book in that month or up to 30 days before.
- **Why:** an old car is checked more often; missing the month means driving with an expired VTV (VEH-DOC-27).
- **How:** (1) month by last plate digit: 1 November, 2 February, 3 March, 4 April, 5 May, 6 June, 7 July, 8 August, 9 September, 0 October; (2) example for a plate ending 7: inspection month is July; booking opens up to 30 days earlier; (3) check the "Plazos" page of vtv.gba.gob.ar for the current rule; (4) for a 1994 car, plan for a possible six-month cycle and ask the plant when your next date is.
- **Verify:** the sticker on the windscreen and the certificate show the next date; the plate's month matches the table.
- **Don't:** assume the national 24-month rule applies to your 1994 car in PBA.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: frequency by age (the official "Plazos" and FAQ pages at vtv.gba.gob.ar could not be read directly; their content was seen in search summaries on 2026-11-15; they say 1 to 20 years annual, 20 or more six-monthly, and the first inspection at 2 years); the decree that sets the maximum interval between inspections (Decreto 532/09 under Ley 13.927, cited by a municipal bulletin, not read). Confirm on the official page, which may have changed to follow Decreto 196/2025.

### VEH-DOC-24: VTV fee in PBA, as a dated example, and the discounts
- **Rule:** the basic VTV fee in the province is set by a ministerial resolution and is updated several times a year. Example as of 2026-11-15: ARS 114,105.99 with IVA for a private car up to 2,500 kg, from 2026-10-15 (Res. 361/2026 of the provincial Ministerio de Transporte; the previous basic fee was ARS 97,057.65 from 2026-01-16). Check vtv.gba.gob.ar for the current fee.
- **Why:** the fee changes; budget it as a recurring cost, and ask about discounts.
- **How:** (1) the fee is paid when booking, on the official site, in prepaid plants; hybrid plants also allow payment on the spot; (2) discounts reported by the press: 50% for cars older than 20 years (limited and needing prior approval, reportedly one vehicle per year), 50% for retired and pensioners with income up to two minimum pensions, 100% for people with disability who meet the requirements (reported need of the disability certificate); (3) check the exact conditions and the dates of campaigns on the provincial site before booking.
- **Verify:** the booking confirmation shows the amount and the discount.
- **Don't:** pay a "gestor" extra to get the appointment.
- **Source:** Resolución 361/2026 (RESO-2026-361-GDEBA-MTRAGP, dated 2026-10-14, article 1: basic tariff ARS 114,105.99 IVA included, effective 2026-10-15; read at normas.gba.gob.ar/documentos/Bg6OMXC3.pdf, 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the previous fee and the discounts (iProfesional, 2026-10-26, and a search summary of the provincial site's front page; discount conditions are not confirmed).

### VEH-DOC-25: Book the PBA VTV and bring the right things
- **Rule:** book the appointment on the provincial portal (portal.vtv.gba.gob.ar): enter the plate and vehicle type, choose a plant, date and hour, and pay as the plant requires.
- **Why:** the plants work by appointment; showing up without one wastes a day.
- **How:** (1) enter the plate and the contact data; (2) pick a plant within your zone; (3) pay online; (4) bring: the card (cédula or digital), the licence of the person driving, the proof of valid insurance, the previous inspection certificate if any, the fire extinguisher and triangles, a clean car with the lights working; (5) go early.
- **Verify:** the booking email with date, hour, plant and amount.
- **Don't:** go without insurance proof: the plant may refuse to inspect.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: booking steps and required items (news and search summaries of the provincial site, read 2026-11-15; the portal could not be read directly).

### VEH-DOC-26: What is checked, the results and the re-inspection
- **Rule:** the inspection checks the systems that make the car safe and its emissions: lights, brakes, steering and suspension, tyres, chassis and body condition, seat belts, windows and mirrors, emissions. The result is "apto" (pass), "condicional" (pass with defects to fix) or "rechazado" (fail). After a conditional result, the new inspection must be done within 60 days for private cars and 30 days for non-private ones, according to a summary of Decreto 196/2025.
- **Why:** old cars fail on worn parts, leaks, lights and rust; knowing the list lets you fix them before paying.
- **How:** (1) before the date check lights (high, low, brake, turn, reverse), horn, wipers, tyres (tread and wear), brake pedal travel and leaks, handbrake, play in steering, exhaust leaks and smoke, seat belts, mirrors, plates; (2) fix cheap items (bulbs, wipers) yourself: for repairs on brakes, steering or fuel parts use a professional workshop (car-maintenance-basics.md); (3) if the result is conditional or rejected, read the report, fix the listed defects and book the re-inspection within the deadline the plant gives; (4) keep the report.
- **Verify:** certificate and sticker with the new date.
- **Don't:** work under a car held only by a jack to "check" it before the inspection.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 34 (systems of active and passive safety and emissions; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the exact checklist and re-inspection deadline of PBA plants (Decreto 196/2025 summaries, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-DOC-27: Do not drive with an expired VTV; a booked appointment is a tolerance, not a right
- **Rule:** circulating with an expired inspection is an infraction with no grace period; the vehicle can be retained if it does not meet safety requirements (Ley 24.449 art. 72 c) and an insurer may argue it in a claim: read your policy. Some people report that showing proof of a booked appointment is tolerated at a stop: treat it as a tolerance, not a right.
- **Why:** a fine and a retention are the lighter risks; an insurer's refusal after an accident is the heavy one.
- **How:** (1) book the appointment before the month ends; (2) if you must drive to the plant with an expired VTV, carry the printed appointment proof and drive only the direct route; (3) when the car has been parked long, check insurance too (insurance.md); (4) see fines-and-points.md for the fine.
- **Verify:** the next due month is on a calendar.
- **Don't:** drive with an expired sticker "just for a week".
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts. 34, 68 and 72 (read 2026-11-15); earlier owner page of the sibling repository (tolerance of a booked appointment, press-reported; carried over). Not verified in a primary source: how insurers treat an expired VTV in a claim (read the policy; ask the insurer).

### VEH-DOC-28: VTV in CABA: the regime is changing in 2026
- **Rule:** the City of Buenos Aires has its own inspection regime and in 2026 it is being reformed (private workshops, new fees, periods); the figures reported by the press during 2026 for a private car range from about ARS 63,000 in March to about ARS 100,000 later in the year. Check the City's official page before booking.
- **Why:** you need the City's rule if the car is registered in CABA; the PBA rules do not apply.
- **How:** (1) search "VTV Ciudad" in buenosaires.gob.ar and use the official booking; (2) read the period of validity for your car's age; (3) compare with PBA if the car could be registered there (VEH-DOC-18); (4) ask about discounts for old cars.
- **Verify:** the booking confirmation from the City's site.
- **Don't:** use a PBA appointment for a CABA car.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: all CABA figures and the reform (news notes of 2026, some contradictory; the official City page was not read; Res. 32/2026 of the City's transport secretariat is cited by one note).

### VEH-DOC-29: National RTO: national routes, commercial and interjurisdictional vehicles
- **Rule:** the national transport authority is the application authority of the RTO system under Decreto 196/2025 (the Secretaría de Transporte); vehicles that are not private (cargo, passengers, interjurisdictional service) have a validity of at most 12 months and a first-inspection grace of at most 12 months from registration. The RTO may be done in any enabled workshop regardless of the jurisdiction of registration.
- **Why:** a private car does not follow this regime, but a car used for hire, delivery or cargo may.
- **How:** (1) if the car is used for commercial or passenger service, ask the transport authority of your jurisdiction and the national authority which RTO applies; (2) keep the digital and physical certificate; (3) a car that circulates in more than one province for work needs the RTO of the national regime.
- **Verify:** the certificate states the category and date.
- **Don't:** use a private-car inspection for commercial use.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: Decreto 196/2025 art. 12 (legal-news summaries of 2025-03, read 2026-11-15; the text was not read).

## Driving licence

### VEH-DOC-30: Licence validity and how to renew it
- **Rule:** the national driving licence (*licencia de conducir*, *Licencia Nacional de Conducir*) is valid up to 5 years; at each renewal the holder must pass a medical (psychophysical) exam and, if the holder has infractions on record, redo the theory and practice exams; from 65 the validity is shorter (Ley 24.449 art. 13). Reported rules of Decreto 196/2025: from 65, classes A, B and G renew every 3 years; from 70, annually. The licence is digital by default (the paper copy has no expiry and is valid while the digital one is).
- **Why:** driving with an expired licence is an infraction and the licence can be retained (art. 72 b); driving without a licence is serious (art. 77 d) and voids the mandatory insurance (insurance.md).
- **How:** (1) renew online (argentina.gob.ar/servicio/renovacion-yo-ampliacion-de-la-licencia-nacional-de-conducir; lncargentina.seguridadvial.gob.ar): active Mi Argentina account; validate data; choose renewal; pay the bills; choose the providers for the course and the medical exam, who upload the results; the digital licence appears in Mi Argentina; (2) the physical card is requested at the licence-issuing centre of your jurisdiction; (3) ordinary cars are class B (with subclasses, e.g. B1 in the earlier owner page); professional work (taxi, delivery by car, trucks) uses other classes with other requirements and fees; (4) renew before the expiry date.
- **Verify:** the digital licence in the app shows the new expiry.
- **Don't:** wait for the expiry date to book the medical exam.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 arts. 13, 14, 72, 77 (text read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar service "Renovar y/o ampliar la Licencia Nacional de Conducir" (read 2026-11-15; no costs or validity by age on that page). Not verified in a primary source: the age rules of Decreto 196/2025 (legal-news summaries, read 2026-11-15); the class B1 label (earlier owner page).

### VEH-DOC-31: The traffic-record certificate (CENAT) and a licence blocked by fines
- **Rule:** the national traffic-record certificate (*Certificado Nacional de Antecedentes de Tránsito*, *CENAT*) gathers traffic records from national, provincial and municipal bodies; the licence issuer must ask the national traffic-record registry for reports before granting a licence (Ley 24.449 art. 14). Unpaid fines can block a renewal.
- **Why:** you cannot renew if the issuer sees open fines.
- **How:** (1) check your fines for free (argentina.gob.ar/servicio/consultar-si-tenes-infracciones-de-transito with your DNI) and in the provincial and municipal sites (fines-and-points.md); (2) pay or contest them; (3) the CENAT payment slip is generated on the national road-safety agency's slip site (boletadepago.seguridadvial.gob.ar) by choosing the province; the proof of payment is uploaded in the licence system; it is used in the jurisdiction where it was requested; (4) its validity (reported as 60 days) and cost change: read the slip.
- **Verify:** the fines search shows none; the licence process accepts the CENAT slip.
- **Don't:** pay a "tramitador" to "clean" fines; pay only through official channels.
- **Source:** Ley 24.449 art. 14 (last sentence; read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar/seguridadvial/licencianacional (index page naming the CENAT service; read 2026-11-15). Not verified in a primary source: the cost and the validity of the CENAT (secondary notes, read 2026-11-15); that every unpaid fine blocks a renewal (secondary source in the earlier owner page).

### VEH-DOC-32: Lost or stolen licence
- **Rule:** if the licence is lost or stolen, request a duplicate through the licence system; the digital licence in Mi Argentina can serve in the meantime if you have it.
- **Why:** driving without the licence is an infraction (art. 77 e) even if you hold one.
- **How:** (1) file a police report for a theft or note the loss; (2) log in to the licence platform or the licence-issuing centre of your jurisdiction and request a duplicate; (3) keep the receipt as a temporary proof; (4) if the licence data changed (address), update it: a physical licence loses validity 90 days after an unreported data change (Decreto 196/2025 art. 4 as reported).
- **Verify:** the digital licence shows in Mi Argentina.
- **Don't:** lend your licence.
- **Source:** Not verified in a primary source: the duplicate procedure (service "Duplicado" listed on argentina.gob.ar/tema/automotor, page not opened; Decreto 196/2025 summaries).

## Lost or stolen vehicle and documents

### VEH-DOC-33: If the car or its papers are stolen: police first, then insurer, then registry
- **Rule:** for a theft or robbery of the car, the first step is the police report at the nearest police station (original and a copy; it is loaded into the stolen-vehicles system); then call the insurer; then start the registry procedure (a report of theft: you receive the ownership statement (*constancia de titularidad*) and the triplicate of Solicitud Tipo 04 two days later). If the car is recovered, tell the registry.
- **Why:** a stolen car used in a crime or a fine run can be tied to you until the registry knows.
- **How:** (1) file the police report; (2) call the insurer within the report deadline (insurance.md); (3) at the registry bring: the police or court report, Solicitud Tipo 04, the title and cédula (if you have them), ID, Solicitud Tipo 13I if the car has fines, and notice to the pledge creditor if any; (4) service "Comunicar el recupero del automotor" when it is found; (5) a stolen title or cédula: VEH-DOC-5, VEH-DOC-6.
- **Verify:** the registry shows the report in the status report.
- **Don't:** wait days to file.
- **Source:** argentina.gob.ar service "Denunciar el robo o hurto del automotor" (read 2026-11-15); the page does not state the effect on circulation, the cost or the recovery steps in detail.

## Old and classic vehicles

### VEH-DOC-34: A 1994 car and the classic-vehicle registry: it exists, but the age rule is not confirmed
- **Rule:** the DNRPA runs a Registry of Classic Automobiles (*Registro de Automotores Clásicos*, Digesto de Normas Técnico-Registrales, Title II, Chapter XXI) for cars and motorcycles that, by their historic, cultural or collector value, form the national vehicle heritage. To register one needs a prior opinion (*dictamen*) from an entity enabled by the DNRPA on whether the car meets the requirements of art. 1 a), including its state of conservation and originality. The minimum age in art. 1 a) was not found in the sources read: a collector-car guide says 30 years or more, which would apply to a 1994 car (about 32 years in 2026), but it is not confirmed.
- **Why:** the status gives special registry treatment, but it also adds requirements and can be revoked if parts are changed.
- **How:** (1) ask an enabled entity (clubs or federations of historic cars recognised by the DNRPA) for the opinion; (2) it must describe the car's history, its approximate year of manufacture and which parts are original; (3) documents (Disp. 161/2026, art. 3): the manufacturing or import certificate (mandatory for nationally made cars from 1977), or a court or administrative order, or a copy of the title if the car is already registered, or a sworn declaration with two witnesses before a notary or court when there is no such document; (4) photographs of both sides, front, rear, interior and the engine and chassis codes; (5) a physical verification at an enabled plant; (6) request on the Solicitud Tipo "CL"; the registry issues a distinctive; (7) restorations or part changes that remove the conditions can lead to revoking the special registration.
- **Verify:** the registry's classic-vehicle certificate and distinctive are issued.
- **Don't:** modify an original car (engine swap, new interior) if you want the classic status; do not assume the status exempts you from VTV, insurance or patente: those depend on the jurisdiction and were not confirmed.
- **Source:** DNRPA Disposición 161/2026 (DI-2026-161-APN-DNRNPACP#MJ, 2026-08-03, Boletín Oficial 2026-08-05; full text read, arts. 1 to 5; art. 1 a) of Chapter XXI itself not reproduced there). Not verified in a primary source: the minimum age (MercadoLibre guide and an import-law note, both secondary, say 30 years); the consequences for VTV (Decreto 196/2025 is reported to require a special RTO for classic cars), patente, insurance and circulation; provincial registries for old vehicles exist in some provinces (Mendoza, Santa Fe: news notes) and were not read.
