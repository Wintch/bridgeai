# Spanish-English glossary

The guide is written in English, but the people the assistant talks to use Argentine Spanish words for the car, its parts and its paperwork. Each row gives the Spanish word people say and the English term the guide uses for it. When you answer in Spanish, use the Argentine word from the left column, not a translation of the English. `gateway vehiculos search WORD` reads this table to translate a Spanish search word.
