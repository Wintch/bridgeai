# Tolls in Argentina and TelePASE

For drivers who use highways, accesses to Buenos Aires, national routes or cross a border. Toll rules and prices change often (decrees, concession changes, privatisation of national routes): every amount in pesos here is an example with a date and a place where to check it, and anything marked "Not verified in a primary source" must be confirmed with the highway company before relying on it. This is information, not legal advice. Safety: never stop, reverse or open the door in a toll lane or under a free-flow gantry; if the barrier does not lift, follow the rule in VEH-PEA-13.

TelePASE became mandatory only road by road (City of Buenos Aires highways since late 2020; national routes are being converted by a schedule that ends on 2026-12-31, see VEH-PEA-3). Do not assume that every toll road in the country is tag-only today.

## What it is and who runs it

### VEH-PEA-1: Understand that TelePASE is one tag that works on many highway companies' tolls
- **Rule:** TelePASE (*TelePASE*, the interoperable electronic toll system) is a radio-frequency device (a sticker called TAG on the windshield, or on the front-light area of a motorcycle) or a plate-reading record that lets a vehicle pass the toll lanes of all the companies in the network without paying cash.
- **Why:** one registration covers many highways, and tag-only lanes and free-flow gantries can only charge you if your plate or tag is registered.
- **How:** the network is defined by an interoperability agreement signed by the highway concession companies; the system opens the barrier by reading the tag or by plate-reading (LPR) when the account is enabled. The official site is `www.telepase.com.ar` (home page: "Sitio oficial"). A vehicle is tied to the tag you registered: you declare under oath the make, model and plate. The system works for vehicles from motorcycles to heavy vehicles of more than 7 axles.
- **Verify:** only the site `telepase.com.ar`, the concessionaire sites (for example `ausa.com.ar`) and the Mercado Pago app list TelePASE registration; the official site links to the Mercado Pago and MODO pages.
- **Don't:** buy a tag from a seller or an ad: the tag is property of the network company and is registered to one plate (VEH-PEA-6).
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Terminos y condiciones, clauses First, Second and Fourth (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-2: Know that there is no single operator: each highway company bills you
- **Rule:** TelePASE is run jointly by the highway concessionaires and not by one company; each one issues its own invoice for the passes on its road, and each one can be called for support.
- **Why:** a debt with one company can disable the tag in the whole network (VEH-PEA-22), so you must know who bills you.
- **How:** per the official site, after you register in the self-service portal you can see the invoices of Autopistas del Oeste (Acceso Oeste), Autopistas del Sol (Acceso Norte), Autopistas Urbanas (AUSA: Illia, 25 de Mayo, Perito Moreno), the concession that includes the Riccheri access (the site names Corredores Viales Tramo IX; the terms of adhesion name Rutas Sur Atlantico, so operators are changing with the privatisation) and AUBASA (Buenos Aires - La Plata highway and the Atlantic coast corridor). For the other roads (for example Corredores Viales, the Camino del Buen Ayre run by CEAMSE, Caminos de las Sierras in Cordoba, the Rosario-Santa Fe highway) the invoice is downloaded from each company's own site. Authority for national routes: Vialidad Nacional (DNV); for the City highways: AUSA under the City's Ministry of Infrastructure. AUSA: 0-800-666-2872 and road assistance *287.
- **Verify:** the invoice names the highway company and the passes by date and place.
- **Don't:** pay an invoice to an unknown company or to a person's account (VEH-PEA-27).
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Como funciona, FAQ "Como se factura" (read 2026-11-15); Terminos y condiciones clause Sixth; ausa.com.ar (phone and *287, read 2026-11-15); the change of Riccheri operator is Not verified in a primary source (terms say Rutas Sur Atlantico; FAQ page says Corredores Viales Tramo IX).

### VEH-PEA-3: Know where TelePASE is mandatory and where it is not, as of late 2026
- **Rule:** TelePASE is mandatory on all City of Buenos Aires highways (which have been tollbooth-free since 2026-05-01); on national routes the law requires barrier-free tolls with a schedule that sets 2026-12-31 for automatic lanes, and cash is already unavailable on the concessions of the Red Federal de Concesiones; do not rely on cash anywhere on a road concessioned today.
- **Why:** a driver without a tag on a free-flow highway cannot pay at a booth; the passage is recorded and becomes a fine (VEH-PEA-19).
- **How:** City of Buenos Aires: TelePASE has been mandatory on the City highways since the last quarter of 2020; since 2026-05-01 all of them are barrier-free (Illia since 2023, Paseo del Bajo since 2019, 25 de Mayo and Perito Moreno). National routes: Decreto 196/2025 (art. 19) says the party in charge of tolls must install barrier-free tolls, and (art. 20, as replaced by Decreto 733/2025, BO 2025-10-14) the DNV must set a schedule so that by 2026-12-31 all concessioned national routes have automatic lanes with barriers and/or free-flow lanes; the DNV can change the date by section. The original schedule was 100% free-flow by 2027-06-30 for all national routes; the amended article does not repeat that date. Vialidad Nacional said on 2026-10-09 that on the Red Federal de Concesiones the only payment modes are TelePASE and electronic manual payment at a kiosk (contactless card or a wallet QR), cash is not available, and that once a company has both modes active in all lanes of a section, drivers without TelePASE pay double. Accesos Norte and Oeste: since 2026-03-07, manual lanes can charge up to double the TelePASE fare (VEH-PEA-20).
- **Verify:** ask the highway company for the status of the road you will use, and read the signs at the toll plaza.
- **Don't:** assume a toll you passed last month in cash is still taking cash.
- **Source:** buenosaires.gob.ar news 2026-04-29 (read 2026-11-15) and 2023-10-12 (mandatory since last quarter of 2020); Decreto 196/2025 and Decreto 733/2025 text on argentina.gob.ar (read 2026-11-15); argentina.gob.ar Vialidad Nacional news 2026-10-09 (read 2026-11-15); Resolucion DNV 297/2026 art. 3 (BO 2026-03-05, read 2026-11-15). The earlier belief that TelePASE became mandatory "on many roads in 2022-2024" is only true for some City highways; it is not general.

## Getting the tag and registering

### VEH-PEA-4: Register on the official site, with your ID and your plate
- **Rule:** register on `www.telepase.com.ar` ("Adherite", then the self-service portal), choose how to pay and how to get the tag, and activate it; registration is free.
- **Why:** the official site is the only place that links the plate to your payment method; copycat sites collect card data.
- **How:** on the site create or log in to your TelePASE user; fill in your data, the plate and the vehicle category, choose the payment method (credit card, debit, MODO, Mercado Pago) and then choose between picking the tag at a placement point (more than 300 points, per the site) or home delivery. After you pick it up or receive it, complete the activation and stick it. Alternatively use the Mercado Pago app: "Ver mas", "TelePASE", enter the plate; the tag is delivered in 24 hours or picked up free at enabled points (the site also says it can be bought in the official Mercado Libre store). AUSA offers a link to adhere free. You can also go to a placement point of the City highways with a booth marked "retire aqui" after adhering online.
- **Verify:** after activation, the account says the plate is enabled; AUSA has an "Estado TelePASE" check on its TelePASE page.
- **Don't:** give your card data on a site you reached from an ad or a message.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Como funciona (FAQ "Como adherirme y usar TelePASE por primera vez", read 2026-11-15); ausa.com.ar/telepase (adhesion free, "Estado TelePASE", read 2026-11-15); buenosaires.gob.ar news 2023-06-21 (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-5: Wait for the activation time and know the placement cost
- **Rule:** the network enables the tag 24 to 96 business hours after installation; tag placement may be free or have a charge depending on the point.
- **Why:** a tag that is not yet enabled is the same as no tag on a tag-only highway.
- **How:** the terms of adhesion say that the enabling takes at least 24 and at most 96 business hours from installation; the site says 72 to 96 business hours for pickup and for home delivery. Example as of 2026-11-15: a placement point with a charge asks ARS 8,500 per vehicle (read on the official page), and there are points with no charge; check at `telepase.com.ar`. If your tag was enabled after paying an overdue debt, the re-enabling takes 96 business hours from the credit of the payment.
- **Verify:** check the account status (VEH-PEA-4) before entering a highway.
- **Don't:** travel on a tag-only highway on the same day you stuck the tag.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Terminos y condiciones clause 3.4 and Como funciona (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-6: Declare the right vehicle and category and keep your data current
- **Rule:** the plate, make, model and category you declare are sworn data; a tag registered with a lower category than the real one is billed an extra category adjustment.
- **Why:** a wrong declaration produces an adjusted invoice and, if false, responsibility for the consequences.
- **How:** use the real category of the vehicle (a vehicle towing a trailer changes category: the site says category 3 is the one that allows towing trailers; with an occasional trailer use the lane marked manual, where available). AUSA's rule: a vehicle that passes with a device registered in a lower category pays an adjustment equal to the toll of its category. Keep the phone and e-mail current on your account.
- **Verify:** compare the category on your account with the invoice.
- **Don't:** put the tag of a car on a different vehicle: it is bound to the plate.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Terminos y condiciones clauses 4.1 and 5 a (read 2026-11-15); ausa.com.ar Reglamento de Explotacion art. 43 (read 2026-11-15); Como funciona (trailers).

### VEH-PEA-7: Choose a payment method that matches the highways you use
- **Rule:** TelePASE bills by credit card, debit card, MODO or Mercado Pago balance; the method must be valid and with funds.
- **Why:** an unpaid invoice disables the tag in the whole network (VEH-PEA-22).
- **How:** the official site lists: prepaid Mercado Pago (from the app); credit card Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Cabal; debit card Visa; MODO (app or bank app). Mercado Pago payment is for final consumers and is not valid, for now, on the Rosario-Santa Fe highway and on Caminos del Rio Uruguay (national routes 12, 14 and 174); with Mercado Pago the passes are debited when you pass, so you need balance. Billing is fortnightly or monthly with the detail of passes. Per the terms, the highway companies waive the administrative fee when you make four passes or fewer in the period on that company's road.
- **Verify:** log in to the self-service portal to see the invoices (VEH-PEA-14).
- **Don't:** leave a card that expired or was reissued as the method.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Como funciona (FAQ "Como se abona el TelePASE"; read 2026-11-15); Terminos y condiciones clauses 3.3 and 6.3 a.

### VEH-PEA-8: Use "TelePASE by plate" only as a temporary or occasional option
- **Rule:** TelePASE by plate (*TelePASE por patente*, a virtual tag, no sticker) is valid only on the City highways run by AUSA and costs 50% more than the tag.
- **Why:** it is meant for visitors who do not have a tag and for a quick start, not for daily use.
- **How:** register on `telepase.com.ar` and, when the site asks how you get the device, choose to activate the by-plate option; you can adhere with automatic debit on a credit or debit card or with post-paid billing against invoice (not with virtual wallets). The by-plate registration is free. Example: if the TelePASE fare is 1,000 pesos, by plate it is 1,500 (calc).
- **Verify:** AUSA's "Estado TelePASE" page tells whether the plate is enabled.
- **Don't:** use it on Acceso Norte, Acceso Oeste or on national routes: it does not work there.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Como funciona (FAQ "TelePASE por patente exclusivo solo para Autopistas Urbanas SA", read 2026-11-15); ausa.com.ar/telepase (read 2026-11-15); Decreto CABA 83/2025 (tag virtual in the Reglamento).

### VEH-PEA-9: Buy a daily pass (*Pase Diario*) in advance if you only come once
- **Rule:** in the City highways a non-adhered user can buy a daily pass; it must be bought before using the highway and is valid only that day.
- **Why:** it avoids a toll-evasion fine for a one-off trip.
- **How:** the Reglamento says the pass must be obtained at least 3 hours before the day of circulation, valid only on that day, with a maximum of 20 passes per calendar year; it is bought through the means AUSA sets (AUSA's page links to "Pase Diario"). Its value is set by the City decree alongside the tolls (Decreto 83/2025, Annex II).
- **Verify:** ask AUSA (0-800-666-2872) or read ausa.com.ar/telepase for the current value and the means of purchase.
- **Don't:** buy it on the spot at the gantry: it will not be valid.
- **Source:** ausa.com.ar Reglamento de Explotacion art. 43 (read 2026-11-15); Decreto CABA 83/2025 (Publication 2025-02-20, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-10: Foreign drivers and motorcycles can register too
- **Rule:** foreign persons and companies can adhere to TelePASE online with a document from their country, an international credit card and the foreign plate; motorcycles can use dynamic toll lanes by fitting the device.
- **Why:** it avoids cash on roads where cash is gone.
- **How:** on `telepase.com.ar` complete the driver registration (country, type and number of the foreign ID), add an international credit card, and register the vehicle with its foreign plate. Motorcycles must install the device before they use TelePASE; the by-plate option is not available for motorcycles (a news article).
- **Verify:** the account lists the foreign plate as enabled.
- **Don't:** drive through a free-flow gantry with a foreign plate and no registration.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Como funciona (FAQ foreign users and motorcycles, read 2026-11-15); the by-plate limit for motorcycles: Not verified in a primary source (iProfesional 2025-10-04).

## Using the tag

### VEH-PEA-11: Stick the tag correctly and use the lane the right way
- **Rule:** fix the tag on the inside of the windshield (cars and heavy vehicles) or on the upper area of the windshield or front-light of a motorcycle, enter only on the green (or yellow) signal, keep to the maximum speed and keep distance.
- **Why:** an incorrectly placed tag does not read and a driver who passes on red can be fined by the jurisdiction.
- **How:** car and heavy vehicle: the tag stuck inside the windshield; speed at most 20 km/h; keep at least 20 m from the vehicle ahead; move only on a green light. Motorcycle: stick it on the upper part of the windshield, wind deflector or front optic; speed at most 10 km/h; only in lanes identified for motorcycles. The tag works only when stuck; it does not work held in the hand. If you tow a trailer eventually, use a lane marked manual.
- **Verify:** the barrier opens and the passage shows on the next invoice.
- **Don't:** peel the tag to move it: it stops working once removed and must not be reused (VEH-PEA-12).
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Como funciona (FAQ "Cuales son las condiciones de uso", read 2026-11-15); Terminos y condiciones clause 2.2 (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-12: Replace the tag when you change the windshield; do not move it
- **Rule:** the tag stops working once it is peeled and cannot be reused; after a windshield replacement request a new device.
- **Why:** a reused tag is not read and you can end up with unpaid passes.
- **How:** request the replacement in the self-service portal and go to any attention centre to have a new tag fitted. The cost of replacing a tag lost, stolen or damaged by the user is equal to the value of the adhesion at that time (Terms, clause 4.3 b). A warranty of one year covers malfunction not caused by the user (clause 4.4). Special windshields (armoured, thermal, tinted or printed) need the tag in specific areas: ask the attention centre.
- **Verify:** the new tag is registered to the same plate.
- **Don't:** stick the old tag on the new glass.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Como funciona and Terminos y condiciones clauses 4.3 and 4.4 (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-13: If the barrier does not lift or the tag is not read, stop and follow the booth, do not reverse
- **Rule:** if the barrier does not open, stop completely behind the signal, do not drive through and do not reverse; use the help of the booth or the intercom, and report the case to the company the same day.
- **Why:** driving through a barrier damages the vehicle and can create a toll-evasion record; the user is responsible for the driving (terms, clause 2.5).
- **How:** the usual causes (according to the terms): windshield composition or devices inside it (defrosters, rain or light sensors, anti-glare film), phones, a tag that was removed and re-stuck, an account disabled for debt, or an unregistered plate. If the lane has an attendant or a manual lane, use it (a manual lane may charge double: VEH-PEA-20). On a free-flow highway there is no lane to stop: check later whether the passage was charged by looking in the self-service portal and at AUSA's "Estado TelePASE" page, and contact the company to explain. Keep the date, time, place and photos of the tag. If the tag is faulty, the company must replace it within the one-year warranty if the fault is not yours.
- **Verify:** the company confirms your tag status or exchange in writing.
- **Don't:** reverse in a toll lane or push the barrier.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Terminos y condiciones clauses 2.3, 2.5, 2.6 and 4.4 (read 2026-11-15); ausa.com.ar/telepase (Estado TelePASE, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-14: Read each invoice and claim within 10 business days
- **Rule:** the invoice is considered accepted if you do not claim in writing within 10 business days of it being made available.
- **Why:** a wrong passage (a trailer, a double charge, a car you sold) is billed otherwise.
- **How:** register in the self-service portal to see invoices and the detail of passes; for the companies that are not shown there (VEH-PEA-2) download from their site. Claim in writing to the company that issued the invoice with the plate, date and hour of the passage. Billing is monthly or fortnightly; the invoice uses the toll in force at the time of the passage and the vehicle type detected by the sensors (the detected category can adjust the billing).
- **Verify:** the company answers with a number of claim.
- **Don't:** wait for the second invoice to complain.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Terminos y condiciones clauses 5 a and 6 (read 2026-11-15).

## Highways of the Buenos Aires area

### VEH-PEA-15: Know the City of Buenos Aires highways and how they charge
- **Rule:** the City highways of AUSA (Illia, 25 de Mayo, Perito Moreno, Paseo del Bajo, and the Alberti and Parque Avellaneda toll points) charge by gantries that read the tag or the plate, with no cabins since 2026-05-01; the toll is paid once even if the highway has two gantries on your route.
- **Why:** you cannot pay on the spot; you pay through your TelePASE account.
- **How:** per the City, the gantries have laser plate-reading cameras and sensors that detect the tag while the vehicle drives without stopping; Illia was the first (2023), Paseo del Bajo has worked as free-flow since 2019 (heavy transport; in 2024 opened to light vehicles on weekends and per a later AUSA news item on weeknights with TelePASE; motorcycles are forbidden), 25 de Mayo has Alberti (since 2023) and a gantry at Avenida Varela (one toll for the two gantries) and Perito Moreno has Parque Avellaneda. Lugones and Cantilo are listed as free by a news article (not verified in a primary source).
- **Verify:** AUSA's page "Autopistas" and the City's news of 2026-04-29.
- **Don't:** enter without a registered tag or plate (VEH-PEA-19).
- **Source:** buenosaires.gob.ar news 2026-04-29 (read 2026-11-15); AUSA news "Paseo del Bajo" (ausa.com.ar novedades, read 2026-11-15); the free highways Lugones and Cantilo and the Paseo del Bajo schedule: Not verified in a primary source (autocosmos.com.ar 2026-02-26).

### VEH-PEA-16: Check Acceso Norte, Acceso Oeste, Riccheri, Buenos Aires - La Plata and the Camino del Buen Ayre before you go
- **Rule:** these roads belong to the TelePASE network, but their status (barriers, manual lanes, free-flow) and their rules are set by each company or by Vialidad Nacional, so check the current rule for each before the trip.
- **Why:** a manual lane can cost double and some lanes are tag-only.
- **How:** Acceso Norte (Autopistas del Sol) and Acceso Oeste (Grupo Concesionario del Oeste): the national authority (DNV) approved a tariff table valid from 2026-03-07 (Resolucion 297/2026) with manual lanes charging up to double the TelePASE fare and instructed the companies to apply the incentive or penalty; the kiosks accept cards and QR (reported). Riccheri and Ezeiza-Cañuelas: Rutas Sur Atlantico (per the terms; the official site's FAQ names Corredores Viales Tramo IX): ask which. Buenos Aires - La Plata and the Atlantic coast corridor: AUBASA. Camino Parque del Buen Ayre: CEAMSE. Use the company's site for the lanes in service.
- **Verify:** the company site, the signs, and the TelePASE site list of highways ("Autopistas").
- **Don't:** take cash for granted on a national-route access.
- **Source:** Resolucion DNV 297/2026 arts. 1-3 (BO 2026-03-05, read 2026-11-15); telepase.com.ar Terminos y condiciones clause First (read 2026-11-15); whether any of these roads is barrier-free today: Not verified in a primary source.

### VEH-PEA-17: Expect peak and off-peak fares and know the City's peak hours
- **Rule:** the City highways charge a higher fare in peak hours: Monday to Friday from 7 to 11 and from 16 to 20, in both directions, and a tourist peak on weekends and holidays (11 to 15 toward the province, 17 to 21 toward the centre).
- **Why:** it changes the cost of a daily commute and of a weekend trip.
- **How:** the City's Decreto 83/2025 keeps the structure of differentiated time bands set by Decreto 155/2024. Acceso Norte and Oeste use peak and off-peak ("pico" and "no pico") tables set by the DNV; read the hours of each company because they differ: Not verified.
- **Verify:** the fare table of the company.
- **Don't:** assume the peak hours of one highway apply to another.
- **Source:** Decreto CABA 83/2025 considerandos (Publication 2025-02-20, read on boletinoficial.buenosaires.gob.ar 2026-11-15; hours of the table in force since 2024).

### VEH-PEA-18: Use TelePASE on national routes, and expect the list of concessions to change
- **Rule:** national-route tolls are moving to TelePASE and electronic kiosks as the new concessions (Red Federal de Concesiones) take over from the old state company Corredores Viales; check the concession of the road you will drive.
- **Why:** you may find a toll on a road that did not charge, or no cash lane on one that did.
- **How:** the official TelePASE site said on its home page that from 2026-10-07 the service can also be used in the following concessions (when the user does not object within 10 days he or she accepts the extension): Arco Portuario (RN 9 Lagos station, RN 33, RN A-008); Covicen (RN 9 Carcarana and James Craik, RN 19 Franck, RN 34 San Vicente); Autovia al Norte (RN 34 Ceres and Fernandez); Autovia del NOA (RN 9 La Florida and Molle Yaco, RN 34 Cabeza de Buey, RN 66, RN A-016); Corredor Litoral (RN 12 Riachuelo, RN 16 Puente General Belgrano and Makalle); Camino Selva (RN 12 Ituzaingo, Santa Ana and Colonia Victoria, RN 105); Caminos del Aconcagua (RN 7 La Paz); Mesopotamico (RN 12 and RN 18); Vias del Parana Medio (RN 11). If you do not accept the extension you can ask for the cancellation of the whole network in the self-service portal.
- **Verify:** the notice on `telepase.com.ar` and Vialidad Nacional's pages.
- **Don't:** ignore the notice: silence for 10 days means you accept.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar home page notice (read 2026-11-15, communications of the Secretaria de Transporte NO-2026-85659878 and others); Vialidad Nacional notice 2026-10-09 (read 2026-11-15).

## Passing without TelePASE

### VEH-PEA-19: Know that in the City a tagless passage is a traffic infraction, not a debt
- **Rule:** in the City highways a vehicle that passes without an enabled TelePASE account and without a valid daily pass is recorded as a toll-evasion infraction; the toll itself is not charged.
- **Why:** you get a fine (*multa*) instead of an invoice; the fine is larger than the toll.
- **How:** the Reglamento says that in those cases the record goes to the authority, which writes the record under art. 3 of Ley 1217 and sends it for the possible fine of the City's Faults Regime (Ley 451, book II, "peaje" fault); the passage "does not give the concessionaire the right to charge the toll". A news article (2025) gives the fine as 150 fixed units (UF) per passage under Ley 6041; at a City UF of ARS 1,173.08 (reported value from 2026-09-02, secondary) that is 150 x 1,173.08 = about 175,962 pesos, against a toll of a few thousand pesos. To avoid it, adhere before travelling (VEH-PEA-4) or buy a daily pass in advance (VEH-PEA-9). The City said in 2023 that people not adhered who used the smart highways should pay on the AUSA web within 30 days of the passage or receive a fine; the current Reglamento does not mention that payment window, so ask AUSA whether it still exists.
- **Verify:** AUSA (0-800-666-2872) and the City's infractions lookup (see fines-and-points.md).
- **Don't:** ignore the notice: a toll-evasion fine is an ordinary traffic fine with the discount and prescription rules of fines-and-points.md.
- **Source:** ausa.com.ar Reglamento de Explotacion art. 43 and Gastos Administrativos (read 2026-11-15; PDF incl. Decreto 83/2025); buenosaires.gob.ar news 2023-06-21 (30-day window; read 2026-11-15); fine of 150 UF and the City UF value: Not verified in a primary source (Ley 6041 reported by a search; iProfesional 2025-10-04; comparaencasa.com 2026-10-21).

### VEH-PEA-20: Know that outside the City, passing without TelePASE costs a higher fare
- **Rule:** on Acceso Norte, Acceso Oeste and the Red Federal de Concesiones, a driver without TelePASE who pays manually or electronically pays a higher fare, up to double, not a fine.
- **Why:** it is a price incentive to move to the tag and to free-flow.
- **How:** Acceso Norte and Oeste: from 2026-03-07 the DNV table allows charging up to double the TelePASE fare on manual lanes (Resolution 297/2026); reported example (news, March 2026): a 2-axle car off-peak TelePASE ARS 994.15 and manual ARS 2,000 (peak: TelePASE 1,192.99 and manual 2,400). Red Federal de Concesiones: once the company has both modes (TelePASE and kiosk) active in all lanes of a section, whoever has no TelePASE pays double. Cash is not accepted on the concessions that already changed (Vialidad Nacional, 2026-10-09); you pay by contactless card or a QR wallet at the kiosk. Calculation example: a 1,000-peso fare becomes 2,000 pesos at the kiosk.
- **Verify:** the price board and the company site; ask Vialidad Nacional's user service.
- **Don't:** plan a trip with cash for the tolls.
- **Source:** Resolucion DNV 297/2026 art. 3 and argentina.gob.ar news 2026-03-05 (read 2026-11-15); Vialidad Nacional notice 2026-10-09 (read 2026-11-15); the example prices: Not verified in a primary source (canal26.com 2026-03-05 and 2026-03-16, c5n.com; the annex IF-2026-22287449 was not read).

### VEH-PEA-21: Know that the national toll-evasion fine is not fully regulated yet
- **Rule:** the national authority said in January 2026 that the national road-safety agency still has to regulate traffic fines for toll evasion; so, outside the City, the practical consequence of no tag is the higher fare, and a fine depends on local rules.
- **Why:** you may see articles that promise "multas millonarias" on every highway.
- **How:** read the rule of the jurisdiction of the road; the City's Ley 451 treats evasion as a fault (VEH-PEA-19); the national text of Decreto 196/2025 only requires barrier-free tolls and sets the schedule. If you receive a notice, check that it comes from the authority of the place and the road (see fines-and-points.md for how to check any fine).
- **Verify:** ask the concessionaire to show the rule that creates the fine.
- **Don't:** pay a "toll fine" from a message (VEH-PEA-27).
- **Source:** Resolucion DNV 117/2026 considerandos (BO 2026-01-29, read 2026-11-15: "resulta necesario que la Agencia Nacional de Seguridad Vial logre reglamentar las multas de transito por evasion de peaje"); whether it has been regulated since: Not verified.

## Debts, selling the car, lost or stolen tag

### VEH-PEA-22: Pay invoices on time: after 15 days of delay the tag is disabled everywhere
- **Rule:** an unpaid invoice has a 5% surcharge plus punitive interest from the due date, and 15 days after the due date the tag is disabled across the whole TelePASE network, whichever company the debt is with.
- **Why:** a debt of a few hundred pesos on one road can stop you on every road.
- **How:** per the terms: delay happens automatically at the due date; the surcharge is 5% plus interest at the National Bank's active rate for 30-day discount operations, plus collection costs; the company can start an executive proceeding (the debt certificate is an executive title); the company can disable all the tags under the same ID or CUIT; reactivation can be asked only after the whole debt is paid, and takes 96 business hours from the credit. Someone with a debt with any company of the network cannot adhere. Pay at the company that holds the debt (VEH-PEA-2); ask for the settlement amount and the debit detail.
- **Verify:** the self-service portal and the company's debt page; ask in writing for a debt-free note when you finish.
- **Don't:** ignore an invoice you disagree with: claim in writing within 10 business days (VEH-PEA-14) and pay the undisputed part.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Terminos y condiciones clauses 3.4, 5 d, 7 (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-23: Cancel the tag when you sell the car, and ask for the registry's sale report
- **Rule:** when you sell or change the holder of the vehicle, ask for the cancellation (*baja*) of the tag so it is disabled and unlinked from the plate; you are responsible for the passes until the company receives that formal notice.
- **Why:** a tag left on a sold car keeps billing you, and the new owner can end up with unread passes.
- **How:** request the cancellation in the self-service portal or at the attention centre of any highway company of the network; once one company registers it, the others receive it in 72 to 96 business hours. If the new owner wants a tag, he or she signs a new adhesion. Keep the cancellation receipt together with the sale documents (see fines-and-points.md for fines on a sold car and ownership-and-paperwork.md for the transfer).
- **Verify:** the portal shows the tag as cancelled and no passes after the sale date.
- **Don't:** hand over the tag with the car and say nothing.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Como funciona (FAQ "Que hago si vendo el vehiculo") and Terminos y condiciones clause 4.2 (read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-24: Report a lost or stolen tag at once
- **Rule:** if the tag is stolen, lost or the vehicle is stolen, report it formally and immediately to any company of the network so it is disabled.
- **Why:** you pay for all the passes made until the company gets the notice.
- **How:** call or write to the company that bills you (VEH-PEA-2) and keep the date and the claim number; a new tag costs the value of the adhesion at that time (terms clause 4.3 b). For a stolen vehicle also attach the police report when you dispute passes after the theft (see fines-and-points.md for fines).
- **Verify:** written confirmation of the cancellation.
- **Don't:** sell or lend the tag: you are the "principal payer" of every pass made with it, whoever drives (clauses 5 b and 5 c).
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Terminos y condiciones clauses 4.3, 5 b and 5 c (read 2026-11-15).

## Prices, discounts and where to look

### VEH-PEA-25: Check the current toll table on the company site, and read these figures only as dated examples
- **Rule:** toll tables change by decree or resolution several times a year; always read the table on the company's site on the day you travel.
- **Why:** news articles repeat old figures and often mix up cabins, peak hours and categories.
- **How:** example as read on the AUSA tariffs page on 2026-11-15 (undated table; the page says "tarifas actualizadas"; the values are with TelePASE; the page lists seven categories and the category of your vehicle is in your TelePASE account): AU 25 de Mayo and Perito Moreno (stretch coded AVE), category 1: 2,032.63 off-peak and 3,252.62 peak; category 2: 4,878.81 off-peak and 6,914.08 peak. Illia / Retiro II / Sarmiento / Salguero (coded KDT), category 1: 1,219.79 and 1,463.42; category 2: 2,032.63 and 2,874.43. Peaje Alberti (down direction), category 1: 1,219.79 and 1,626.33. Acceso Norte and Oeste (secondary, March 2026): a 2-axle car pays about ARS 994 with TelePASE off-peak and 1,193 at peak. Another news article of July 2026 gave a car fare of ARS 3,982 off-peak for the City highways, so sources disagree: use the AUSA page. The City table includes a 10% rail contribution (Contribucion Especial Ferroviaria) and the Decreto 83/2025 set monthly updates of 2% plus inflation for twelve months (until early 2026).
- **Verify:** AUSA tariffs page `ausa.com.ar/telepase/tarifas`; Acceso Norte and Oeste: the Autopistas del Sol and GCO sites and Vialidad Nacional's news; AUBASA for La Plata and the coast.
- **Don't:** budget a trip with a fare from a news headline.
- **Source:** ausa.com.ar/telepase/tarifas (data in the page, read 2026-11-15); Decreto CABA 83/2025 (Publication 2025-02-20, read 2026-11-15); Acceso Norte/Oeste figures: Not verified in a primary source (canal26.com 2026-03-05; c5n.com); the City's 2026-07 figure: iProfesional 2026-07-16.

### VEH-PEA-26: Use the small discounts and the fee waiver the terms give
- **Rule:** there is no charge for the TelePASE adhesion, the highway companies waive the administrative fee with four or fewer passes in a billing period, and the City bonifies the toll of electric cars.
- **Why:** a few thousand pesos a month can add up for a family car that rarely uses the highway.
- **How:** the terms of adhesion waive the administrative expenses when the billed period shows four or fewer passes on that company's road (clause 6.3 a). AUSA's FAQ lists a "Bonificacion para autos electricos" and says so in its help centre; read the conditions there. Older news (2020) mentioned discounts of 10% on AUSA highways and up to 30% in the network for registered users: it may be outdated, do not rely on it. A procedure exists on argentina.gob.ar to obtain a free pass at the access tolls of the City for vehicles carrying people with disability: the page was not readable when checked; ask the service or the company.
- **Verify:** the invoice shows the bonus or the waiver.
- **Don't:** assume a discount you saw in a news article applies to you.
- **Source:** telepase.com.ar Terminos y condiciones clause 6.3 a (read 2026-11-15); ausa.com.ar FAQ (headings, read 2026-11-15); the 2020 discounts: buenosaires.gob.ar news 2020-08-06 (read 2026-11-15; likely outdated); the disability free pass: Not verified in a primary source.

### VEH-PEA-27: Treat a toll bill or a toll fine that arrives by link as a scam until the company confirms it
- **Rule:** do not pay a toll invoice, a TelePASE debt or a "toll fine" from a link, QR code or number in an SMS, WhatsApp or e-mail; open the official site yourself or call the company's published line.
- **Why:** fake toll and fine messages are used to take card data; Brazil's transport agency states that neither it nor the concessionaires send toll bills by WhatsApp, e-mail or SMS, and the same prudence applies here.
- **How:** type `telepase.com.ar` or the company's address yourself, log in and look at the invoices and the debt; for a toll-evasion fine look it up in the issuer's official lookup with the plate (see fines-and-points.md). Warning signs: urgency, a short link, a payment to a personal account, a request for card codes or for a tag number by message. If you typed card data, call your bank at once and block the card.
- **Verify:** the domain ends in `.gob.ar` or is the company's known site; the passages in the message appear in your portal.
- **Don't:** answer the message or open the attachment.
- **Source:** gov.br/antt notice on scams (read 2026-11-15, Brazil); Not verified in a primary source: that Argentine toll-bill scams exist at scale (no official notice found); the safe-payment rule follows telepase.com.ar official channels (read 2026-11-15).

## Tolls abroad

### VEH-PEA-28: Driving into Uruguay: get the foreigner pass (*Abono Extranjero*) at the first toll
- **Rule:** a vehicle with a foreign plate in Uruguay uses the foreigner pass (*Abono Extranjero*) bought at the toll when entering; it covers all tolls for 15 consecutive days with a tag fitted in the car.
- **Why:** it avoids stopping at every toll and paying in cash.
- **How:** the procedure is done at the toll plazas when entering the country, where the tag is installed; cost example: UYU 1,141, equal to six basic toll fares (MTOP FAQ of 2025-12-18 and Presidencia news of 2026-02-09). After 15 days you can renew at a toll, or keep using the same Telepeaje account by topping up on `telepeaje.com.uy`, at a toll or at collection networks (Abitab, Red Pagos). If you do not renew and have no balance on day 16, the barrier does not lift and you top up on the spot. The earlier Tourist Pass (*Pase Turista*, 24 hours or up to 6 passes with deferred payment) is the previous system. Check also Uruguayan rules on speed cameras before you drive (see trip-preparation.md).
- **Verify:** MTOP's FAQ page on gub.uy and `telepeaje.com.uy`.
- **Don't:** assume the price above is current: it is the price as of 2026-02-09.
- **Source:** MTOP Uruguay "Preguntas frecuentes sobre Abono Extranjero de peajes" (2025-12-18, read 2026-11-15); Presidencia Uruguay news 2026-02-09 (read 2026-11-15); MTOP news 2022-12-07 (Pase Turista, read 2026-11-15).

### VEH-PEA-29: Driving into Chile: urban highways need a TAG or a late payment
- **Rule:** the urban highways of Santiago and several interurban routes use free-flow or tag lanes; a car without a TAG must regularise the passages afterwards, paying more, or it can be fined under Chile's traffic law.
- **Why:** since the single daily pass ended there is no one-day option for visitors.
- **How:** per Chilean press (April 2026), the single interoperable daily pass (*Pase Diario Unico*) stopped on 2025-07-01; it was replaced by late payment of transactions (*Pago Tardio de Transacciones*): each highway is paid separately, from day 11 to day 30 after the passage, by the number of gantries crossed in a day multiplied by a factor (about double for cars). The official portal is `pasastesintag.cl` ("Pasaste sin TAG": enter the plate to see the passages; they appear from day 11 to day 30 counted from the passage). The official portal says it is mandatory to drive on the highways with a tag system with an enabled means of payment. Rental cars usually come with a TAG: ask the rental company about fees.
- **Verify:** `pasastesintag.cl` and the highway company's site.
- **Don't:** leave Chile with passages unpaid: an unpaid debt is reported to the Ministry of Public Works and it generates daily late charges.
- **Source:** pasastesintag.cl home page (read 2026-11-15); autopistacentral.cl home page (read 2026-11-15); Not verified in a primary source: the end of the daily pass, the factor and the late charges (Meganoticias 2026-04-21; T13 2025-07-01); foreign-plate cars were not addressed in any source read.

### VEH-PEA-30: Driving into Brazil: free-flow tolls are paid afterwards by plate or with a tag
- **Rule:** in Brazilian free-flow highways every passage is recorded by plate reading, and whoever has no tag must pay through the concessionaire's channels within the time the concessionaire sets; the toll is still owed.
- **Why:** an unpaid passage can become a toll-evasion infraction under the Brazilian traffic code.
- **How:** the national transport agency (ANTT) says that without a tag the plate is read and the payment must be made in the concessionaire's website, app, kiosks or accredited network within its deadline; the agency's model sets up to 30 days but some concessionaires say 15 days: read the signs of the road. Tags such as Sem Parar, ConectCar, Veloe, Taggy and others are sold by operators. The agency also warns that neither it nor the concessionaires send toll bills by WhatsApp, e-mail or SMS. A temporary suspension of free-flow fines and points was reported until 2026-11-16 (Contran Deliberation 277, via a state agency); after that date the normal deadlines and penalties apply; the toll remains owed. How a foreign plate is handled: Not verified in any source read; ask the concessionaire or the rental company. The Argentine tag does not work in Brazil.
- **Verify:** the concessionaire's site with the plate; ANTT's free-flow pages on `gov.br/antt`.
- **Don't:** pay a "toll bill" you received by message.
- **Source:** gov.br/antt "Nao tenho tag. E agora?" (read 2026-11-15); ANTT notice on scams (listed in search, read 2026-11-15); the 200-day suspension: Not verified in a primary source (Agencia Minas, mercadoeconsumo.com.br 2026-04-28).
