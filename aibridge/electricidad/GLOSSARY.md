# Spanish-English glossary

The guide is written in English, but the electricians and owners the assistant talks to use Argentine Spanish trade and legal words. Each row gives the Spanish word people say and the English term the guide uses for it. When you answer in Spanish, use the Argentine word from the left column, not a translation of the English.
